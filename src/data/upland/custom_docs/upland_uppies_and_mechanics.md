# Upland Uppies, Residents, Life & Core Gameplay Guide

**Source:** https://www.upland.me/learn/

## Uppies, Property Sales & Swaps (Resident Ownership)
- **What happens to a resident Uppie when a property is sold or swapped?** **Yes — the Uppie stays with its original owner.** Uppies are tied to the player's account rather than transferring with the property deed, so selling or swapping a property does not transfer the resident Uppie to the buyer.
- **Duty Lock While Assigned:** While an Uppie is actively assigned to a property duty (starting at Level 3 for plant care or future Service Structure automation), that Uppie cannot be merged, sold, or swapped until unassigned.
- **Where to Check Your Uppies:** View all your owned Uppies under the **Uppies** tab in your in-game **Asset Wallet**, and check the top-right corner of a property card for the Uppie resident indicator.
- **Leveling & Merging Uppies:** Uppies can participate in Uppie Quests (all levels) and can be merged up to Level 10; merging two Level 10 Uppies creates a rare **Iconic Parody Uppie (IPU)**.
- **Resident Score & Influence Score:** **Resident Score** tracks how many Uppies live on your properties (updates every 24 hours). **Influence Score** measures how attractive your neighborhoods are to potential residents.

## Life, STEM, Farming, Totems & Troves
- **STEM:** Account-bound, non-tradable Life currency used for plants, farming, animals, and Life gameplay.
- **Troves & Recipes:** Fungible in-game items produced by structures, plants, fishing, animals, and trade routes, combined in **Recipes** at Service Structures to craft higher-order goods requested by Uppies.
- **Totems:** Collectible digital items that generate **STEM** and interact with Life mechanics.

---

## Property Swaps, Metaventures & Tilia (USD)
- **Property Swaps:** Players can swap properties directly with other players; swaps must reflect fair market value and must not be used for asset funneling or collusion.
- **Metaventures:** Player-run shops selling digital assets (Block Explorers, Legits, Karts, Map Assets, Structure Ornaments).
- **Selling for USD (Tilia Pay):** Requires reaching **Uplander** status (10,000+ UPX net worth) and completing **KYC** identity verification through Tilia Pay.
- **Additional Community Reference:** For detailed guides, city calculators, and gameplay walkthroughs, see [Upland Guide](https://upland-guide.com).
