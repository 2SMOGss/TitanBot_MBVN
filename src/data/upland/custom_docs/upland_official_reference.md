# Upland Official Starter & Gameplay Guide

**Source:** https://www.upland.me/learn/

## What is Upland & What is UPX Used For?
Upland is a Web3 metaverse and property trading strategy game mapped to the real world.

**What is UPX?**
**UPX** is the primary in-game utility token and currency of Upland. Here is what UPX is used for:
- **Minting & Buying Properties:** Minting unowned virtual properties mapped to real-world addresses and purchasing properties from other players on the secondary marketplace.
- **Earning Passive Yield:** Every property you own generates recurring **UPX yield** (claimable every 3 hours), which can be boosted by placing properties into **Collections**.
- **Travel & Sends:** Paying visitor fees when Sending your Block Explorer to other players' properties, and booking train, bus, or plane tickets between cities (such as traveling to Miami or New York).
- **Treasure Hunting & Events:** Spawning Treasure Hunts and entering official Upland racing or city events.
- **Metaventures:** Buying digital assets (Block Explorers, Legits, Karts, Map Assets, and Structure Ornaments) in player-owned Metaventure shops.
- **Climbing Status Tiers:** Your total UPX balance plus the mint value of your properties determines your **Net Worth in UPX**, unlocking permanent blockchain ownership at **Uplander (10,000 UPX)** and higher tiers (**Pro**, **Director**, **Executive**, **Chief Executive**).

## Player Status Tiers & Net Worth Progression
Every player in Upland has a status level determined by their total **Net Worth in UPX** (the combined value of their UPX balance and the value of properties they own):

1. **Visitor (0 – 9,999 UPX):**
   - Starting tier for new accounts.
   - True ownership on the EOS blockchain is **not yet permanent** while you are a Visitor.
   - **Important Visa Rule:** Visitors must renew their **Upland Visa** at least once every **7 days** by logging in and clicking the Visa renewal button on their profile, or their Visitor account and starter assets will be recycled back to the game.
   - Visitors cannot buy properties from other players on the secondary marketplace until reaching Uplander status.

2. **Uplander (10,000 – 99,999 UPX):**
   - Permanent blockchain account is created automatically upon reaching **10,000 UPX** net worth.
   - **No more Visa renewals required** — your properties are permanently yours on the blockchain.
   - Unlocks the secondary marketplace (buying and selling properties with other players for UPX or USD via Tilia Pay, after completing KYC for USD transactions).

3. **Pro (100,000 – 999,999 UPX):**
   - Unlocks enhanced Block Explorer customization and Pro perks.

4. **Director (1,000,000 – 9,999,999 UPX):**
   - Unlocks custom Block Explorer requests and higher-tier community recognition.

5. **Executive (10,000,000 – 99,999,999 UPX):**
   - Elite tier for major metaverse landowners.

6. **Chief Executive (100,000,000+ UPX):**
   - Highest status tier in Upland.

## Fair Start Act (FSA) Properties
To help new players get started affordably, Upland enforces the **Fair Start Act (FSA)**:
- **Who can mint FSA properties?** Only **Visitors** and **Uplanders** (players with a net worth **under 100,000 UPX**) can mint unowned properties marked with the **FSA** tag. Once a player reaches **Pro status (100,000+ UPX)**, they can no longer mint unowned FSA properties directly from the game.
- **FSA Selling Cooldown / Weekly Limit:** Players who mint FSA properties are subject to a weekly FSA selling limit (typically 2 FSA properties sold or listed on the secondary market per calendar week) while under Pro status, preventing bots or multi-accounting abuse.

## UPX Earnings, Property Minting & Collections
- **Minting Properties:** Unowned properties appear as **light green** when your Block Explorer passes near them, or **dark green** if they are unowned outside your discovery range (unless unlocked in city releases where non-FSA properties can sometimes be minted without proximity depending on city mechanics).
- **Base UPX Yield:** Every owned property generates passive **UPX yield** (calculated annually as a percentage of the original mint price and paid out continuously, claimable every 3 hours).
- **Collections:** Placing properties into matching **Collections** (such as owning 3 properties on the same street, 3 properties in the same neighborhood, or special city collections) multiplies the passive UPX yield of those properties (e.g., 1.1x to 3.0x boost) and grants a one-time UPX completion reward.
- **Property Colors on the Map:**
  - **Dark Blue:** Properties you own.
  - **Light Blue:** Properties owned by other players (not currently for sale).
  - **Dark Green (with Dollar Sign):** Properties owned by other players that are currently listed for sale on the secondary market.
  - **Light Green:** Unowned properties within your Block Explorer's discovery range that you can mint right now.
  - **Gray:** Unowned properties outside your Block Explorer's range.

## Sends & Travel Mechanics
- **Block Explorer Movement:** Your Block Explorer wanders randomly across the map and leaves a temporary trail.
- **Sends:** To move your Block Explorer directly to a specific property, you use a **Send** (paper airplane icon).
  - You can hold up to **11 Sends** at a time (plus daily free sends you receive upon login).
  - Additional Sends can be picked up inside **Paper Airplanes** scattered on the map.
  - Sending to another player's property costs a small UPX visitor fee set by the property owner (sending to your own properties is **free** and does not cost UPX, though it consumes 1 Send in standard cities).
- **Inter-City Travel:** To travel between cities (e.g., from San Francisco to Manhattan or Miami), players must take a **Train Terminal**, **Bus Terminal**, **Hyperloop**, or **Airport** flight, which costs UPX and takes real travel time.

## Treasure Hunting
Treasure Hunting allows players to earn UPX and **Sparklet** by triangulation:
- **Standard Tier:** Free to spawn once every 24 hours (or for a small UPX fee on cooldown). One player hunts a private chest in their current city.
- **Limited, Exclusive & Rush Tiers:** Competitive multi-player treasure hunts where players race to follow distance/direction arrows from property to property using Sends before someone else claims the chest.
- **Pro Tip for New Players:** Build a small web of inexpensive properties spread across a single city so you can Send to your own properties for **0 UPX visitor fees** while triangulating treasure arrows.

## Spark & Sparklet (Construction & Manufacturing)
- **What is Sparklet?** Sparklet (formerly Spark; 1 Spark = 1,000 Sparklet) is the resource and token used to build structures (houses, apartments, factories, showrooms) on your properties and to power manufacturing and Metaventure operations.
- **How is Sparklet Used?** Sparklet is **staked** (not burned/consumed) during construction. Once a building finishes construction, your staked Sparklet is immediately returned to your account to be staked on another build or rented/staked elsewhere.
- **How to Get Sparklet:** Daily login bonus streaks (completing a 7-day login streak after verifying your account), Treasure Hunting chests, seasonal events, contests, or acquiring Sparklet tokens.
