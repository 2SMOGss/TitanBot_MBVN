# Upland Events & Miami Beach Expansion Patch Notes

**Source:** https://upland.me/events/

## Miami & Miami Beach City Guide & Expansion Notes
Miami and **Miami Beach** are marquee coastal expansions in Upland, featuring waterfront properties, Art Deco district neighborhoods, high-value collections, and seasonal live events.

### Key Highlights of Miami & Miami Beach in Upland
1. **City Tier & Pricing:**
   - Miami and Miami Beach include a mix of **FSA (Fair Start Act)** starter neighborhoods and high-tier beachfront/coastal neighborhoods.
   - Waterfront and South Beach properties are among the most sought-after cultural and commercial hubs for Metaventures, showrooms, and community events.
2. **Miami & Miami Beach Collections:**
   - Players should check the in-game **Collections** tab under Miami before minting or buying on the secondary market to identify Neighborhood, Street, and Ultra-Rare collections.
   - Always verify whether a collection reward has already been claimed on a secondary-market property, though the ongoing **yield boost multiplier** always applies as long as you hold the required number of properties in the collection slot.
3. **Traveling to Miami / Miami Beach:**
   - To reach Miami from another city (such as New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, or San Francisco), head to an **Airport Terminal** in your current city and book a flight to **Miami International Airport (MIA)**, or use the regional train/bus terminal if coming from a connected regional hub.
4. **Miami Events, Hackathons & Community Meetups:**
   - Official Upland live events, city release stress tests, treasure hunt tiers, and seasonal challenges are posted on [https://upland.me/events/](https://upland.me/events/) and announced in the official Upland Discord `#announcements` channel.
   - During city opening events (Vanilla Mode or Standard Mode), properties are released in scheduled neighborhood waves. Check the official event schedule for exact UTC launch windows and FSA allocation percentages.
