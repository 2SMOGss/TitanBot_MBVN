**Unknown values:** If a field is blank or shows a dash (—), that data hasn't been officially confirmed for this seed type. You can enter your own estimate. Seed names that have "N/A" are seeds that cannot yet be planted, and thus the miracle growth and feeding values are unknown.

ℹ️
**Grass and Sod:** Grass and Sod are sold in the [Upland Store](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/upland-upx-store). Grass is sold for UPX, with the default price listed as the best bundle value available. Sod is sold for USD. [Learn more about grass and sod.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/grass-and-sod)

🌿
**Seed Cost (UPX):** Seeds can be purchased directly using **UPX**. However, some players choose to generate their own seeds by spending **[$SPARKLET](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet)** instead. To effectively utilize this calculator, you will have to convert your $SPARKLET to UPX for the sake of these calcuations. This value should be your estimated average seed price.

💰
**Miracle Grow (UPX):** This default value is the UPX cost assuming you will apply the maximum miracle growth to bypass all growing stages of the plant.

📅
**Feeding frequency:** The STEM feeding cost shown here is a *per-plant, per-feeding cycle*. In practice, each plant has its own feeding schedule — determined by its type, growth stage, and current [wellbeing.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/health-and-wellbeing) A plant with low wellbeing may need feeding more frequently. Use the feedings/week as a variable to better estimate the total costs.

🏠 Uppie Cost (UPX)

Optional one-time UPX cost to attract Uppie residents to your property. Added to the total one-time planting cost. This *future feature *will allow you to allocate a level 3 (or higher) Uppie to your property. This Uppie will automatically pet your plants on that property ensuring you maintain a high wellbeing score. [Learn more about merging Uppies](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppy-merge)

The UPX cost represents the total planting cost of all the seeds on your property, and growing them to full size. This includes both the base planting cost per seed and the Miracle Grow cost applied to accelerate growth, summed across every row — plus any Uppie attraction cost added below.

The recurring cost is the STEM cost required to keep your plants healthy on a weekly basis. This number scales directly with how many plants you have, so plan your garden size around what you can comfortably feed each week.
