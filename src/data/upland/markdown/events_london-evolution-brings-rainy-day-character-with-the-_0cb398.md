# London Evolution Brings Rainy-Day Character With The Brolly Shoppe

November 13, 2026

City Evolution returns to London with new property access, chances at properties from the city’s existing Collections, and a signature modular structure inspired by London’s rainy-day character. **European Modular Apartments: The Brolly Shoppe** brings the familiar charm of an umbrella shop to the modular lineup, giving London builders another way to make their blocks feel unmistakably local.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release adds more opportunities to own property across London’s existing footprint. Both pack types carry a pack-level chance of containing an existing London Collection property, while The Brolly Shoppe adds a distinctly London-inspired option to the European modular lineup.

### Neighborhoods

No new London neighborhoods are opening in this release. Pack properties come from London’s existing playable footprint, giving players another way to build within the city they already know.

### Collections

No new London Collection is being introduced. Existing London Collections remain the Collection targets for this release.

The **London Standard Pack has a 10% pack-level chance** of containing an existing London Collection property, while the **London Deluxe Pack has a 20% pack-level chance**. A Collection property is not guaranteed, and these are pack-level odds rather than independent rolls for each property.

### Details

Both **London Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, November 13 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, November 14 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, November 20 at 9 AM PT

### London Standard Pack

Includes one random property in London with a **guaranteed minimum property UPX value of 10,000 UPX** and a **10% pack-level chance** of receiving a property from an existing London Collection.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10% per pack

### London Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in London with a **guaranteed combined minimum property UPX value of 43,000 UPX** and a **20% pack-level chance** of receiving a property from an existing London Collection.

For players building out their London footprint, the Deluxe Pack brings together two properties, this release’s stronger Collection-property chance, and more ways to give a city block unmistakable London character.

**Price:** $20
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 43,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 20% per pack

**European Modular Apartments: The Brolly Shoppe** brings London’s rainy-day rhythm to the block. Picture **Uppies trading weather jokes, gathering beneath their brollies, and carrying on through a passing shower with the neighborhood’s usual good humor**. Its umbrella-shop character gives builders a distinctly London-inspired modular piece and another way to make a street feel lived in.

Like other modular structures, it supports flexible building and long-term customization, giving players more ways to develop city blocks while preserving a strong sense of place and regional identity.

### Looking Ahead

This London Evolution release is about adding depth to an existing city rather than opening new territory. With new property access, pack-level chances at existing London Collections, and **The Brolly Shoppe bringing rainy-day personality to the European modular lineup**, players have more ways to shape their place in London.
