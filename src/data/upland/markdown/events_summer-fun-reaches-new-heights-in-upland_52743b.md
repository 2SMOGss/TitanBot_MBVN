# Summer Fun Reaches New Heights in Upland

July 29, 2026

***Editor’s Note: ****This announcement has been updated to correct the prices listed for The Climbing Collective and The Upland Needle so they accurately match the prices shown in the Upland Store*

**Sizzle Season** is in full swing, and this week’s **Service Structures** are serving up another round of **summer fun** across Upland. From salty boardwalk snacks and beach-ready style to climbing adventures and unforgettable skyline views, this lineup gives players four fresh ways to make their neighborhoods feel like must-visit summer destinations.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, July 29 at 9 AM PT**. As always, all structures are available to players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### The Salty Twist Pretzel Stand

Warm pretzels, salty twists, and the perfect grab-and-go snack for a busy summer day. **The Salty Twist Pretzel Stand** adds a tasty stopping point to boardwalks, festival grounds, shopping districts, and neighborhood gathering spots where **Uppies** can refuel before the next adventure. (Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)

**Spark Hours:** 3,600
**Price:** $1.99

### Beach Feet Sandals

Flip-flops, slides, and breezy footwear made for sand, sun, and long walks by the water. **Beach Feet Sandals** brings laid-back coastal style to beachfront districts, boardwalk shopping rows, resort neighborhoods, and any summer destination where **Uppies** want to keep things cool and comfortable. (Essentials, 3 Service Units)

**Spark Hours:** 12,150
**Price:** $4.99

### The Upland Needle

A soaring landmark with an observatory deck built for taking in the city from above.**The Upland Needle**gives skyline districts, tourist hubs, and landmark-focused neighborhoods an unmistakable centerpiece where**Uppies**can enjoy a summer view that stretches for miles. (Entertainment, 12 Service Units)

**Spark Hours:**61,650
**Price:** $6.99

### The Climbing Collective

Walls, routes, and challenges for **Uppies** ready to trade the beach chair for a new kind of summer thrill. **The Climbing Collective** gives recreation districts, fitness-focused neighborhoods, and energetic downtown builds an active destination where every visit offers another route to conquer. (Essentials, 17 Service Units)

**Spark Hours:** 32,850
**Price:** $9.99
