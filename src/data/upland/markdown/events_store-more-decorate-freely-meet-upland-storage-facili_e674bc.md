*Editor’s Note (November 17): Storage Clarification*

*We’ve seen some questions about how Storage works, so let’s clear a few things up!*

- ***Storage is OPTIONAL****. It’s a convenience feature, not a requirement to own or decorate with map assets.*
- ***Your wallet remains unlimited****. You can continue owning as many map assets as you want. Nothing changes there.*

- ***Your properties aren’t limited by Storage****. Storage does not introduce new restrictions on how many map assets you can place.*

- ***Storage makes decorating easier****. Keep assets you want quick access to and move them between Storage and your properties directly from the new decorating interface, without leaving the property you’re decorating.*

- ***Everyone gets 25 FREE slots****. Every player receives these when Storage launches, with no purchase required.*

- ***Want more convenience?**** Build optional Storage Facilities to expand your capacity when the system launches.* *Here’s the simplest way to think about it:*

- ***Wallet****: Where you own unlimited map assets.*
****
- ***Properties****: Where you place and display your assets.*
****
- ***Storage****: An optional convenience for quickly accessing and moving assets between properties.*
- ***The bottom line****: Storage doesn’t limit what you own or require you to purchase anything. It simply makes decorating with your existing assets easier!*

Let’s take a look at how Storage will work, what’s coming to decorating, and how you can start building your storage capacity today.

### A New Way to Decorate Is Coming

We recently gave players a[first look at the future of decorating in Upland](https://www.upland.me/events/your-property-your-vision-a-whole-new-way-to-build), featuring a redesigned interface that will make customizing your properties easier than ever. ### Introducing Storage

To make decorating easier than ever, we’re introducing **Storage**, a dedicated area within your wallet that will make managing and placing map assets on your properties** more convenient **than ever.

When the new decorating experience launches, **you’ll be able to access Storage directly from the property you’re decorating, without ever leaving the decorating screen.** Simply browse your stored map assets, place them on your property, and return them to Storage whenever you’re ready for a new look. No jumping between properties or navigating back and forth through menus. Everything you need will be right there as you decorate!

**One important distinction: Vehicles are not supported by Storage.** Vehicles cannot be placed in Storage or moved between properties through the Storage interface. Once a vehicle is placed on a property, it must be driven to its next destination.

Every player will receive **25 free storage slots** when the system activates. And don’t worry, **there’s no limit to how many map assets you can own in your wallet.** Storage slots simply determine **how many items you can keep in Storage** as part of the new decorating experience.

### Build More Storage, Unlock More Space

Want more than the default 25 slots? That’s where **Storage Facilities** come in.

Starting with this sale, players can purchase blueprints for four different Storage Facilities and begin constructing them on their properties. Each completed facility will provide additional storage capacity once the Storage system activates.

**Every blueprint is reusable**, meaning you can construct the same Storage Facility as many times as you want, on properties throughout Upland. The more facilities you build, the more storage capacity you’ll have.

**Important:** Purchasing a blueprint or completing a facility won’t immediately unlock storage slots. Additional capacity becomes available when the future Storage system launches, and only for **completed facilities**. Individual blueprints will remain available in the store after the limited-time bundle ends.
