# Your Property. Your Vision. A Whole New Way to Build.

October 06, 2026

**Upland is better with friends.** Share this announcement with someone who’s been away, or a friend who hasn’t joined us yet. Let’s bring them into what we’re **building together**!

We’re becoming **the Upland you always dreamed of**. The Upland you joined to help build.

Troves are just around the corner. But today, we’re giving you a first look at another feature we’re working on, for after** Troves go live**: a new way to **decorate and develop **your properties.

**Property decor isn’t a side feature.** It’s how you make your mark on Upland. Our decorating tools were built for Upland 1.0. It’s time they caught up with your imagination.

The experience we’re developing puts your map assets right at your fingertips. Select your property, scroll through your assets along the bottom of the screen, then **drag and drop them into place**. Less navigating menus. **More bringing your property to life**.

And we’re exploring something we’ve never offered before: **dragging map assets off your property and back into your wallet**. An easier way to change things up, rethink your space, and keep creating.

This is a work-in-progress preview for after Troves launches, **not part of the Troves release**. The interface and features shown may change before release.

See what we’re working on! Join us for a **first look at the new decorating experience** and how it could change the way you bring your properties to life.

Upland is a community game first. Know someone who hasn’t visited in a while? **Send them this preview**. Know someone who would love making a place their own? Introduce them to Upland.

We’re moving fast. **Bring your friends. Bring your community.** Let’s build the Upland we’ve always imagined.
