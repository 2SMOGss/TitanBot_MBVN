# Harvest Season 2026: Fresh Missions, New Discoveries

October 01, 2026

A new season is taking root in Upland. **Harvest Gamified Earnings missions begin Tuesday, October 6**, bringing a simpler seasonal chase: **10 missions, all weekly, with no season-long missions.** Jump into each week’s challenges, work toward next season’s UPX earnings, and get ready for something new to collect with **Troves expected to arrive during October**.

From your first paid send to your final completed mission, Harvest is about getting out into Upland and making progress. Here is what is changing, what is staying, and how to start your run.

### What’s Changing This Harvest

This season, **all 10 missions are weekly missions**, spread across six mission windows. The **season-long Influence Score and Residents missions** are not returning for Harvest. Your Gamified Earnings mission progress will come from completing the challenges revealed each week.

The mission schedule starts on **Tuesday, October 6**, rather than on the season’s October 1 opening day. The final mission window closes on **Tuesday, November 17 at 7 AM PT**.

The format is changing, but the headline earnings targets stay the same: **complete any 5 missions for 14.7% annual UPX earnings, or all 10 for the maximum 15.2%** for the following season.

### Sweat or Build. Pick Your Path.

Every mission this season is labeled** Sweat or Build**. Sweat missions are experiences, racing, treasure hunting, exploring the map. Build missions are about building in Upland, construction, minting, decorating. Two ways to play Harvest Season.

Choose the missions that fit **how you enjoy Upland**, or take on the full set and go for the sweep.

### A Fresh Drop Every Tuesday

One or two new missions drop **every Tuesday at 9 AM PT**, starting October 6. Each week’s completion deadline is the **following Tuesday at 7 AM PT**.

Only the current week’s missions can be completed, and missed missions cannot be recovered. The remaining missions will stay a mystery until their weekly reveal, so check back each Tuesday for your next challenge.

**Mission period:** October 6 through November 17, 2026. The final weekly window runs November 10–17 and keeps its full Tuesday-to-Tuesday schedule.

### Show Up or Go for the Sweep

- Complete **any 5 of the 10 missions** for **14.7% annual UPX earnings** in the following season.
- Complete **all 10 missions** for the maximum **15.2% annual UPX earnings** in the following season. You do not have to complete every mission to reach the five-mission tier. Pick your opportunities, finish them within their weekly windows, and keep building your total.

### How Gamified Earnings Work

Already familiar with Gamified Earnings? You have everything you need above. Here is the full earnings breakdown for players who want a refresher.

Gamified Earnings are **forward-looking**: missions completed during Harvest determine your annual UPX earning rate for the following season, not the rate you receive during Harvest itself.

- **1 mission completed (1.2x):** 5.88% annual UPX earnings
- **2 missions completed (1.5x):** 7.35% annual UPX earnings
- **3 missions completed (2x):** 9.8% annual UPX earnings
- **4 missions completed (2.5x):** 12.25% annual UPX earnings
- **5 missions completed (3x):****14.7% annual UPX earnings**
- **10 missions completed (3.1x):****15.2% annual UPX earnings**, the highest tier

Gamified UPX earning introduces a **forward-looking reward system** where tasks completed in the current season determine the UPX earnings for the following season.

For example,** tasks completed** during **Harvest Season **will set the earning rate for the upcoming **Wonderland Season**. Similarly, activities undertaken in Wonderland Season will influence earnings for the next season, and so on.
