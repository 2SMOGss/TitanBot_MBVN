# Donuts, Books, Quilts, and a Grist Mill Come to Upland

November 04, 2026

This week’s four new **Service Structures** add one Entertainment destination and three Essential businesses to neighborhood builds. **Uppies** can grab a donut, browse for a good read, admire patchwork craft, or meet near the mill. Invite a friend back to explore the new lineup, or bring someone new along to see what the community is building.

These **Service Structures** will be available for a few weeks from **Wednesday, November 4 at 9 AM PT** for layers with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes The Sugar Ring Donut Kiosk, The Dusty Tome Bookshop, The Patchwork Hearth Quilt Shop, and The Willowbrook Grist Mill for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, a **$6 saving** compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, November 4 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, November 5 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Sugar Ring Donut Kiosk

The Sugar Ring Donut Kiosk gives market streets and morning routes a cheerful snack stop, where **Uppies** can compare glazes and pick the ring that looks worth the detour. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### The Dusty Tome Bookshop

The Dusty Tome Bookshop brings a quiet, bookish corner to busy blocks, where **Uppies** can browse old favorites, hunt for a surprising title, and linger between neighborhood errands. *(Essential, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 12,150
**Price:** $5.00

### The Patchwork Hearth Quilt Shop

The Patchwork Hearth Quilt Shop adds handmade warmth to shopping streets and cozy districts, where **Uppies** can admire colorful patterns and imagine the perfect quilt for a favorite room. *(Essential, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 15,750
**Price:** $7.00

### The Willowbrook Grist Mill

The Willowbrook Grist Mill gives rural edges and heritage districts a sturdy centerpiece, where **Uppies** can meet beside the mill and bring a little old-world character to the neighborhood. *(Essential, 5 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 22,050
**Price:** $10.00

Whether **Uppies** are choosing a donut, finding a new book, admiring a warm patchwork design, or gathering near the mill, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four more ways to shape neighborhoods with personality. Collectors who want all four can grab the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share the lineup with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.
