# Harvest Finds, Vintage Treasures, and a Savory Stop

October 07, 2026

Another chapter of **Storybook Harvest** is ready for your neighborhood. This week’s four new **Service Structures** bring farm-market charm, vintage toys, curious finds, and a savory snack stop to your streets. Invite a friend back to explore your autumn neighborhood, or introduce someone new to the community you are building.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, October 7 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the Service Structure Value Pack, which includes Salem Shawarma Kiosk, Black Cat Curiosities Shop, Olde Wonder Toys, and Old Crow Farm Market for $18. That is 25% off regular pricing, a $6 saving compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for **24 hours only**, from **Wednesday, October 7 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, October 8 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### Salem Shawarma Kiosk

Follow the savory aroma down a crooked lane to Salem Shawarma Kiosk, a comforting little stop for **Uppies** with a taste for midnight wandering. It brings warm, bustling charm to market corners and storybook streets. If a shadow joins the queue behind you, perhaps it is just hungry too. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### Black Cat Curiosities Shop

A little peculiar, a little inviting, and probably best explored before dark. Black Cat Curiosities Shop belongs on the sort of winding street where every doorway has a story. Give your **Uppies** a charming stop for unusual finds, just do not be surprised if one seems to be looking back. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 10,800
**Price:** $5.00

### Olde Wonder Toys

Childhood wonder has a slightly mischievous side at Olde Wonder Toys. This nostalgic little shop brings storybook charm to historic shopping rows and quiet village squares. Your **Uppies** may come for a favorite old toy and leave wondering whether that rocking horse was moving when they arrived. *(Entertainment, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,500
**Price:** $7.00

### Old Crow Farm Market

The harvest is plentiful, the welcome is warm, and the crows seem unusually interested in your shopping. Old Crow Farm Market brings rustic charm and a whisper of local folklore to country lanes and autumn village squares. Give your **Uppies** a cozy neighborhood stop where even a simple market run feels like the beginning of a story. *(Essentials, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 14,500
**Price:** $10.00

Whether players are browsing market stalls, rediscovering old favorites, seeking out curiosities, or stopping for shawarma, this week’s **Service Structures** bring more character to **Storybook Harvest** neighborhoods. Collectors who want all four can pick up the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share your neighborhood with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
