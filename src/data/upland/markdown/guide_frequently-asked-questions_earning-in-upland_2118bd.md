# How to Earn in Upland : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/earning-in-upland
Upland is a play to earn game enabling you to earn USD for playing! This site details all the ways you can earn UPX, Sparklet and USD while playing!

Earning in Upland takes on several forms. There are three types of currency that you can earn in Upland.

- UPX - the in game currency within Upland (not to be confused with the crypto coin of the same name. They are unrelated).

- US Dollars

- [Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet), an ERC-20 crypto token on the Ethereum blockchain. This utility token is used to power the creation of non-living elements in Upland

- [STEM](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem), the token responsible for sustaining all living things in the metaverse.

In addition to these the [Stem](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem)token is an in game utility token that powers living items in Upland.

Earn property yield 



Every property in Upland produces an annual yield.  The base annual yield is currently 4.9%.  Through consistent engagement players can increase this yield to up to 15.2%  [Learn more about these gamified yields.](https://upland-guide.com/economy/gamified-upx-earnings) These earnings can be collected on a 3 hour basis. The yield is calculated from the mint price of the property. [Read hear to understand the property card](https://upland-guide.com/property-card)and how to calculate important factors like mint price, neighborhood value and monthly earnings.





** 
** 

Completing a property collection 



By completing a property collection within Upland, you are rewarded with a one time bonus for completing it. The rarer the collection is to complete, the higher the bonus is.

You can also complete collections by minting collection properties, or buying or trading them on the secondary market. Certain collections require you to hold the properties in that collection for a minimum period in order to collect the one time bonus.

[New neighborhood collections](https://upland-guide.com/community/neighborhood-ratings) can also be spawned through neighborhood rating collections.

[Read more about collections](https://upland-guide.com/upland-metaverse-strategy/collections) here, and the [strategy for creating your first collections](https://upland-guide.com/collections-strategy).





** 
** 

Earn boosted yield from collection properties 



Once you have completed a collection, the properties you have selected to be a part of that collection have their earnings multiplied by the factor of that specific collection, providing you with a greater yield. You only earn this additional boost if the collection is active. These earnings are also paid out on a 3 hour basis.

Treasure hunting 



Treasure hunts involve you sending your block explorers to different properties within a city to try and find a treasure within a short period of time. If you are able to find the treasure you will win UPX or there is also a possibility of winning [spark](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/spark). Note that you are not guaranteed to win, and it can cost you money, as you must use sends to travel across a city. There is a learning curve for treasure hunting and it is not recommended for new players with a small net worth and minimal liquidity. Part of the strategy is owning a large number of properties spread across the city so that you can send yourself to your own properties, which do not cost UPX or using up a send. If you are able to master the skill of treasure hunting, this can be a good source of revenue. If you haven't started hunting yet, make sure you read up on our [top 5 things to understand before treasure hunting.](https://upland-guide.com/top-5-things-to-understand-before-treasure-hunting)

** 
** 

Collect visiting fees 



When someone visits a property that is not their own, the player must pay the visitor fee. Depending on the city, the visiting fees vary. The minimum and maximum prices can vary from 5 UPX to 100 UPX. Within the fixed range, the owner of a property is able to set the visiting fees for each of their properties. It is important to note that if you receive a visit from a visitor, the fee is not immediately paid out. Only once he becomes an uplander is the visitor fee paid out. If you are playing for free, there are many communities who will visit your properties to help you reach uplander status. Aside from this, do not expect that receiving visits will be a substantial source of revenue for you. Read more about sends [here](https://upland-guide.com/visiting-fees-and-sends).

Rent out sparklet 




Sparklet is the resource used to build structures on your properties. Read more about sparklet and how to get it [here](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet). If you own sparklet, there are numerous people and/or groups that are willing to pay you to rent your spark.  In the future there will be the ability within the game to rent out your sparklet, but for now all the deals are made directly between the players, typically within discord channels. You can be paid by UPX transfers, visits to your property purchasing of burner properties, etc.

Transfer UPX 



Many players have the need to transfer UPX between players.  One example of this is if a player is running a contest.  If I wanted to give away 100,000 UPX, historically this was done through buying a "burner" property from another player.  This features (introduced February 2022) allows for the transfer of UPX without it being tied to an asset.  Note that in order to send/receive UPX in the fashion you must have completed the KYC process for the ability to sell for USD.
