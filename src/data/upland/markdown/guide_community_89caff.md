# Community : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/community
The three pillars of the Upland Metaverse are [play](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment), [earn](https://upland-guide.com/economy)and connect. As social animals, humans want to connect to each other, build and belong to communities. The Metaverse is no different. If you always wanted to live in Hollywood or Manhattan, Upland provides you with the opportunity to do so. You can find like minded players, connect and begin to develop your neighborhood. Players are even able to spawn [new neighborhood collections](https://upland-guide.com/community/neighborhood-ratings) by working together with their neighbors and developing their neighborhoods.

The most important part of connecting with others and being part of a community is having an identity. Having an identity is crucial for self-expression and defining individuality. It facilitates social interaction, shaping how a person is perceived by others and contributing to their opportunities and relationships. It also promotes mental health by providing a sense of self-worth and purpose, aiding in decision-making that aligns with values and beliefs. Through 3D avatars and block explorers, you are able to express yourself in Upland. [Learn more about that here.](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity)

Players are able to connect in [cafes](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/upland-cafes), which are immersive 3D [metaventures](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures)where you can chat with friends and hold community events. Upland's City Center Plaza, located at 52 Grove St., San Francisco, where you can learn more about Upland, and enter the Community Corner where large scale events will be held. [Learn more about the City Center Plaza here.](https://youtu.be/tPlJZ2vRP1k)

You have the ability to define your Upland [permanent residence](https://upland-guide.com/community/governance-and-voting) and have [voting rights](https://upland-guide.com/community/governance-and-voting/how-to-vote) on important things that relate to your neighborhood and city. Unlike other Metaverses, Upland has created barriers to [travelling](https://upland-guide.com/transportation). Moving between cities takes time and costs you UPX. This will add further importance to the need to choose your permanent residence wisely. Just like in the real world, there will be players who won't frequently travel far from home and this will further strengthen the community aspect within Upland.

The Upland Guide also has a [community blog](https://upland-guide.com/updates) that you can read here.
