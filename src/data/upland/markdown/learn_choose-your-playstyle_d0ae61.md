# Choose Your Playstyle

Upland is one system with many ways to play. You don't have to pick one. Most Architects mix several. But knowing which one is calling you helps you make smart early decisions.

**District Builder.** Acquires adjacent properties, places structures, sets a theme, attracts residents. Loves seeing a neighborhood come to life.

**Marketplace Trader.** Spots undervalued properties before openings, masters collections, flips neighborhoods at the right moment, and outsmarts the competition.

**Metaventure Owner.** Opens a shop. Sells his or others' Architect's creations, vehicles, and ornaments. Earns revenue from other players.

**Event Competitor.** Races, treasure hunts, completes seasonal missions, climbs leaderboards.

**Manufacturer & Logistics.** Produces assets in factories, manages SPARKLET, runs Trade Routes.

**Collector.** Collects properties, befriends Uppies, and merges them up to Iconic Parody Uppies.

**Farmer.** Plants, harvests, raises animals. Produces Troves at scale and earns STEM through gameplay.

You can switch tracks, combine them, or invent new ones. The map remembers every choice.
