# Hot Dogs, High Scores, Axes, and Paintball Hit Upland

August 26, 2026

**Sizzle Season** is going strong with a lineup built for classic boardwalk food and competitive fun. From hot dogs and high scores to axe throwing and paintball battles, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four new ways to turn their neighborhoods into lively destinations where **Uppies** can eat, play, and compete.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, August 26 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Each individual structure is scheduled to remain available until **Wednesday, September 23 at 9 AM PT**, giving players roughly four weeks to add it to their collection. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes all four of this week’s new structures for **$17.99**. That is **25% off** the cost of buying them individually, making it the best way to add the entire release to one collection.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, August 26 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, August 27 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately through September 23.

### Coney Island Franks

Classic hot dogs, quick service, and plenty of boardwalk flavor. Coney Island Franks gives waterfront districts, festival grounds, shopping streets, and busy neighborhood corners a casual food stop where **Uppies** can grab a bite before the next adventure. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $1.99

### Blockade Arcade

Rows of cabinets, flashing screens, and one more chance to beat the high score. Blockade Arcade brings competitive gaming energy to entertainment districts, shopping centers, family neighborhoods, and nightlife builds where **Uppies** can play for bragging rights. *(Entertainment, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 14,400
**Price:** $4.99

### Upland Axes

Targets are lined up and the axes are ready to fly. Upland Axes adds an axe-throwing destination to sports districts, nightlife areas, recreation hubs, and competitive neighborhoods where **Uppies** can test their aim and settle friendly rivalries. *(Entertainment, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $6.99

### Paintball Upland

Obstacles, cover, and a full paintball course built for fast-moving team battles. Paintball Upland gives recreation districts, sports complexes, and high-energy communities a major destination where **Uppies** can plan their attack, hold the line, and chase victory. (Entertainment, 23 Service Units)

**Spark Hours:** 30,600
**Price:** $9.99

Whether players are serving hot dogs, chasing high scores, throwing axes, or battling across a paintball course, this week’s **Service Structures** bring another round of food, games, and competition to **Sizzle Season**. Collectors who want all four can save with the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears.
