# XStacks : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/xstacks-home
The first casual game on X is XStacks - a Tetris like game created by Upland where you can win Sparklet rewards

XStacks is a play and earn 3d blocks mini game where players can win [Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/courses/into-to-sparklet)- the utility token that is used to build the Upland Metaverse. XStacks is the first X-native game that is not only made on X, but it's made ** *for * **X. This innovative game leverages the social connectivity of the X platform where its powered by the community that turn your posts on X into integral part of the gameplay.

Make sure to follow [Upland](https://x.com/UplandMe), [XStacks](https://x.com/XStacks_Game), and us - the [Upland Guide](https://x.com/upland_guide) on X.

Score points by clearing blocks similar to Tetris, completing the 99 available levels, and by sharing links to your completed level on X. When players complete a level through your link, you will earn as a bonus 10% of the points they were awarded for that level.

All points awarded help to contribute to your team's score. Each team is a city that exists in the Upland metaverse. Players who contribute points to the top 3 teams at the end of the campaign share in the Sparklet prize pool.

XStacks players don't need to be a player in Upland in order to get started with XStacks, but in order to claim any Sparklet rewards you will need to have reached Uplander status within the game and [connect your XStacks account to your Upland account](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/xstacks-home/connect-upland-account). [Learn how to become an Uplander here](https://upland-guide.com/upland-metaverse-strategy/becoming-an-uplander), and below this video you can see a step by step instructions on how to get started.
