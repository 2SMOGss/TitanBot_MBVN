# Sizzle Season 2026 Is Heating Up: Sweat, Build, Earn

July 31, 2026

Sizzle Season missions are live, and the chase for next season’s earnings starts now. The new **Sweat and Build system** follows the ways Uplanders love to play. **Mission costs are down from last season** because we heard the feedback.

### Sweat or Build. Pick Your Path.

Every mission this season is labeled Sweat or Build. Sweat missions are experiences, racing, treasure hunting, exploring the map. Build missions are about building in Upland, construction, minting, decorating. Two ways to play Sizzle Season.

### A Fresh Drop Every Tuesday

There are **10 missions total**: two season-long missions live now and eight weekly missions revealed throughout the season.

One or two new missions drop **every Tuesday at 9 AM PT**. The deadline is the following **Tuesday at 7 AM PT**. Only the current week’s missions can be completed, and missed missions cannot be recovered. Every Tuesday is a fresh chance to jump back in.

### Show Up or Go for the Sweep

- Complete **any 5 of the 10 missions** for a **14.7% earnings**, the reward for showing up regularly.
- Complete **all 10 missions** for the maximum **15.2% earnings** and finish the full Sizzle run. With an activated Sizzle Season Pass, completionists can also bring home **Wave Rider 26**, an exclusive surfboard trophy Block Explorer. The Season Pass Wildcard can cover one missed weekly mission for Block Explorer eligibility.

### The Season-Long Chase Is Live

- Net increase in Influence Score
- Net increase in Residents Both are measured from **Wednesday, July 15 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, September 15 at 9 AM PT**.

The baseline is the end of last season, so growth between seasons already counts for you, and there is no benefit to shrinking builds before a season starts.

### Week 1: Send and Sizzle (Aug 4–11)

The opening drop runs from **Tuesday, August 4 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, August 11 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Sweat:** Do 10 paid sends to 10 unique properties in Upland
- **Build:** Decorate 5 structures with Sizzle Season Ornaments. Must be one ornament per property to count. Get moving, fire up the decorations, and put your first two missions on the board.

## Week 2: Building Boom (Aug 11–18)

This week’s mission runs from**Tuesday, August 11 at 9 AM PT**to**Tuesday, August 18 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Build: **Begin construction on 3 different Living Structures on 3 different properties anywhere in Upland. Time to break ground and get building.

## Week 3: Race Ready (Aug 18-25)

This week's mission runs from **Tuesday, August 18 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, August 25 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Sweat:** Complete 5 public multiplayer Kart races.

Hit the track, finish five races, and put this week's Sweat mission on the board.

## Week 4: Amsterdam Bound (Aug 25-Sep 1)

This week's missions run from **Tuesday, August 25 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, September 1 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Sweat:** Travel to Amsterdam.
- **Build:** Mint 1 property in Amsterdam. Make your way to Amsterdam, claim your first property, and put both missions on the board.

## Week 5: Grow Your Roots (Sep 1-8)

This week's mission runs from **Tuesday, September 1 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, September 8 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Build:** Plant any 3 plants (excluding grass) on a property you own. Get planting, add some life to your property, and put this week's Build mission on the board.

## Week 6: Treasure Rush (Sep 8-15)

This week's mission runs from **Tuesday, September 8 at 9 AM PT** to **Tuesday, September 15 at 7 AM PT**.

- **Sweat:** Collect 5 Standard Treasures in Treasure Hunt. Start hunting, track down five treasures, and put this week's Sweat mission on the board.

### The Map Is Just Getting Warm

Something big is coming to the map this season. Follow Upland on **Discord and X** for each Tuesday’s fresh mission drop.

#### *How Gamified Earnings Work*

*The earning increase in Upland is structured around the completion of tasks, providing a clear incentive for player activity and engagement through a series of tiered rewards:*

***1 Task Complete (1.2x):**** 5.88% annual earning
****2 Tasks Complete (1.5x):**** 7.35% annual earning
****3 Tasks Complete (2x):**** 9.8% annual earning
****4 Tasks Complete (2.5x):**** 12.25% annual earning
****5 Tasks Complete (3x):**** 14.7% annual earning
****10 Tasks Complete (3.1x):**** 15.2% annual earning, the highest tier*

*Gamified UPX earning introduces a ****forward-looking reward system**** where tasks completed in the current season determine the UPX earnings for the following season.*

*For example,**** tasks completed**** during ****Sizzle Season ****will set the earning rate for the upcoming ****Harvest Season****. Similarly, activities undertaken in Harvest Season will influence earnings for the next season, and so on.*
