# Kansas City Evolution Brings the Liquor Store Edition to the Block

September 25, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Kansas City** with a fresh opportunity to grow your property footprint and add a new storefront to your builds. This release introduces the **East Coast Modular: Liquor Store Edition**, the signature structure included in the Deluxe Pack.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this Evolution keeps the focus on property access and neighborhood character. Standard Packs offer a **guaranteed minimum property value of 10,000 UPX**, while Deluxe Packs pair a **guaranteed combined minimum property value of 60,000 UPX** with the new modular structure.

### Neighborhoods

**No new neighborhoods** are opening in this release. Pack properties come from **Kansas City**’s existing footprint.

### Collections

**No new Collections** are being introduced with this Evolution.

### Details

Both **Kansas City Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, September 25 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, September 26 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, October 2 at 9 AM PT

### Kansas City Standard Pack

Includes one random property in **Kansas City** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX

### Kansas City Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in **Kansas City** with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 60,000 UPX**, plus the **East Coast Modular: Liquor Store Edition**.

For players looking to grow their property portfolio and add another piece to their modular builds, the Deluxe Pack brings both together in one release.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 60,000 UPX combined

Give your next block a little more local character. The **East Coast Modular: Liquor Store Edition** brings a liquor-store theme to the East Coast modular lineup, adding another storefront option for players shaping their neighborhoods.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Kansas City Deluxe Pack in this release**.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about **building cities with depth**, not just opening new territory. This **Kansas City** release gives players another opportunity to establish a foothold, grow an existing portfolio, or add a new storefront to a modular build.

Choose the pack that fits your next move and bring a little more character to **Kansas City**.
