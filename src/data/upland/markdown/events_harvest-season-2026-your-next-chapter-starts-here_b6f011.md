# Harvest Season 2026: Your Next Chapter Starts Here

October 01, 2026

Golden leaves. Lantern-lit streets. A bakery on the corner and a new story waiting down the road. **Storybook Harvest** is coming to Upland, bringing four collectible Season Pass designs, weekly cosmetic drops, and a **Harvest Fusion Pass** for collectors ready to bring the whole story together.

And this season, there is something even bigger to discover: **Troves are coming.** With their launch expected during October, Harvest is shaping up to be a season of fresh finds, new collection goals, and more reasons to explore the world you have been building.

**Harvest Season begins Thursday, October 1.** Whether you are putting the finishing touches on a favorite property, growing your collection, or getting ready for your first Trove discoveries, it is time to turn the page.

### Welcome to Storybook Harvest

This season trades summer heat for the charm of an autumn storybook: warm storefronts, colorful trees, cozy neighborhood corners, and a little mystery after sunset. It is a celebration of the places that make a neighborhood feel like home, and the discoveries that make you want to see what is around the next corner.

### Harvest Season Pass: One Burn Unlocks the Season

The **Harvest Season Pass** keeps the same simple approach: buy a pass and **burn it to activate**. One activation makes your account eligible for subsequent weekly Harvest cosmetic drops and adds a season-specific badge to your profile.

There is no separate pass-level track to grind through. Activate, collect the weekly rewards, and bring together the four unique Harvest character collectibles needed to qualify for Fusion.

**What Your Pass Unlocks:**

- One of four unique, collectible Harvest pass designs
- Six weekly cosmetic drops, with one new drop each week
- A **Wildcard Token** for Gamified Earnings Completionists
- A **Harvest season badge** for your profile
- A contribution to your **Seasonal and All-Time Collector Score** through Season Passes and Badges
- The opportunity to complete the **Harvest Fusion Pass** collection **Price:** $6.99

### Troves Are Coming This Harvest

The season’s biggest discovery is still ahead. **Troves are expected to launch during October**, opening a new collecting and progression experience in Upland.

The Genesis Week beta offered a first look at 180 Troves reaching three levels deep. For launch, the team is preparing **over 500 Troves and deeper evolving recipes** alongside a **brand-new progression system**. That means a much larger collection to explore and more discoveries waiting beyond your first find.

Think of Harvest as the start of a new chapter: seasonal collectibles to pursue week by week, and a new Trove journey on the horizon. Give your favorite properties some attention, keep your plants cared for, and get ready to discover what comes next.

The exact Troves launch date will be shared once confirmed. Troves are a separate gameplay release, not a reward included with the Season Pass, and later features such as Uppie Quests and Trove-collection automation will follow separately.

### Activate Once, Collect All Season

Each week, Upland takes a snapshot of eligible players at **7:00 AM PT on Tuesday**. To receive that week’s cosmetic drop, your **Harvest Season Pass** must be activated by burning it before the snapshot.

You only need to **burn one pass for the entire season**. Once activated, your account remains eligible for all subsequent weekly cosmetic drops. You do not need to burn all four designs to activate the weekly rewards.

Missing a weekly snapshot means missing that week’s drop. Activate early to give yourself the best chance of collecting the complete weekly set. Weekly rewards are tied to your account after delivery.

### Mission Wildcard Token: Protection for Completionists

Every burned **Harvest Season Pass** includes a **Wildcard Token**. If you miss exactly one weekly Gamified Earnings mission, the Wildcard automatically fills that single gap at the end of the season, protecting your eligibility for the **Completionist Block Explorer reward**.

**How It Works:**

- Included with every burned **Harvest Season Pass**
- Applies only to weekly Gamified Earnings missions
- Automatically activates if you miss exactly one weekly mission
- Does not count toward the five-mission minimum for general Gamified Earnings rewards
- Can be used only once The Wildcard protects your Completionist run; it does not replace missions for earnings qualification. Full Harvest mission requirements and rewards will be covered in the separate Gamified Earnings announcement.

### Complete the Collection and Earn the Harvest Fusion Pass

The **Harvest Fusion Pass** is the final piece of the **Storybook Harvest** collection. To qualify, assemble **all four unique Harvest character collectibles** and make sure they are associated with your account when the Fusion snapshot is taken.

Whether you **keep the characters in your wallet or burn them**, all four must count toward your account at snapshot time. You must then maintain the complete qualifying collection for at least **24 hours after the snapshot** so eligibility can be verified.

- **Fusion Pass Snapshot:** Tuesday, November 10, 2026 at 9:00 AM PT
- **Requirement:** All four unique Harvest characters must be in your account at snapshot time
- **Hold Time:** Keep the complete qualifying collection in your account for at least 24 hours after the snapshot

Start with one pass. Activate it. Collect your weekly drops. Bring the four Harvest characters together and set your sights on Fusion. Along the way, make your neighborhoods feel like autumn and get ready for a new world of Trove discoveries.

From Hearthside Bakery to Lantern Lore, every corner of **Storybook Harvest** has its own character. The next chapter belongs to the players who bring it to life.
