# Add Some British flavor with London Map Assets

May 02, 2023

In celebration of the successful launch of London in Upland, we are excited to announce the addition of London Map Assets. These Map Assets are designed to add posh British vibes to your properties and feature the theme of King's Guards Chess Pieces.

‍

Starting today, there are 14 London Map Asset Chess Piece designs currently in production at the newly established Upland Bureau Factory. These Map Assets will be available for purchase at any of the five newly opened Upland Bureau Showroom locations throughout Upland, as they come off the production line.

To add to the excitement, we will be holding a London Map Asset giveaway where players will have the opportunity to win Rare Chess Pieces! Be sure to check out all the details below and get ready to turn your Upland Property into a chess board!
