# April Spark Week is Here!

April 14, 2023

Get ready to ignite your excitement because April Spark Week has arrived! This is your chance to get your hands on some more Spark – the ultimate resource that can bring any Map Assets and property structures to life. [Spark is what makes our cities](https://www.upland.me/guides-and-tutorials/spark-property-development) and neighborhoods in Upland stand out, so make sure to seize this chance to score some Spark before it’s gone! April Spark Week officially begins **Sunday, April 16 at 9 AM PT** and will end on **Monday April 24 at 9 AM PT**. 

‍

Spark week this month comes with Status-Up Spark Rewards, Sparked Up Treasure Hunt Tier, and a Spark Sale! Read on for all the details. 

## Status-Up Spark Rewards

One of the best ways to earn extra free Spark during Spark Week is by leveling up your Upland Status, which offers greater rewards than the standard Spark earned from leveling up. So make sure to take advantage of this opportunity if you are close to a level-up!

‍

Status-Up Spark Rewards run from **Monday, April 17 at 9 AM PT to Monday, April 24 at 9 AM PT. **

‍

The amount of Spark rewarded differs for each level. Here’s a breakdown: ‍

‍

- Visitor to Uplander: 0.06 Spark‍
- Uplander to Pro: 0.12 Spark‍
- Pro to Director: 0.6 Spark‍
- Director to Executive: 1.2 Spark‍
- Executive to Chief Executive: 2.4 Spark‍

## New to Spark? 

If you’re a new player or are just trying to [figure out what Spark is and what it does](https://www.upland.me/guides-and-tutorials/spark-property-development), no problem! In Upland, we are simulating many aspects of the real world. There is a lot of building in Upland, including Structures on properties, as well as things like cars and Map Assets in factors. 

‍

Think of Spark as fuel that keeps [Upland factories](https://www.upland.me/events/new-structure-factories) running and feeds the little llama construction workers building on properties. (Okay, we don’t actually have llama construction workers, but use your imagination!)

‍

Every object that needs to be built or constructed takes a minimum amount of Spark. The more Spark put into building something, the faster that object is built. 

‍

For example, if you have a new property, you’ll need Spark to build a structure on the property. You’d do this if you want to have an [Upland Home Address](https://www.upland.me/events/upland-home-addresses-the-beginning-of-community-governance) or [Metaventure Shop](https://www.upland.me/metaventures) on your property. 

‍

Unlike UPX, Spark isn’t available at any time. There are certain events – such as Spark Week – when players can earn or buy Spark. If you plan on building in Upland, be sure to grab Spark while you can!
