# Getting Started

## First steps to get started

1. **Create your account** (an email is enough; see the next section).
2. **Pick a city & neighborhood** to start your portfolio.
3. **Complete collections** and beginner tasks for momentum.
4. **Join your local community** (in‑game chat & Discord) to find opportunities.
5. **Set your priorities:** flip properties, develop a block, open a shop, or chase event rewards.

## The Architect's loop

Every session in Upland runs the same simple loop. Master it once, and every other system in the game starts to make sense. Remember: Upland is a forever game; there is no "end goal". It's just you who defines what to achieve.


The harder you play, the more the city compounds. Scores, Troves, and Recipes are covered in detail in the **Uppies, Life & Troves** section.
