### What are Construction Vehicles?

Meet the **hardest-working vehicles in Upland**. Construction Vehicles represent the vital infrastructure behind every great city, and soon they’ll be **playable assets** that can help you, and your community, **build smarter and faster**.

The **Motor Grader**, **Road Roller**, and **Wrecking Ball Crane** will play a key role in **construction **gameplay. Placing these vehicles on properties under development **reduces total build times**, giving players who own and deploy these trucks a **real advantage**. Even better? In the future, you’ll be able to offer their services to others, **expanding your business empire** with mobile **construction support**.

Some of these features are not yet live, but are** actively being developed**. This sale is both an opportunity to** prepare early** by securing the vehicles that will drive these **upcoming systems, **as well as a chance to take advantage of their current utility.
