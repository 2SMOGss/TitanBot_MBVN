# Sparklet Warz : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/sparklet-warz
Sparklet Warz is Upland's newest layer 1 collaborative tower defense game. The goal is for your faction to earn the most points through strategic building, structure decoration and asset placement. With prizes including [Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet), [badges](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity/upland-badges), and [block explorers](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers), there is a more to these warz than faction pride. [Learn more about the Sparklet Warz prizes here.](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/sparklet-warz/prizes)

Sparklet Warz occur in the 2 week downtime period between [Upland season](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons), with the inaugural Sparklet Warz in open beta occurring in January 2025 following the Wonderland Season.

Learn about how to [register](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/sparklet-warz/registration)for the competition [score points](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/sparklet-warz/scoring), and how to [attack/defend](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/sparklet-warz/weapons) your factions structures.
