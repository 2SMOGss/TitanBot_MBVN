# Trove Recipes : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/recipes
Recipes are what give [troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves)in Upland a purpose. Whether it is to [mint an Uppie through quests](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppie-quests), or to progress to create a different type of trove, players are required to collect specific combinations of troves, or recipes. For Uppie quests, each Uppie will require a different recipe, and certain troves will have fixed recipes found through service structures. For example, wheat troves and milk troves could be brought to a bakery, and combined to receive bread troves. Bakeries are a form of service structure that will play a large part in the trove economy. [Learn more about service structures here.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types/service-structures)This bread trove isn't a collectible but something that is required elsewhere. In order to befriend or mint an Uppie, it may require a certain number of bread troves.

There are a variety of troves to be collected in the following categories. As more information about these are released, this page will be updated accordingly.

- Care

- Resource - these types of troves are harvested from STEM-bearing plants. These troves can then be exchanged for [STEM](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem).

- Social

- Utility

Some troves convert directly into STEM, while others are used to create new items, fulfill requests, or unlock [progression](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/progressions-score). Over time, nearly everything in Upland can become part of a recipe, either as an input or an output.

Recipes are fixed. Utilizing the example below, to craft a pocket knife, you require flint, a hinge, and clay. Its possible to receive a pocket knife trove through other recipes or even discovering it at a structure, but a flint, hinge and clay troves can always be exchanged for that pocket knife trove. There are also dynamic recipes, which come in the form of [Uppie quests.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppie-quests)

While fulfilling recipes, you have a limit to the number of troves you can collect based on your block explorer's stamina, however crafting troves does not decrease your stamina. Ensure you have several (and rarer) block explorer to enable yourself to keep fulfilling recipes and collecting troves before having to cool down. [Learn more about the stamina feature.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers/stamina) When placing an Uppie on your property, certain [level Uppies](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility) are able to collect troves automatically - this feature is independent from the block explorer stamina which only takes into account manually collected troves.
