The wait is over. **Amsterdam, Netherlands** is coming to Upland on Thursday, August 27th at 9 AM PT.

Known for its **iconic canals, historic architecture, and unmistakable character**, Amsterdam brings a fresh European destination to Upland. Players will have new streets to explore, new collections to chase, and new opportunities to build a lasting presence in one of the world’s most recognizable cities.

Whether you are looking to secure property early, collect limited edition items, expand onto the waterfront, or prepare for the full city release, **Amsterdam offers something for every kind of Uplander**. Get ready to explore, collect, and build in the Netherlands.

### Amsterdam City Pass Sale

The City Pass is back, and this time, it celebrates the spirit of Amsterdam.

This beautifully designed, limited-edition digital pass captures the energy and character of one of Europe’s most iconic cities. Whether you are a **passionate collector** or a dedicated city builder, this pass **adds lasting value** to your Upland portfolio.

Holding a pass **at the snapshot date** will **permanently unlock** the ability to build the exclusive **Amsterdam Townhouse **on any of your properties across Amsterdam. This structure can **only be built within the city of Amsterdam**.

Beyond its visual appeal, the Amsterdam City Pass boosts both your All-Time and current season Collector Scores, making it a valuable asset for dedicated collectors and city builders alike.

**What You Get with an Amsterdam City Pass:**

- 15% chance to reserve a Collection property
- Tradable pass that adds to your Collector Score
- Permanent unlock for a unique **Amsterdam Townhouse**

**Registration Start:** Monday, August 24 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Start:** Tuesday, August 25 at 9 AM PT
**Price:** $5
**Quantity:** 1,000 Passes
**Snapshot Date:** Friday, August 28 at 9 AM PT
**Mint Period:** Friday, August 28 through Monday, August 31, ending at 9 AM PT

Players who hold the pass on the snapshot date will receive a specific Amsterdam property address to mint, along with the ability to build their unique Amsterdam structure. You will need to travel to Amsterdam to mint your property before the window closes. Any unminted properties will be released for public minting after the deadline.

While not a modular structure, The Amsterdam Townhouse looks great built side by side.
