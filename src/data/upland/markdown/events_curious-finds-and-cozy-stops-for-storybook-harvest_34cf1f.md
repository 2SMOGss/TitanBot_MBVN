# Curious Finds and Cozy Stops for Storybook Harvest

September 30, 2026

**Harvest Season starts October 1**, and another chapter of **Storybook Harvest** is arriving just ahead of it. This week’s four new **Service Structures** bring seaside snacks, curious antiques, cozy cafe stops, and a mysterious hotel to the neighborhood. Continue building out your old New England-inspired streets with a lineup made for autumn wandering and a little Salem-style intrigue.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, September 30 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. The individual structures are scheduled to remain available until **Wednesday, November 11 at 9 AM PT**. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes all four of this week’s new structures for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, making it an easy way to bring the whole **Storybook Harvest** lineup to your collection.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for **24 hours only**, from **Wednesday, September 30 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, October 1 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Crooked Cod

A savory snack stop with a name straight out of an old harbor town. The Crooked Cod brings a fishball kiosk to waterfront walks, market squares, and winding village streets. It’s a natural addition to a seaside neighborhood where **Uppies** could use a quick bite between stops. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### Cobweb & Co Antiques

Every good antique shop feels like it has a few stories tucked away. Cobweb & Co Antiques brings that sense of discovery to historic shopping streets, village centers, and autumn market districts. Give your **Uppies** a neighborhood stop for curious finds and old-fashioned charm. *(Essentials, 2 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 15,300
**Price:** $5.00

### The Tipsy Teapot Cafe

A warm cup, a little gossip, and no particular reason to rush. The Tipsy Teapot Cafe adds a cozy gathering spot to town squares, leafy side streets, and storybook shopping rows. It’s the kind of neighborhood cafe that makes an autumn afternoon feel worth lingering over. *(Entertainment, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 18,000
**Price:** $7.00

### The Grimwood Hotel

A welcoming stay at a hotel **rumored to be haunted**. The Grimwood Hotel brings a little local legend to historic districts, village gateways, and autumn getaways. Pair it with nearby shops and a cafe to create a storybook destination for visiting Uppies — just don’t ask who’s staying in the room upstairs.*(Essentials, 21 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 49,500
**Price:** $10.00
