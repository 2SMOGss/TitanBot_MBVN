# Madrid Evolution Serves Up the Mantequeria

August 21, 2026

City Evolution returns to Madrid this week with a major expansion into **five new neighborhoods: Costillares, Castilla, Valdezarza, Valdeacederas, and Almenara**. The release also introduces **Valdezarza, a new Standard Collection**, and the all-new **European Modular Apartments: Mantequeria Edition**.

This week’s Madrid Evolution Pack includes a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property**. The Deluxe Pack also introduces another much-needed **middle structure** for European modular builds. With plenty of corner pieces already available but fewer **middle structures** to connect them, the Mantequeria is a **big addition** for builders who want to create longer, more complete street-facing developments.

### Neighborhoods

Five new Madrid neighborhoods are opening with this release:

- Costillares
- Castilla
- Valdezarza
- Valdeacederas
- Almenara These additions expand Madrid’s footprint with fresh areas for players to explore, collect, and develop.

### Collections

This release introduces one new Collection:

**Valdezarza (Standard)**

Both the Madrid Standard Pack and Madrid Deluxe Pack have a **10% overall chance of containing a Collection property from Madrid**. That property may come from any qualifying Madrid Collection and is not necessarily from the new Valdezarza Collection.

Collection properties are not guaranteed in every pack.

### Details

Both Madrid packs will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, August 21 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, August 22 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, August 28 at 9 AM PT

### Madrid Standard Pack

Includes a random property from Madrid with a guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX and a **10% overall chance of receiving a Collection property**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 250
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10%

### Madrid Deluxe Pack

Includes random properties from Madrid with a guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 57,000 UPX, a **10% overall chance of receiving a Collection property**, and the all-new **European Modular Apartments: Mantequeria Edition** structure.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the Madrid Deluxe Pack, making it a standout addition for players looking to bring more everyday character to their European modular builds.

**Price:** $20
**Quantity:** 350
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 57,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10%

The **European Modular Apartments: Mantequeria Edition** brings a traditional neighborhood provisions shop to Upland’s European streetscape, with a welcoming storefront on the ground floor and residential space above.

More importantly, the Mantequeria is another **middle structure** for the European modular lineup. Builders already have plenty of corner pieces, but fewer **middle structure**s to connect them. This addition makes it easier to extend street-facing designs, bridge corner buildings, and create larger modular developments that feel complete.

Like all Modular structures, this build is fully modular and can be combined with other European Modular Apartments to create larger, customized developments. This structure is region-locked to European cities.

### Looking Ahead

With **five new neighborhoods**, the new **Valdezarza Standard Collection**, and another much-needed **middle structure** joining the European modular lineup, this Madrid expansion gives players new territory to claim and more flexibility to create complete modular developments.
