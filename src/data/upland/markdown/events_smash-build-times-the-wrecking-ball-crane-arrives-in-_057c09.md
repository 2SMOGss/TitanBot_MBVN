### How Construction Vehicles Work

Construction vehicles are more than display pieces. Deploy an eligible vehicle on a property under construction and it contributes a **Sparklet boost** that helps reduce the build time.

Players can make those boosts even stronger by forming **Construction Crews**. A completed Crew is created by placing **three unique eligible construction vehicles** on the same active build. Each completed Crew adds a **+5% bonus** to the total Sparklet staked into that project before individual vehicle boosts are added. Multiple completed Crews stack, giving fleet owners even more ways to accelerate major developments.

In short: stake Sparklet, deploy your construction vehicles, complete Crews, and build faster. [Read the full details here](https://www.upland.me/events/construction-boost-is-live-start-speeding-up-your-builds).
