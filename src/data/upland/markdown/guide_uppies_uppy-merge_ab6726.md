# Level Up Your Uppies : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppy-merge
Upgrade and evolve your Uppies to increase their levels and rarity. Uppie Evolution isn’t just about upgrading a collectible, it’s about shaping the community that lives on your properties and in your neighborhoods. Every Uppie you discover, trade, and evolve contributes to the lore, culture, and personality of Upland of the metaverse. Each Uppie has unique traits, and the more traits it has, the higher its level. [Levels](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels) range from 1 to 10, with Iconic Uppies reaching a Special Star Level, which follows level 10. [Learn how to befriend and mint Uppies here.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/befriending-uppies)

An Uppie’s level reflects a combination of how much creative freedom the simulation applied in generating it, along with how far it visually deviates from the neutral base form. In addition to this, Uppies that reach certain levels unlock [additional utility.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility)

Its important to note that traits and levels don’t always align one-to-one. A Level 3 Uppie might appear more restrained than a Level 2 in some ways, but carries deeper logic in how it was constructed. The result is a spectrum of personality, expression, and complexity.

Some Uppies will feel simple. Others will be richly layered, packed with style and story. And over time, what begins as a casual collection becomes something far more personal: your strategy, your journey, your cultural imprint inside Upland.

Uppie Evolution allows you to combine two Uppies of the same level to create a brand-new, higher-level Uppie. The more they evolve, the closer you get to unlocking the rarest and most unique Uppies in Upland. When you evolve your Uppies, the original ones will be permanently burned. They’re gone forever, replaced by the new evolved Uppie. You must think if you want to keep that specific Uppie or burn it for a chance at something more rare and unique. In order to evolve the Uppie, you will have to spend a certain amount of Sparklet - [learn more in this video](https://youtu.be/ZhSCpoC_fx4). You also have the opportunity to list them for sale in [Variety Legit Shop Metaventures](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/variety-legits-shop).

The following is a breakdown of how many of the most common level 1 Uppies you require to merge in total to receive a higher level Uppie:
- 2 Level 1 Uppies = Level 2
- 4 Level 1 Uppies = Level 3
- 8 Level 1 Uppies = Level 4
- 16 Level 1 Uppies = Level 5
- 32 Level 1 Uppies = Level 6
- 64 Level 1 Uppies = Level 7
- 128 Level 1 Uppies = Level 8
- 256 Level 1 Uppies = Level 9
- 512 Level 1 Uppies = Level 10
- 1024 Level 1 Uppies = Iconic Parody Uppie (IPU) - Star Level

To view all the Uppies, check out the Uppiepedia - found in the game menu. [Learn more about the Uppiepedia here.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppiepedia)
