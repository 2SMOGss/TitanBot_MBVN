# Condiments, Coffins, and Ghostly Stops Come to Upland

October 28, 2026

This week’s four new **Service Structures** bring one playful food stop and three delightfully haunted businesses to the neighborhood. **Uppies** can pick a favorite condiment, trade spooky stories, gather for a ghost tour, and give the block a little more macabre personality. Invite a friend back for the haunted lineup, or bring someone new along to discover the neighborhoods the community is building.

These **Service Structures** will be available from **Wednesday, October 28 at 9 AM PT through Wednesday, December 9 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes More Than Three Bottles Condiment Kiosk, Crooked Coffin Workshop, Haunted Ghost Tour House, and Grim Hollow Tombstone Makers for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, a **$6 saving** compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, October 28 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, October 29 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### More Than Three Bottles Condiment Kiosk

You asked for more than three bottles. We took that personally. More Than Three Bottles Condiment Kiosk brings a playful stop to food streets and market corners, where **Uppies** can debate the perfect finishing touch for every neighborhood snack. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### Crooked Coffin Workshop

Crooked Coffin Workshop adds a darkly funny edge to old-town streets and haunted districts. It is the kind of neighborhood business where **Uppies** can trade ghost stories and give the block a wonderfully spooky sense of humor. *(Essential, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $5.00

### Haunted Ghost Tour House

Haunted Ghost Tour House gives curious **Uppies** a natural meeting point before a night of neighborhood exploring. Place it near historic streets or mysterious corners when a district needs a little more suspense. *(Entertainment, 5 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 19,800
**Price:** $7.00

### Grim Hollow Tombstone Makers

Grim Hollow Tombstone Makers brings a macabre finishing touch to graveyard-inspired builds and gloomy streets. It gives **Uppies** one more memorable stop in a neighborhood where every block feels ready to tell a ghost story. *(Essential, 7 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 24,300
**Price:** $10.00

Whether **Uppies** are picking a favorite condiment, trading spooky stories, gathering for a ghost tour, or putting the final macabre touch on a haunted district, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four more ways to shape neighborhoods with personality. Collectors who want all four can grab the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share the lineup with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.
