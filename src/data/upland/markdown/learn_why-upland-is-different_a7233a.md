# Why Upland Is Different

Most city-builder games give you a private map only, which resets (all the time). Upland gives you the real world, one shared map, and a memory.

**One shared map, real places.** Every Architect (you, the player) acts on the same global map tied to real-world addresses. Your property becomes your block. Together with other Architects, you shape your neighborhood.

**A real player-driven economy.** Players own and trade properties, structures, vehicles, plants, animals, and Uppies peer-to-peer. Supply, demand, and strategy set every price — not an algorithm.

**Permanence.** Live since 2019. No seasonal wipes. The first San Francisco properties from 2019 still belong to the players who minted and haven't sold them.

**Sell in-game items.** Eligible digital assets can be listed on Upland's marketplace for USD via Thunes, our regulated payment partner. After identity verification, you can withdraw USD proceeds to PayPal. Live since 2020. Regional availability, fees, and tax obligations apply.

**Accessible depth.** Start fast on mobile or web. Go deeper by completing collections, property trading, shops, city planning, finding troves, befriending Uppies, and live events.

**A positive, helpful community.** Many years of accumulated assets, social bonds, player-run businesses, and shared history. AI can't clone this.
