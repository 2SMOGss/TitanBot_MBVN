# Football Legits : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/upland-legits-main/football-legits
You can now collect digital collectible NFTs of your favorite football (soccer) players. For Uplanders familiar with the NFLPA Legits, these football (soccer) Legits will function in a similar manner, with some minor changes. We have a [beginners Legits quiz](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/quiz-home/legits-quiz), and we recommend taking the NFLPA quiz, as many of the questions are relevant for football legits as well. You can watch all the Upland Guide youtube videos related to football (soccer) Legits below.

These NFT Legits can be minted at the team's home stadium. You can mint [Essentials](https://upland-guide.com/upland-legits-main/football-legits/types-of-football-soccer-legits/football-soccer-essential)and [Mementos](https://upland-guide.com/upland-legits-main/football-legits/types-of-football-soccer-legits/minting-mementos) - which are 1 of 1 NFTs of your favorite players and a specific game's performance. In the future, you will even be able to get these digital collectibles autographed. [Learn more about the different types of Legits here.](https://upland-guide.com/upland-legits-main/football-legits/types-of-football-soccer-legits)
