# Los Angeles Evolution Rolls In with Zoomies Skate Shop

August 07, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Los Angeles** this week with a new way to build in one of Upland’s most iconic cities. **Los Angeles is already open**, so this release focuses on adding more property opportunities and the all-new **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Zoomies Skate Shop Edition**.

This week’s **Los Angeles Evolution Pack** gives players another way to enter or expand in the city without opening any new neighborhoods or introducing new Collections. With **250 Standard Packs** and **350 Deluxe Bundles** available, players can grow their Los Angeles footprint while the Deluxe Bundle brings a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property** and a special new **middle structure** for modular apartment builds.

### Neighborhoods

No new neighborhoods are opening with this release. **Los Angeles is already open**, and all properties included in these packs come from the city’s existing footprint.

### Collections

No new Collections are being introduced with this release. The **Los Angeles Deluxe Bundle** has a **10% chance** of containing a Collection property from the city’s existing Collections.

Collection properties are not guaranteed in every Deluxe Bundle. The **Los Angeles** Standard Pack does not include a chance to receive a Collection property.

### Details

Both **Los Angeles** offerings will be available in **limited quantities** in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, August 7 at 9 AM PT

**Sale Starts:** Saturday August 8 at 9 AM PT

**Sale Ends:** Friday, August 14 at 9 AM PT

### Los Angeles Standard Pack

Includes one random property from **Los Angeles** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 15,000 UPX**. This pack does not include a chance to receive a Collection property.

**Price:** 15,000 UPX

**Quantity:** 250

**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 15,000 UPX

**Collection Property Chance:** None

### Los Angeles Deluxe Bundle

Includes one random property from **Los Angeles** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 50,000 UPX**, a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property**, and the all-new **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Zoomies Skate Shop Edition** structure.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the **Los Angeles Deluxe Bundle**, making it a standout addition for players looking to build larger, more complete modular apartment complexes.

**Price:** $20

**Quantity:** 350

**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 50,000 UPX

**Collection Property Chance:** 10%

The **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Zoomies Skate Shop Edition** brings skate-shop energy to the streets of **Los Angeles**, with a lively storefront on the ground floor and residential space above.

What makes this release **especially useful** is its place within the modular apartment system. While many recent releases have focused on corner structures, Zoomies is a **middle structure**, giving builders an important new piece for filling out longer street-facing designs and creating more complete modular complexes.

Like all Modular structures, this build is **fully modular**, making it ideal for combining corner and middle pieces into larger, customized builds.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about more than opening new neighborhoods. It is also about **building more depth into cities that are already open**. With more **Los Angeles** properties entering circulation, a Collection chance in the Deluxe Bundle, and a much-needed **middle structure** joining the modular lineup, this release gives builders more freedom to turn individual pieces into complete complexes.

For players ready to expand in **Los Angeles** and take their modular builds further, Zoomies is ready to roll.
