# Seed and Plant Database : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/seed-legits/plant-database
Here you can find a complete database of all Upland Plants. This can help you when planning a garden, and understanding some of the specific use cases of each of the different plants. Before starting to garden, we highly recommend reviewing the [Upland Guide Ultimate Guide to Gardening](https://uplandguide.substack.com/p/the-ultimate-guide-to-gardening-in-the-metaverse). The article covers the 5 step process to excelling at gardening. Even if you don't intend on planting hundreds of plants, the guide will give you a good understanding of how the plant economy operates in Upland.

Here are a number of other important plant related topics to familiarize yourself with before starting your plant journey:

- Plan your garden - use the [Upland Guide seed calcultor](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/seed-legits/plant-database/calculator)

- Looking to generate or sell seeds? Consider using [Dale's Hedge Fund Nursery](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/dales-metaventures/dales-hedge-fund)in Manhattan.

- Learn about [STEM](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem), [life](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/life), [seed legits](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/seed-legits), and [plants](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants).

- [Plants take time to grow, you can spend UPX to accelerate the process.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/seasonal-bloom-cycle)

- Learn when and how to [feed your plants](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/health-and-wellbeing/feeding).

- [Feed your plants automatically with Uppies](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility)
