# Upland Seasons : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons
The Upland metaverse revolves around 6 seasons, each lasting 6 weeks. During each season there are several different scheduled sales and activities to partake in, giving you opportunities to purchase and win exclusive items like [badges](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity/upland-badges), UPX, [Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet), [Lore Legits](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/lore) and more. The following is a list of the different seasons and dates in Upland:

*

Below is a list of different activities and competitions to partake in during each season. The very first season in Upland was the 2024 Frost season. Each season has activities that shine a spotlight on a specific city. To learn more about the active season, visit the [gamified earnings page.](https://upland-guide.com/economy/gamified-upx-earnings)










* 
** 

Gamified UPX Earnings 



During each season you have an opportunity to influence the amount of yield you earn for the following season.  UPX earnings are set at a base of 4.9% but you have the ability to increase that to 15.19% by completing a number of tasks.  Learn how to ensure you can [maximize your earnings here.](https://upland-guide.com/economy/gamified-upx-earnings)

(Don't forget to collect your earnings before the start of a new season, [learn why here).](https://upland-guide.com/economy/gamified-upx-earnings/collecting-upx-earnings)





** 
** 

Builders Score Contest 



Players compete in tasks related to building with Sparklet in order to increase their building score throughout each season.  [Learn more about this contest here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/builder-score)





** 
** 

Collection Score Contest 



Players compete to collect [Block Explorers](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers), [City Passes](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/new-city-release-process/city-pass), [Season Passes](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/season-pass), and [Badges](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity/upland-badges) released during the current season.  At the end of each season, prizes are awarded to the top players.

[Learn more about collection scores and how they are calculated here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/collectors-score)





** 
** 

Property Decor Contest 



Decorate your property according to the season theme for your chance to show off your design skills and stand out as a model home designer in the metaverse.  [Learn more here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/property-decor-contest)





** 
** 

Neighborhood Collection Challenge 



Each season, neighborhoods compete in challenges to spawn a new neighborhood collection.

All neighborhoods who have previously spawned this new collection are competing to further increase their collection earning boost and level up their connection to even an **ultra rare **collection!

[Learn more about these competitions here.](https://upland-guide.com/community/neighborhood-ratings)





** 
** 

Cosmetic Items and Season Passes 



Season passes are for players who appreciate style and cosmetics. Burning a pass during rewards you with a badge as well as a variety of cosmetic items that give you freedom to tailor your in-game identity. Whether you favor a discreet appearance or wish to make a bold impression, these cosmetics offer a platform to exhibit your distinct style.  [Learn more about these passes and the cosmetic items here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/season-pass)





** 
** 

Decorate your home with structure ornaments 



You can decorate your home with any structure ornament year round, but by decorating it with an ornament corresponding to the current season you are participating in special seasonal challenges where you can win prizes

- [seasonal ornament challenge](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/season-ornament-challenge)

- [Seasonal builders score challenge](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/builder-score)

[Learn more about structure ornaments here.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornaments)

[Learn how to decorate your home here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MR6kMMg_kEY).





** 
** 

Go Kart Challenge 



Compete against a leaderboard with go kart challenges that will help you improve your racing abilities on a level playing field.

This challenge changes during each season, focusing on a different track and skill set.

[Learn more here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/go-kart-challenge)





** 
** 

Upland Racing Series 



Race for stakes using vehicles or karts, climb the leaderboards, and win prizes.  [Learn all the details, and how to win here.](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/racing-series)

Learn about [racing for stakes](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/racing-series) and how you can win UPX through car racing.





** 
** 

Sparklet Weekend 



Weekends throughout the season (starting from Friday 9AM PT - Monday 9AM PT) players will have the ability to level up their status to receive bonus sparklet.

There is also a Sparklet sale within Upland as an alternative to purchasing Sparklet on exchanges.

[Learn more about sparklet weekend here.](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend)

Ensure you wait for an official announcement from Upland to ensure Sparklet weekend in fact is occurring.
