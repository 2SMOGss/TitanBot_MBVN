# Storybook Harvest Arrives in Upland

September 23, 2026

**Harvest Season starts October 1**, and we’re getting ready with an early reveal of **Storybook Harvest**, the theme bringing old New England charm, cozy neighborhood shops, and a little Salem-inspired mystery to Upland. The first look begins with this week’s four new **Service Structures**, giving players a head start on preparing their neighborhoods for the season. From fresh pizza and a curious magic shop to a welcoming bakery and a candle store, this lineup creates new places where **Uppies** can eat, shop, and enjoy the autumn atmosphere.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, September 23 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Each structure is expected to remain in the store for roughly four weeks. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes all four of this week’s new structures for **$13**. That is approximately **24% off regular pricing**, making it the best way to add the entire release to one collection.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for **24 hours only**, from **Wednesday, September 23 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, September 24 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Crispy Crust Pizza (Kiosk)

Fresh slices, quick service, and a warm stop on a crisp autumn day. The Crispy Crust Pizza adds an easy food destination to town squares, shopping streets, and busy neighborhood corners where **Uppies** can grab a bite before their next stop. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### The Curious Cauldron

A little mystery, a little magic, and a reason to peek inside. The Curious Cauldron brings a Salem-inspired magic shop to village centers, old-fashioned shopping streets, and storybook neighborhoods where **Uppies** can explore something out of the ordinary. *(Entertainment, 2 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $5.00

### The Harvest Oven

Bakery charm and a cozy reason to make one more stop. The Harvest Oven gives village squares, market districts, and autumn shopping streets a welcoming neighborhood bakery where **Uppies** can pick up a treat along the way. *(Essentials, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 10,350
**Price:** $5.00

### The Flickering Wick

A little candlelight goes a long way. The Flickering Wick brings a cozy candle store to old-fashioned shopping districts, winding village streets, and storybook town centers where **Uppies** can find a little warmth for the season. *(Essentials, 2 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 11,700
**Price:** $5.00

Whether players are serving pizza, adding a little magic, opening a bakery, or bringing candlelight to the shopping street, this week’s **Service Structures** introduce the warmth and whimsy of **Storybook Harvest** to neighborhoods across Upland. Collectors who want all four can save with the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
