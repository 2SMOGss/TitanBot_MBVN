# Arlington Evolution Steps Up To The Plate With Sandlot Sporting Goods

October 23, 2026

City Evolution returns to Arlington with limited property access, an existing Limited Collection target, and a signature modular structure built around the city’s baseball spirit. **East Coast Modular Apartments: Sandlot Sporting Goods** brings a hometown sporting-goods theme to the modular lineup, giving Arlington builders a fresh way to add character to familiar city blocks.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release is focused on building deeper within Arlington’s existing footprint. Deluxe Packs carry a **30% pack-level chance** of containing a property from the existing **Central Limited Collection** and include the signature Sandlot Sporting Goods structure.

### Neighborhoods

No new Arlington neighborhoods are opening with this release. Properties in both pack tiers come from the city’s existing footprint, keeping the focus on adding depth to established blocks rather than unlocking new territory.

### Collections

No new Collection is being introduced. The **Central Limited Collection** is already published and remains the Collection target for the **Arlington Deluxe Pack’s 30% pack-level chance**.

The **Arlington Standard Pack** does not include a Collection-property chance in this release. A Collection property is not guaranteed in a Deluxe Pack.

### Details

Both **Arlington Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, October 23 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, October 24 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, October 30 at 9 AM PT

### Arlington Standard Pack

Includes one random property in Arlington with a **guaranteed minimum property UPX value of 10,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** Not included in this release

### Arlington Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in Arlington with a **guaranteed combined minimum property UPX value of 42,000 UPX**, a **30% pack-level chance** of receiving a property from the existing **Central Limited Collection**, and the **East Coast Modular Apartments: Sandlot Sporting Goods** structure.

For players building Arlington blocks with more personality, the Deluxe Pack brings together two properties, this release’s Collection-property opportunity, and a signature modular structure with unmistakable baseball character.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 42,000 UPX combined
**Collection Property Chance:** 30% per pack

**East Coast Modular Apartments: Sandlot Sporting Goods** brings ballpark spirit to the block. Picture **Uppies debating yesterday’s game, swapping baseball stories, and showing off a well-worn glove** as they make their way through the neighborhood. Its baseball sporting-goods theme gives Arlington builders a lively, lived-in piece of local character for their modular streetscapes.

Like other modular structures, it supports flexible building and long-term customization, giving players more ways to develop city blocks while preserving a strong sense of place and regional identity.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Arlington Deluxe Pack in this release**.

### Looking Ahead

This Arlington Evolution release is about **building depth in a city players already know**. With property access across the existing footprint, a chance at the **Central Limited Collection** in Deluxe Packs, and Sandlot Sporting Goods adding baseball spirit to the modular lineup, players have another way to shape Arlington blocks with local character.
