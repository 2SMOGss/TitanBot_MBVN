# Your Troves Are Going Places: Introducing Three New Service Structures

October 15, 2026

Three new structures are bringing a** whole new purpose **to your Troves**.** Introducing the **STEM Foundry**, **UPX Mint**, and **Sparklet Refinery**. Public Service Unit structures where players will, in the future, bring collected Troves and** exchange them through special recipes** to obtain **STEM, UPX, and Sparklet**, respectively. These structures arrive in the Upland Store today, with their Trove-powered functionality coming later.

Like other Service Structures, these can be used by any player to exchange Troves through recipes. However, **using your own****structure gives you a chance at better returns** than using someone else’s.

To celebrate their arrival, **each structure is just $9.99 USD** for the **first 24 hours** following this announcement. This is their **one and only discount**. After that, each will remain permanently available in the store for **$14.99 USD**.

### STEM Foundry

Give your Troves a new purpose at the STEM Foundry. In the future, players will be able to bring collected Troves here and **exchange them through special recipes to obtain STEM**. Your next Trove discovery could become an ingredient for your next recipe. *(Public Service, 27 Service Units)\*

### UPX Mint

Put your Troves toward UPX at the UPX Mint. In the future, players will be able to bring collected Troves here and exchange them through special recipes to obtain UPX, a new reason to keep collecting and see what your discoveries can become. *(Public Service, 28 Service Units)*

### Sparklet Refinery

Turn your Trove discoveries toward Sparklet at the Sparklet Refinery. In the future, players will be able to bring collected Troves here and exchange them through special recipes to obtain Sparklet, giving your collecting adventures another exciting goal. *(Public Service, 27 Service Units)*

### Your Troves Are Just Getting Started

Today marks the arrival of the structures; their Trove-powered functionality comes later. After Troves launches, we’ll reveal the special recipes, exchange amounts, and activation details as we get ready to fire up the **STEM Foundry**, **UPX Mint**, and **Sparklet Refinery**.

A foundry. A mint. A refinery. Three new places for your Troves to take you — and a new reason to get excited about what’s ahead. Pick up your structures during the 24-hour launch offer, and share the news with friends who are ready to build their next chapter in Upland alongside you.
