# Car Racing : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing
Car racing is an exciting 3D interactive experience in Upland. Upland Racing has entered the beta testing stage, but the future of racing is bright. Uplanders are able to race cars and [karts](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing/karts) to compete with other Uplanders to win more than bragging rights - [but UPX](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing/racing-for-stakes)! One of the most exciting opportunities that racing provides is that players are able to create [speedway metaventures](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/speedway-racetrack) where you can design your own racetracks and in the future create their own public or private racing leagues where they can compete with their friends.

[Race for stakes](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing/racing-for-stakes), increase your [MPS (Maximum Performance Score)](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing/maximum-performance-score)and compete in [Upland's seasonal racing series](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons/racing-series) to win UPX, [badges](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity/upland-badges)and other great prizes. If you are looking for a racetrack to race, consider one of the [Upland Guide's tracks.](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/dales-metaventures/speedways)

Racing will obviously require a car (or kart), and also a [block explorer](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers). Block explorers will function as the drivers of the cars. Each car and in the future, block explorer will have unique traits, thus creating near infinite different combinations of drivers, cars, and race tracks to make competitions more interesting. Don't forget to download the Upland Racing app to your phone in order to race on mobile.
