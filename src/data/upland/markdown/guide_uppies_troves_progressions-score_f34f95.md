# Progression Score : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/progressions-score
The introduction of progression score is a new way to measure your Upland journey. A [player's status](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status) only factors in a player's net worth which includes properties as well as UPX. The progression score enables Upland to further gamify other aspects of the platform.

You can think of the Progression System as your Upland level: a numerical representation of your activity, achievements, and advancement throughout the game. Together, your status and progression level will provide a more complete picture of your Upland journey.

To advance your level, you will need to banking [Troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves), develop your property collections, complete progression objectives, continuing to increase your Upland status, and potentially more.

To reach certain progression milestones, players may need to satisfy a combination of requirements. An example of how to increase your level is tied to [banking troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/trove-banking) as well as other milestones as detailed in the following example.

It's not just prestige to show off your level and progress. At specific progression milestones, players will also receive rewards recognizing how far they have advanced. These rewards can be map assets, UPX bonuses, collectibles, customization items, and more.
