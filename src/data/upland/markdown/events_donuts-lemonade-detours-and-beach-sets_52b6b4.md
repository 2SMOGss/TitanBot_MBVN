# Donuts, Lemonade, Detours, and Beach Sets

August 12, 2026

**Sizzle Season** keeps rolling with four new **Service Structures** built for snack stops, cold drinks, neighborhood wayfinding, and beachside competition. From fresh donuts and lemonade runs to travel tips and sand-court rallies, this week’s lineup gives players four new ways to make their neighborhoods feel useful, active, and ready for summer.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, August 12 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Each structure is scheduled to remain available until **Wednesday, September 9 at 9 AM PT**, giving players roughly four weeks to add it to their collection. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

There is no **Service Structure Value Pack** this week, so all four structures are available individually.

### Glazed & Confused Donuts

Fresh glaze, warm pastries, and a display case capable of turning any quiet corner into the neighborhood’s favorite morning stop. Glazed & Confused Donuts is a natural fit for boardwalks, shopping streets, commuter routes, and busy plazas where an **Uppie** can grab something sweet on the go. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 3,600

**Price:** $1.99

### Main Squeeze Lemonade

Ice-cold lemonade, bright citrus flavor, and the perfect excuse to slow down on a hot summer day. Main Squeeze Lemonade adds an easygoing refreshment stop to parks, festival grounds, beach routes, and family neighborhoods where every **Uppie** could use one more cup. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500

**Price:** $1.99

### Welcome Point Travel Center

Maps, local tips, and a friendly first stop for every new arrival. Welcome Point Travel Center gives downtown gateways, transit hubs, resort districts, and city entrances a helpful place where **Uppies** can get their bearings before exploring the neighborhood. *(Public Services, 2 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 6,750

**Price:** $4.99

### Set Point Beach Club

Sand underfoot, a net at center court, and enough competitive energy to keep the beach buzzing all afternoon. Set Point Beach Club brings a major recreation destination to waterfront neighborhoods, resort builds, park districts, and summer communities where **Uppies** can serve, dive, and battle for the next point. *(Essentials, 45 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 46,800

**Price:** $9.99

Whether players are serving donuts, pouring lemonade, welcoming travelers, or hosting beach volleyball matches, this week’s **Service Structures** bring more flavor, refreshment, guidance, and competition to **Sizzle Season**.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
