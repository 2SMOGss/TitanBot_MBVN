# Apply to be an Exhibitor at Genesis Week 2023!

April 13, 2023

Genesis Week 2023 is just around the corner! This year, we’re taking community involvement to the next level, and we want you to be a part of it! We're excited to announce that applications to be an exhibitor at Genesis Week are now open. As an exhibitor, you'll have your very own booth to showcase your Upland projects to the community, along with a host of other perks. This is the perfect opportunity for you to join us at Genesis Week 2023 and showcase all of your hard work to the Upland Community in person! 

‍

## Genesis Week Exhibitor Details 

 All exhibitor details are provided below to ensure you are well-prepared to become an exhibitor at Genesis Week.

### Exhibitor Application 

There are no strict requirements to meet, making it easy to apply. After submitting your application, you will receive a submission confirmation email acknowledging your application has been received. The Upland Team will carefully review your submission, and within 2 to 4 weeks you will be notified via email of your approval or denial. Exhibitor applications will close on **Sunday, April 23 at 11:59 PM PT.** 

‍

[Click here to apply to be an Exhibitor at Genesis Week 2023!](https://forms.clickup.com/10551844/f/a20h4-29240/WBUWJ9DDUKJIX15H2D)

### Exhibitor Costs

As an exhibitor at Genesis Week, there is a small fee associated. If your Exhibitor application is approved by the Upland Team, we will send you a private link via email to pay your exhibitor fees. The cost of being an exhibitor at Genesis Week varies depending on whether you have already purchased a Genesis Week ticket or not.

‍

The exhibitor fees are as follows:

**Exhibitor Price if you have not purchased a Genesis Week Ticket:** $200 (Comes with a Genesis Week 2023 General Admission Pass)

**Exhibitor Price if you have purchased a Genesis Week Ticket:** $100

### Exhibitor Perks

Now let’s dive into all the exciting perks of being an exhibitor at Genesis Week. Each exhibitor will receive each perk mentioned below.

‍

- Booth with a Six-foot table and cloth
- Custom 23.5”(W) x 79”(H) Retractable Banner for Booth
- Unique Listing on Genesis Week Website
- Officially a part of the Genesis Week Programming
- Highlighton Genesis Week Emails 
- Mention on Upland’s Socials 
- Press Release Mention ‍

**Important Note: **There are a **limited number** of available Exhibitor slots, carefully hand-selected by the Upland Team. If you are approved as an exhibitor, we will provide instructions on how to submit your design for a custom retractable banner. The deadline for submitting custom designs is **Monday, May 1 at 11:59 PM PT**.
