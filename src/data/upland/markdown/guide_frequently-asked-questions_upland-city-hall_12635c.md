# The Upland Bureau - and City Hall : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-city-hall
Fittingly, Upland's First City Hall is located in San Francisco - the first city in the Upland Metaverse. The City Hall serves a number of important function, and likely more in the future. The exact address of City Hall is 1 Dr. Carlton B Goodlet Pl., San Francisco California. All Upland cities will have their own city hall, which will be an important location for players to visit. The account that owns the city halls is 'uplandbureau'.

See below the things that can be done in City Hall:

- [Upload your custom block explorer concept](https://upland-guide.com/)

- [Upload your custom model concept](http://custom-model)

- [Metaverse Registration](https://upland-guide.com/)

### Custom Block Explorer

One of the benefits of reaching [Director status](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status) (1,000,000 UPX net worth) is the ability to have a custom 1 of 1 block explorer created just for you. Before travelling to City Hall, make sure you have your design, as well as your block explorer story.

The design can a quick sketch or even a written word explanation. Upland's custom block explorer story policy is detailed below.
[Click here to read the complete guidelines.](https://www.notion.so/uplandme/Content-Guidelines-ccd2db5954e140679ba624c5de480412)

### Custom Model

One of the benefits of reaching [Executive status (](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status)10,000,000 UPX net worth) is the ability to have a custom model created just for you. This 3D object can be placed on one of your Upland properties. Before travelling to City Hall, make sure you have your design, as well as your story.

### Metaverse Registration

City Hall will serve as the place to register for your metaventure. Its important to note that you must travel to the city hall in the city where you want to create the metaventure. [Read more about metaventures here.](https://upland-guide.com/directory/metaventures)
