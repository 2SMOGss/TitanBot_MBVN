# 2020 NFLPA Legits are Back

October 29, 2021

Now that the first batch of[2020 NFLPA Legits](https://www.upland.me/events/the-2020-legits-kickoff-sale) have been delivered, it’s time for them to return to the Upland Store! Registration for the next batch of 2020 NFLPA Legits will begin tomorrow, **Saturday, October 30th, at 8:00 AM PT.** The start of the sale will begin the following morning, **Sunday, October 31st, at 9:00 AM PT. **

**Bundles Overview**

To avoid confusion, packs will now be referred to as bundles, *as they are ****not**** tradable*. Each item within the bundle will be, however, tradable as soon as swapping is enabled. Please see below for the full breakdown of available bundles.

**How to Purchase 2020 Legits**

2020 NFLPA Legits will be sold directly in the Upland store, which is accessible by clicking on the “Get UPX” button at the top right of the screen. While the first batch of 2020 Legits was purchasable exclusively with USD, we are proud to announce that both this sale and future sales will be purchasable in UPX!

Players will have 24 hours to register prior to each sale of 2020 items. Once the sale begins, players will be placed into batches and randomly selected to mint their desired bundles. After all registered participants have had a chance to mint a pack, players will be able to purchase additional bundles after a brief 10 minute cooldown period. 

We aim to deliver Legit bundles within 3 business days after purchase; players will be notified upon delivery. Players will also be notified in the event of a delay.

**Future 2020 Legits Sales**

As we prepare for 2021 NFLPA Legits Primary Markets, expect to see 2020 Legits return to the Upland Store on a weekly basis through the remainder of the year. 2020 Legits sales will recur until all items from the 2020 season have been sold or until December 31st. 

**An Update on NFT Swaps**

NFT Swapping is actively in development and should be ready for deployment in the near future. Once swapping is enabled, players will be able to exchange any NFT for NFT (excluding properties) inside of the Upland metaverse. Expect to see an NFLPA Legits trading activity shortly following the launch of Swaps.
