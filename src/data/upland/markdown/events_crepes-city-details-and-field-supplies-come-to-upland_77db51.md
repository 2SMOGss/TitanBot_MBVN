# Crepes, City Details, and Field Supplies Come to Upland

November 18, 2026

This week’s four new **Service Structures** bring a sweet street stop, two familiar city fixtures, and a countryside storefront to neighborhood builds. **Uppies** can meet at a colorful crepe kiosk, add a recognizable detail to a shopping corner, round out a busy public plaza, or give a rural road a hardworking supply-store anchor. Invite a friend back to explore the lineup, or bring someone new along to see what the community is building.

These **Service Structures** will be available from **Wednesday, November 18 at 9 AM PT through Wednesday, December 30 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes The Crepe Corner, QuickCash ATM, City Public Restrooms, and Field & Supply for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**!

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, November 18 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, November 19 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Crepe Corner

The Crepe Corner gives market lanes and festival blocks a cheerful focal point, where **Uppies** can meet by the colorful kiosk before wandering through the rest of the neighborhood. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### QuickCash ATM

QuickCash ATM adds a familiar city detail to shopping streets and transit corners, giving **Uppies** a recognizable meeting spot that helps a busy block feel complete. *(Essential, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 5,850
**Price:** $5.00

### City Public Restrooms

City Public Restrooms bring an everyday civic detail to parks, plazas, and event routes, giving **Uppies** another believable landmark around which to shape a lively public space. *(Public Service, 2 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 10,350
**Price:** $7.00

### Field & Supply

Field & Supply adds rustic storefront character to rural blocks and market roads, where **Uppies** can gather around a hardworking countryside build that gives the neighborhood a strong sense of place. *(Essential, 3 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,050
**Price:** $10.00

Whether **Uppies** are meeting at a crepe kiosk, adding a familiar city detail, rounding out a public plaza, or giving a country road a supply-store anchor, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four more ways to shape neighborhoods with personality.
