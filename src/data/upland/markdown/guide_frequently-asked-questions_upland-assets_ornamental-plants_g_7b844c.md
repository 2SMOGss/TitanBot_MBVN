# Grass and Sod : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/grass-and-sod
The grass is always greener in Upland! Grass and sod are the most basic seeds in Upland that should be accessible to all at the lowest possible cost. This will encourage players to start decorating and adding flare to their properties. In order to have these [seeds](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/seed-legits)as accessible as possible, grass and sod are the only types of [ornamental plant](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants) that are not generated as seeds through [nurseries](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/nursery). They are purchased directly from the [Upland store](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/upland-upx-store).

Players can purchase grass seed bundles for UPX, or Sod for USD. Below is an image showing the sizes of the different items, and a table detailing the costs.
