# Troves Are Coming. Get Your Neighborhood Ready.

October 08, 2026

**In the next 2 weeks, Troves are going live in Upland!**

Tell your neighbors. **Bring back the friends** who have been away.** Invite someone new** to build alongside you.** The next era of Upland is about to begin**, and now is the time to get ready together!

You**r properties**, your **plants**, your **structures**, and the **neighborhoods** you have helped shape are at the heart of what comes next. Troves will bring **new reasons to explore**, **collect, build**, and keep coming back. And it starts with giving everyone time to get out there and play.

### Collect First. New Recipes Next.

**Troves, Trove Banking, and Progression will launch together within the next 2 weeks.** All Troves available to collect through gameplay will go live first, giving you the opportunity to start collecting and build up your inventory.

**The full set of recipes will not be available at launch.** Instead, we will unlock new recipes in batches at a regular cadence over the **following weeks**, continuing until the full set is released.

That rollout is **intentional**. We want you to have time to** learn how Troves work**, discover where to collect them, and build up your collection through gameplay **before new recipes start arriving**. When a batch drops, you will have time to explore those recipes, understand what they need, and decide what you want to work toward next. Then another batch will open up **more possibilities**.

Time to **collect**. Time to **learn**. More to **discover **with each drop.

The Troves available at launch are **just a portion** of what is coming. As new recipes roll out over the following weeks, **over 500 Troves** will become available to discover and collect.

### Start Building Now

You do not have to wait for the launch to prepare. Start with your neighborhood and the properties you want to develop.

**Get to know your neighborhood.** Explore what is already there. Find the Service Structures, look at the residential development, and** connect with the players** building around you. Talk about what you would like to add together.

**Plant plants and trees.** Bring more Life to your properties and keep up with plant care. If you have been meaning to fill out a garden or bring a quiet property to life, now is a great time to get started.

**Build Service Structures and residential structures.** Think about the mix of services and homes in your neighborhood, and where your next build could make a difference. Keep developing the places you want to spend time in as Troves and recipes roll out.

You do not need to do everything at once. Pick a property. Start a build. Plant something. Get a conversation going with your neighbors. **The preparation starts with players building together.**

### Bring Your Friends Into the Next Era

Have a friend who has not opened Upland in a while? Send them this announcement. Show them what you have been building and invite them back to prepare with you.

Know someone who has never played? Introduce them to your neighborhood. **Share your **[personal referral link](https://play.upland.me/referral-program) to invite them to Upland. Give them a place to start and people to explore with as Troves arrive.

This is the beginning of the next era of Upland, and the community will help shape it, one property, one neighborhood, and one new discovery at a time.

**Troves are coming. Gather your friends. Prepare. Build. Go.**
