# Cape Town Evolution Brews Up Six New Neighborhoods

November 06, 2026

City Evolution returns to Cape Town with **six new neighborhoods**, fresh property access, and a signature modular structure shaped by the city’s cafe rhythm. **Cape Town Modular: Cafe Edition** brings the easy energy of a neighborhood coffee stop to the modular lineup, giving Cape Town builders a warm way to add more everyday character to their city blocks.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release opens new property access across **Thornton, Century City, Windermere, Townsend Estate, Sanddrift, and Marconi Beam**. Both pack types carry a pack-level chance of containing a property from the existing **Woodstock Standard Collection**, while Deluxe Packs include the signature Cafe Edition structure.

### Neighborhoods

Six new Cape Town neighborhoods are opening in this release:

- Thornton
- Century City
- Windermere
- Townsend Estate
- Sanddrift
- Marconi Beam Together, these neighborhoods expand Cape Town’s playable footprint and give players more places to claim property, grow their portfolios, and shape the city’s next chapter.

### Collections

No new Collection is being introduced. The **Woodstock Standard Collection** is already published and remains the Collection target for this release.

The **Cape Town Standard Pack has a 10% pack-level chance** of containing a property from the existing **Woodstock Standard Collection**. The **Cape Town Deluxe Pack has a 17% pack-level chance**. A Collection property is not guaranteed, and these are pack-level odds rather than independent rolls for each property.

### Details

Both **Cape Town Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, November 6 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, November 7 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, November 13 at 9 AM PT

### Cape Town Standard Pack

Includes one random property in Cape Town with a **guaranteed minimum property UPX value of 10,000 UPX** and a **10% pack-level chance** of receiving a property from the existing **Woodstock Standard Collection**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10% per pack

### Cape Town Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in Cape Town with a **guaranteed combined minimum property UPX value of 46,000 UPX**, a **17% pack-level chance** of receiving a property from the existing **Woodstock Standard Collection**, and the **Cape Town Modular: Cafe Edition** structure.

For players opening up new Cape Town blocks, the Deluxe Pack brings together two properties, this release’s stronger Collection-property chance, and a signature modular structure with the easygoing energy of neighborhood cafe life.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 46,000 UPX combined
**Collection Property Chance:** 17% per pack

**Cape Town Modular: Cafe Edition** brings the gentle buzz of a neighborhood morning to the block. Picture **Uppies catching up over coffee, lingering over a favorite conversation, and turning a quick pause into part of the day’s rhythm** as Cape Town hums around them. Its cafe character gives builders a warm, lived-in modular piece that makes a street feel more personal.

Like other modular structures, it supports flexible building and long-term customization, giving players more ways to develop city blocks while preserving a strong sense of place and regional identity.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Cape Town Deluxe Pack in this release**.

### Looking Ahead

This Cape Town Evolution release is about **opening more of the city while building on a Collection players already know**. With **six new neighborhoods**, pack-level chances at the **Woodstock Standard Collection**, and Cafe Edition bringing the rhythm of Uppie cafe life to the modular lineup, players have more ways to shape their place in Cape Town.
