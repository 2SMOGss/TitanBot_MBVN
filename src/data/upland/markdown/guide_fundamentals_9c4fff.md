# Fundamentals : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals
If you are have just discovered Upland and are getting started, know that there is a learning curve to the game. At first, the game is intriguing as it offers endless possibilities to [play](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment), [earn](https://upland-guide.com/economy), and [connect](https://upland-guide.com/community). Before that you do need to understand the fundamentals of the game and how to play. The Upland Guide [YouTube playlist for new players](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3zT4OlFr_bkCQcfK0nvKxPVEknHskmen) and the [top 10 mistakes to avoid as a new player](https://upland-guide.com/top-10-mistakes-made-in-upland-metaverse) are the perfect places to start.

The Upland Guide has all the information you need to know starting with our [FAQ](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions)page. Our menu at the top of the page will help you navigate the important aspects of the Metaverse. We also dive into general strategies and detail all the [different ways you can earn](https://upland-guide.com/earning-in-upland) in Upland. Developing a [beginner strategy](https://upland-guide.com/beginner-strategy), [becoming an Uplander](https://upland-guide.com/becoming-an-uplander) and additional strategies to progress to several [statuses](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status)- [pro](https://upland-guide.com/i-am-an-uplander-now-what)and [director](https://upland-guide.com/upland-metaverse-strategy/becoming-a-director) are also important milestones you may strive to acheive. We teach you about [real estate](https://upland-guide.com/real-estate) in the Metaverse, how to have a [minting strategy for new city releases](https://upland-guide.com/new-city-release-strategy), how to begin construction with [spark](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/spark) and creating life with [STEM](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem).

If you happen to be a sports fan - NFL, soccer, racing, or e-sports, check out [Upland's partners](https://upland-guide.com/about/partners) as a great way to connect to other fans and collect Legits and other collectibles

If you think you are ready to dive into the Metaverse, [test your knowledge by taking our quizzes](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/quiz-home) to make sure you have the basic understanding of the fundamentals before starting. It can prevent you from making mistakes.

Whether it be [cars](https://upland-guide.com/transportation/cars), [Legits](https://upland-guide.com/upland-legits-main), [treasure hunting](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/how-to-treasure-hunt), or other future features, the Upland Guide will be the first to provide you with the important information and tips on how to include this in your Metaverse life and strategy.
