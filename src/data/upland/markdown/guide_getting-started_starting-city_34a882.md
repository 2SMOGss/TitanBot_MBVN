# Entering the Metaverse : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/getting-started/starting-city
When you join the Metaverse, you will need to choose your starting city. Ideally, your first task is to mint a property. Minting a property refers to being the first person to purchase the property and creating it on the block chain. As mentioned, your starting city can differ based on your [initial strategy](https://upland-guide.com/beginner-strategy) and the [amount of money](https://upland-guide.com/referral-bonus) you are putting into the game. At any given time, the answer could change. Upland announcements, new in game features, secondary market prices are all dynamic and can have influence on the question *"What city should I start in?"* This page may not have the answer to where you should start, but hopefully it will help you narrow down your choices of where **not** to start. Watch the video below for some additional insight on helping you to define your starting strategy or continue below for the list of cities. Learn about how to mint your first property as part of our [free introductory missions course](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/missions/beginner-course)that will help you earn thousands of UPX on your way to Uplander status.

If you are looking to reach Uplander status by completing missions, [this video](https://youtu.be/yPxKSMZ6CxE?si=sWR5rkpeLhyoP_FC) is a complete guide on how to do this in the most efficient way possible.

Below are the cities in order of their release dates, and their statuses. Feel free to send us a message using the blue button in the bottom right corner with any questions you might have about where is the best place to start right now. Alternatively, feel free to message YK2012 using the in game chat.

If you are joining Upland right before the release of a new city, you should know that new city releases are the best opportunity for you to [grow your net worth](https://upland-guide.com/new-city-release). Upland typically gives up to one weeks notice prior to releasing a new city. There are many announcements leading up to the release, and [this page will help to explain](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/new-city-release-process) that in more details so you can be better prepared.

* The * in the table below indicates that the city is not fully released, and it has yet to reach its final treasure hunting tier. A city that has not been fully released will have properties sold as part of [city evolutions](https://upland-guide.com/getting-started/starting-city/city-evolutions) on a regular basis.
