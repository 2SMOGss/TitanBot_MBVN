# Treats, Tailoring, and Tavern Tales Come to Upland

October 21, 2026

**Storybook Harvest** is serving up a fresh mix of neighborhood flavor. This week’s four new **Service Structures** give **Uppies** a pretzel stop, a tailor shop, a welcoming tavern, and a candy shop to discover across the streets players are building. Invite a friend back to explore the new lineup, or introduce someone new to the community taking shape.

These **Service Structures** will be available from **Wednesday, October 21 at 9 AM PT through Wednesday, December 2 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes The Twisted Pretzel, Stitch & Spell Tailors, The Tipsy Pilgrim Tavern, and Sugar & Spell Candy Shop for **$18**. That is a **$6 saving** compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, October 21 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, October 22 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Twisted Pretzel

The Twisted Pretzel brings an easy snack stop to busy corners, market streets, and neighborhood gathering spots. Give hungry **Uppies** a place to pause between seasonal adventures and add a little savory charm to the route. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### Stitch & Spell Tailors

Stitch & Spell Tailors gives every neighborhood a stylish place for a little seasonal polish. It fits naturally along shopping streets and town centers where **Uppies** can get ready for gatherings, special occasions, or an ordinary day with extra flair. *(Essential, 2 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $5.00

### The Tipsy Pilgrim Tavern

The Tipsy Pilgrim Tavern adds a warm social anchor to the neighborhood. Place it near busy streets or tuck it into a cozy district where **Uppies** can meet, swap stories, and unwind after exploring. *(Entertainment, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $7.00

### Sugar & Spell Candy Shop

Sugar & Spell Candy Shop brings playful treat-stop energy to shopping districts and festive streets. It gives **Uppies** another reason to wander through the neighborhood, pick a favorite sweet, and keep the **Storybook Harvest** fun going. *(Essential, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,950
**Price:** $10.00

Whether **Uppies** are grabbing a pretzel, finding a fresh look, sharing tavern stories, or choosing a sweet treat, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four more ways to shape a neighborhood with personality. Collectors who want the full lineup can grab the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share the lineup with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
