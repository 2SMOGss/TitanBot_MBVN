# Harvest 2026 Championship Racing: Six Races. Every Point Counts.

October 02, 2026

Six weeks of racing. Twelve spots on the road to Genesis Week. One Harvest champion crowned on points.

**Harvest 2026 Championship Racing** is bringing a new reason to keep your foot on the gas. This season, we’re replacing the standalone Season Championship Final with a **sixth points-scoring race**, giving racers another full week to climb the standings, close the gap, and fight for a place in the **top 12**.

The **Harvest 2026 Championship Racing Pass is live in the Upland Store now** and remains available until **Wednesday, October 7 at 9 AM PT**. Get your pass, get your setup ready, and make every week count.

### Six Races to Make Your Move

Your season will no longer come down to a separate championship final. Instead, **the racer with the most season points after all six weeks will be crowned the Harvest champion**.

That means Week 6 is another opportunity to earn points, not a race reserved for racers who have already qualified. A rough opening week does not end your run. Keep racing, keep improving, and use every opportunity to move up the leaderboard.

There is more than a title on the line, too. **The top 12 racers at the end of Harvest will qualify for the Harvest semifinal during Genesis Week 2027**, held on the **Paris Monument track**. Whether you are chasing first place or fighting your way into the **top 12**, you have six weeks to make your move.

**There is no standalone Season Championship Final during Harvest.** The weekly competition format stays the same, including its qualifying rounds, semifinal heats, and final heat. What changes is how the season champion is decided and when the season’s **top 12** move on.

### The Road to Genesis Week

The season earns you your place. **Genesis Week is where you race for the Grand Final.**

During Genesis Week, racers will compete in **each seasonal semifinal they have qualified for**. Qualifying across multiple seasons gives you more opportunities to race and accumulate points during Genesis Week.

There will be **six races in total during Genesis Week, including the Grand Final**. Points earned in the Genesis Week semifinals will determine the **top six racers who advance to the Grand Final**.

For Harvest, the route is clear: **race all six weeks, finish in the top 12, and earn your place in the Paris semifinal at Genesis Week**. Then compete for the points that can put you on the final starting grid.

### Week 5 Is Bonus Points Week

**Week 5 remains Bonus Points Week**, giving racers another opportunity to close the gap, climb the standings, or defend their position.

The exact bonus point boost will be announced before Week 5 qualifying begins. And this time, the fight continues afterward: **Week 6 is another points-scoring week**, with the Harvest title and Genesis Week qualification decided only once the season’s racing is complete.

### Complete Racing Rules

That is everything you need to know about what is new, how to register, where to race, and what is at stake in Harvest 2026. The remainder of this announcement is the complete rules reference. Returning racers who have read the format changes above and already know the weekly Championship Racing rules can stop reading here.

**Season Format**

- The **Harvest 2026 Championship Racing Season** includes **six weeks of points-scoring racing**, with no standalone Season Championship Final.
- Each week features a public track on the official schedule.
- Qualifying runs from **Thursday at 9 AM PT through Monday at 9 AM PT**.
- Racers may run the qualifying track as many times as they want. Only their fastest time counts.
- The **top 24 fastest drivers** qualify for that week’s competition.
- Weekly points accumulate across all six weeks. **The racer with the most points at the end of the season is the Harvest champion.**
- The **top 12 racers** in the final Harvest standings qualify for the **Harvest semifinal at Genesis Week 2027 on Paris**.
- **Week 5 is Bonus Points Week.** The bonus point boost will be announced before Week 5 qualifying begins.
- Week 6 is open through the same weekly qualifying process as the other weeks; it is not restricted to the season’s **top 12**.
- At Genesis Week, racers compete in **each seasonal semifinal they have qualified for**. **Points earned in those semifinals determine the top six racers who advance to the Grand Final.** Genesis Week includes **five races total, including the Grand Final**. **Weekly Race Settings and Advancement**

- **Max Performance Score Cap:** All Championship races are limited to **MPS 260**.
- **Public Races:** All qualifying and competition races must be set to public.
- **Qualifying Phase:****Thursday at 9 AM PT through Monday at 9 AM PT** on the designated weekly track.
- **Eliminations:** The six slowest qualifiers are eliminated. The top 18 advance.
- **Weekly Semifinals:** The 18 advancing racers are split into three balanced heats based on qualifying times:
- **Heat 1:** Positions 1, 6, 7, 12, 13, and 18
- **Heat 2:** Positions 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, and 17
- **Heat 3:** Positions 3, 4, 9, 10, 15, and 16
- **Weekly Final Heat:** The top two finishers from each weekly semifinal advance to the final heat, for a total of six racers.
- **Weekly Final Rankings:** All 24 racers are ranked across the three race phases:
- Positions 1 through 6 are based on final heat results.
- Positions 7 through 18 are based on semifinal race times.
- Positions 19 through 24 are based on qualifying times. **Weekly Points System**

- Power-ups are included in a racer’s total **Max Performance Score**.
