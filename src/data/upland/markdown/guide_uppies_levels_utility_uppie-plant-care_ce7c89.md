# Uppie Plant Care : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility/uppie-plant-care
An integral part of plant care on your Upland properties is the [health and wellbeing](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/health-and-wellbeing) of the plant. [Stem](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem)is the resource used to feed your plants, and the wellbeing is maintained by petting and interacting with them. Not only does petting your plant increase its wellbeing, but it can also generate a [trove](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves).

There is an alternative to manually petting your [plant](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants). This can be automated by assigning an Uppie to your property. Adding a [level 3 Uppie](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility) will automatically pet all your plants on the property, and adding a level 5 Uppie will also collect troves from your plants.
