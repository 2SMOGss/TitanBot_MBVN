# Troves Are Almost Here. Get Your Uppies Ready!

September 24, 2026

**Troves are almost here, and it’s time to prepare!** This Uppie sale brings back two limited bundles, giving you another opportunity to stock up on UPX, grow your Uppie collection, and put eligible Uppies to work on your properties.

Whether you are getting your first plant caretaker or preparing a larger collection for the gameplay ahead, this is your chance to get ready before Troves arrive.

### Put Your Uppies to Work

Uppies are more than faces in your collection. They can already help care for the properties you are building, with more utility planned as Upland’s connected systems expand.

- **Level 1 and 2 Uppies will have a role, too.** When [Uppie Quests](https://www.upland.me/events/uppie-quests-are-coming-turn-troves-into-new-friends) arrive after Troves, you’ll be able to assign them to properties with Residents, making those properties eligible to spawn quests.
- **Care for your plants now.** Assign a **Level 3 or higher Uppie** to one of your properties to handle ongoing plant petting automatically. Less manual care means more time to explore, build, and play. **You still need to feed your plants.**
- **Prepare for plant Troves.** Plants are planned to produce Troves when the system launches. Keeping them healthy prepares your properties for that next step, and your assigned Uppies can help maintain their wellbeing.
- **Build toward more automation.** In later releases, **Level 5 and higher Uppies** are planned to automatically collect Troves from plants on their assigned properties. Employment at eligible service structures is also planned, allowing Uppies to help level up those structures and collect Troves there. Higher-level Uppies are planned to have greater daily collection capacity, separate from your personal collection limit. Plant-care automation is available now. Trove collection automation and service-structure employment are planned additions, and will not be available at the initial Troves launch.

You choose where to focus: care for a favorite property, build up your Uppie collection, or prepare for a more active role across your neighborhoods.
