### How the Harvest Kart Classic Works

The **Harvest Kart Classic** is a three-day racing challenge where every second counts. Each day, you’ll have **three official attempts** to set your fastest time. Your best valid time from those attempts counts toward your final score.

Each daily scoring window begins at **9 AM PT and runs until 9 AM PT the following day**, giving you a fresh set of three attempts every day.

At the end of the event, your **three daily best times are added together**, and the racer with the lowest combined time wins! To qualify for the leaderboard, you must record a valid time in all three daily scoring windows.

- **Entry Fee**: 100 UPX

- **MPS Setting**: Fixed at 80 MPS
- **AI Opponents**: ON

**Rules**

- **Official Attempts**: Your first three eligible race starts in each scoring window are your only official attempts. Attempts are counted when the race starts, not when it finishes.
- **Concurrent and Unfinished Races**: Concurrent starts each count as an attempt. Abandoned or unfinished races also consume an attempt and do not produce a valid time.
- **Daily Best Time**: Your fastest valid finish from your three official attempts becomes your time for that scoring window.
- **Final Score**: Your three daily best times are added together, and the lowest combined time wins.
- **Qualification**: You must record a valid time in all three scoring windows to qualify.
- **Attempt Limits**: Starts after your first three official attempts cannot improve your score, and unused attempts do not carry over. Grab your Kart, invite a friend, and come back for each new scoring window to give it your best shot.
