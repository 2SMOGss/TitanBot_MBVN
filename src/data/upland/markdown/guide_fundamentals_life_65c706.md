# Life in the Upland Metaverse : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/life
Life in the Upland Metaverse is coming closer. With the announcement of the Stem token that will represent life, Upland is working to make this a reality. The precursor to Stem is Protem. Protem is earned by feeding or spending protem on your totem to generate more protem. Once stem is introduced, all your protem will be converted to stem. Continue reading these pages to learn more about [totems](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/life/totems), [protem and stem](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/stem).
