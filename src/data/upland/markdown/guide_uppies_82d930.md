# Uppies - Upland Residents : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies
Both Uppies and residents are what populate the Upland Metaverse. Where will they live? This page details what Uppies and residents are and how you can draw them to your neighborhood and to live in your homes. Upland describes Uppies as "Think of them as real digital citizens with personalities, interests, and unique stories. From young families looking for schools and parks, to professionals eager for great jobs and nightlife, Uppies are searching for more than just a place to live. They want places where they can truly thrive." Each city will have its own distinctive Uppies, adding depth, personality, and a cultural flavor to every future city opening.

The foundation of the Upland metaverse is virtual land you are able to develop and build upon. On your properties, these structures are built with [Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet). There is a variety of [structures that serve different utility purposes](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types) in the metaverse. Residential structures are the specific type that will house these virtual residents. Individual players are able to [select their home address](https://upland-guide.com/community/governance-and-voting), but this is very different from the concept of these Uppies.

There is a distinction between what Upland calls "virtual residents" and Uppies. Uppies are mintable digital citizens, and residents are these virtual residents that are just represented by numbers who populate residential structures, neighborhoods and cities. Uppies can be [assigned to properties where they can perform automatic tasks](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility), and even discovered through [Uppie Quests.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppie-quests)

Residents are virtual and the total number of them in the metaverse will continue to grow as more residential structures get built. The [Upland Census](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/census) tracks the Upland population and which neighborhoods house the most residents. The exact metaverse population will be tied to the supply of [living units](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types/living-units), but the number of residents will always be much less than the total number of residential structures. This means there will be a lot of competition to have these virtual residents live in neighborhoods where you own properties. You will have to make your neighborhood appealing to compel these residents to reside there. Each residential building has a specific number of maximum residents that can live there as seen in the picture below. The maximum number of residents that can live in a neighborhood can be no more than the number of residential living units there.

Residents are attracted to specific neighborhoods based on several factors. They’ll gravitate toward neighborhoods with diverse [service structures](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types/service-structures), strong infrastructure, and all the amenities of a bustling, well-rounded community. Schools, shops, entertainment, safety, jobs, and transit are just a few of the keys to transforming your area into a thriving hub for residents. Your direct contribution to the neighborhoods ability to attract residents will increase your [influence score](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/influence-score), helping you to attract more residents.

Residents that are allocated to a specific neighborhood are then distributed amongst all the living units in that neighborhood. This number will change on a daily basis, and is your [residents score](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/residents-score). As new structures are being built across the metaverse, the desirability of residents to live in a specific neighborhood also changes. It is not enough for a neighborhood to have residential homes to house these residents. [Service structures](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types/service-structures), [plants](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants), [structure ornaments](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornaments), [metaventures (businesses)](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures)and many factors contribute to the desirability to live in a certain area. This is no different from the real world.

One of these contributing factors will be [neighborhood ratings](https://upland-guide.com/community/neighborhood-ratings), which is a metric that considers level of economic activity. Service structures are required to increase the desirability of residents to come to a specific neighborhood. It is important to collaborate and plan with other members of your neighborhood. Having a variety of different structures will balance that with residential building will be the key to drawing residents to your neighborhood.
