# Cinnamon, Forgecraft, Clockworks, and Lantern Light Come to Upland

November 11, 2026

This week’s four new **Service Structures** add one Entertainment destination and three Essential businesses to neighborhood builds. **Uppies** can stop for a cinnamon swirl, admire a rugged forge, meet at the clockworks, or bring lantern-shop warmth to a favorite street. Invite a friend back to explore the lineup, or bring someone new along to see what the community is building.

These **Service Structures** will be available from **Wednesday, November 11 at 9 AM PT **for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes The Cinnamon Swirl Kiosk, The Black Iron Forge, The Tick & Tock Clockworks, and The Lantern & Wick Shoppe for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, a **$6 saving** compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, November 11 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, November 12 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### The Cinnamon Swirl Kiosk

The Cinnamon Swirl Kiosk gives market squares and morning routes a cheerful, sweet stop, where **Uppies** can compare swirls and pick the treat that looks worth the detour. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500
**Price:** $2.00

### The Black Iron Forge

The Black Iron Forge adds rugged workshop character to heritage blocks, where **Uppies** can pause by the sturdy storefront and trade bold ideas for the neighborhood’s next build. *(Essential, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 9,000
**Price:** $5.00

### The Tick & Tock Clockworks

The Tick & Tock Clockworks brings old-world precision to busy shopping streets, where **Uppies** can meet before the next errand and settle who arrived exactly on time. *(Essential, 2 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 12,600
**Price:** $7.00

### The Lantern & Wick Shoppe

The Lantern & Wick Shoppe gives cozy districts a welcoming storefront, where **Uppies** can browse for the perfect glow and imagine a warmer corner for the evening. *(Essential, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,500
**Price:** $10.00

Whether **Uppies** are choosing a cinnamon swirl, gathering near the forge, meeting at the clockworks, or browsing lanterns, this week’s **Service Structures** give players four more ways to shape neighborhoods with personality. Collectors who want all four can grab the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share the lineup with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
