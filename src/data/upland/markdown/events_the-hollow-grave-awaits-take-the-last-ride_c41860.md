# The Hollow Grave Awaits. Take the Last Ride.

October 19, 2026

Upland’s Halloween celebration is pulling up with a home for the haunting and a ride built for collectors. **The Hollow Grave Funeral Home** and the **Last Ride Hearse: Collector’s Edition** arrive in separate sales on **Tuesday, October 20**, pairing a creepy seasonal atmosphere with a specific matching Service Unit benefit planned for the two assets.

Before the doors open, invite a former Uplander back for the haunting or bring a new friend along for their first Halloween in Upland.

### The Hollow Grave Funeral Home

Every city has a lively side. This Halloween, give yours a proper home for the dearly departed. **The Hollow Grave Funeral Home** brings a creepy new Service Structure to neighborhoods made for eerie stories, seasonal displays, and Uppies who do not mind a few extra chills after dark *(Public Service, 4 Service Unit).
*
**Spark Hours**: 16,650

There is **no registration** for the Funeral Home sale. It opens at **9:00 AM PT on Tuesday, October 20** for **$7**.

### Last Ride Hearse: Collector’s Edition

Some rides are once in a lifetime. This is one of them.

The **Last Ride Hearse: Collector’s Edition** is a **one-time-only Halloween 2026 release**, limited to **200 vehicles at $100 each**. This is the one and only sale run for the Collector’s Edition, available from October 20 through October 25, subject to its 200-unit quantity.

**Registration Opens:** Monday, October 19 at 9:00 AM PT
**Sale Begins:** Tuesday, October 20 at 9:00 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Sunday, October 25
**Quantity:** 200
**Price:** $100

### A Matching Pair With a Purpose

The Halloween theme goes beyond the name. The **Last Ride Hearse: Collector’s Edition** and **The Hollow Grave Funeral Home** will provide a **Service Unit bonus** as a specific matching vehicle-and-Service Structure pair, following the same kind of paired mechanism used by compatible police cars and Police Stations, ambulances and medical Service Structures, and other matching service combinations.

This benefit applies to the matching hearse and funeral home. It is not a blanket boost across unrelated Service Structures. The activation timing and full details will be shared separately.

### Make Halloween Feel at Home

Build the block that gives your Uppies chills, take the Last Ride before it disappears, and share the news with a former Uplander or a friend ready to create their first creepy corner of Upland.
