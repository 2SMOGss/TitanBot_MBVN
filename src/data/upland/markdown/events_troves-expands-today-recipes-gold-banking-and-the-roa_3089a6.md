# Troves Expands Today: Recipes, Gold Banking, and the Road to Level 40

October 27, 2026

Your Trove collection is about to take you further**, **with **new recipes to craf**t, **more Troves to discover**, and **new milestones****to reach.**

Today, **284 crafting recipes** go live at Service Structures, **Gold banking** opens, and **20 additional progression levels** unlock, giving you new goals to pursue all the way to **Level 40**. With this release, there are now **372 total Troves available to collect**. Put your finds to work, craft higher-level Troves, and see how far your collection can take you!

This** first batch of recipes** is just the beginning, with** more arriving** as the **phased Troves launch continues**.

### Turn Your Finds into Higher-Level Troves

Recipes let you use collected Troves as** ingredients to craft higher-level Troves**. Some of those crafted Troves can then become ingredients in other recipes, opening up **even more to discover** as you explore and collect.

Finding a Trove for the first time **reveals the recipes that use it**. Each discovered recipe shows the ingredients you need, so you can see what you already have and what you still need to find.

### How to Craft

- **Discover a recipe.** Find a Trove for the first time to reveal recipes that use it.
- **Gather the ingredients.** Check the recipe’s requirements and keep collecting to fill the gaps.
- **Visit the Service Structure.** Move your Block Explorer to the structure where the recipe is available, open its full-screen view, and select **Craft**.
- **Fulfill the recipe.** Choose an available recipe and use the required ingredients to craft. **Those ingredients are consumed.** *Recipe interface example. Requirements and rewards vary by recipe.*

Each recipe has a main result, and some recipes may also give **additional or rarer Troves**. Keep discovering, crafting, and exploring what your new Troves can become.

### Bank to Gold. Progress to Level 40.

**Gold** joins Bronze and Silver as the **newest banking tier**, while** 20 additional Upland progression levels** raise the current limit to **Level 40**.

Banking is the **primary way** to increase your **Upland progression level**. Permanently deposit Troves into your Trove Vault to meet progression requirements and work toward rewards. Check your profile to see your next milestone, its requirements, and the reward you can earn.

Remember: **banked Troves cannot be withdrawn or used in recipes**, so keep the ingredients you want to craft with before making a deposit.

**Gold is the newest banking tier **to unlock, with **higher tiers coming** as the phased Troves launch continues.
