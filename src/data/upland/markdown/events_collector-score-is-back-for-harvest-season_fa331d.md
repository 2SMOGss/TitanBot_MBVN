# Collector Score is Back For Harvest Season

October 05, 2026

Every collection has a few favorites. The Block Explorer you had to have. The pass that completed your set. The badge you worked all season to earn. This Harvest, those pieces could help put your name among Upland’s top collectors.

The **Harvest Season 2026 Collector Score Challenge** is back, with **UPX, collector badges, and an exclusive Block Explorer** waiting at the finish line. Build your seasonal collection, follow the standings, and see how far you can climb.

### Meet The Gilded Acorn ‘26

A little collector with a very big find. **The Gilded Acorn **’26 is a storybook squirrel with a leaf-lined satchel, proudly holding a golden acorn almost as big as its head. Warm autumn colors and a tiny pile of collected treasures bring the spirit of **Storybook Harvest** to this season’s collector reward.

Finish in the **top 25** of the Harvest Collector Score Challenge to earn **The Gilded Acorn ’26 Block Explorer**. It is a keepsake for the collectors who turn this season’s discoveries into a collection worth celebrating.

**1st–3rd Place**

- 200,000 UPX each
- Harvest Season Collector Badge
- The Gilded Acorn ’26 Block Explorer **4th–10th Place**

- 50,000 UPX each
- The Gilded Acorn ’26 Block Explorer **11th–25th Place**

- The Gilded Acorn ’26 Block Explorer

### Make Every Find Count

The seasonal challenge focuses on **eligible collectibles released during Harvest Season 2026**, not the size of your entire collection. Your Block Explorers, Season and City Passes, and Badges all have a part to play.

Keep an eye on new seasonal releases, pursue the badges you want to earn, and check your **Harvest seasonal ranking** as your collection grows. The All-Time leaderboard celebrates your wider collection, but this challenge’s prizes are decided by your Harvest score.

There is a golden acorn waiting at the end of this story. Give it a place in yours.

#### *Here Are the Collector Score Rules*

*Already familiar with Collector Score? You have the season’s reward details above. Read on for a refresher before you start your Harvest collection.*

- ***Eligible categories:**** Block Explorers, Season Passes, City Passes, and Badges released during Harvest Season 2026.*
- ***Score weighting:**** Block Explorers contribute 30%, Season and City Passes 20%, and Badges 50%.*
- ***Burned assets:**** Burned items do not count toward Collector Score. Season Pass activation and Fusion qualification are separate mechanics; qualifying for those benefits does not mean a burned pass still contributes to your Collector Score.*
- ***Seasonal standings:**** Harvest challenge rewards are based on the final Harvest seasonal standings, not the Overall Score ranking.*
- ***Ties:**** If players finish with the same score, the player who reached that score first ranks higher.*
- ***Leaderboard updates:**** The external leaderboard updates every 12 hours, so changes may not appear immediately.*
