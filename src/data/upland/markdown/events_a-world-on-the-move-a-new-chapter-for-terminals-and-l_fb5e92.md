# A World on the Move: A New Chapter for Terminals and Landmarks

October 14, 2026

Your city already has residents. Soon, **where they travel and what they visit will matter**.

This is a **first look **at a feature we’re developing for **after Troves launches**. It will give residents and Uppies **dynamic daily journeys**, turn **terminals and landmarks** into **active parts **of those journeys, and create concrete **new benefits** for the players who own them.

Share this preview with a friend who helped you build your neighborhood, or someone you’d love to welcome into it. **The next chapter is more exciting when we build the community together.**

### From Residents to Daily Journeys

**Residents** already connect desirable neighborhoods with a population living across Upland’s cities, as reflected in the [Census Score](https://www.upland.me/census). The next step is to **set that population in motion**. Planned **simulated travel** will change from day to day, reflecting where **residents and Uppies are traveling** from and to.

Players will be able to **open a screen and see where residents are traveling and which destinations they visit**. That view will make the rhythm of the world easier to understand: which cities are **sending travelers**, which connections are busy, and which **destinations **are drawing attention.

**Terminals and landmarks** have different jobs in that movement. **Bus, airport, and train terminals connect travel between cities. Landmarks attract visits.** Player activity, including travel through terminals, can help boost that travel, while resident journeys turn landmarks into destinations within a **living daily network**.

### Why Landmark and Terminal Ownership Matters

**Landmark owners benefit by attracting visitors.** The more residents and Uppies visit your landmark, the more **Troves will automatically accumulate** there for you. This gives landmark ownership a new purpose. A landmark is no longer just part of the skyline; it’s a **hub of attraction that generates rewards** based on the activity it draws.

And there’s more on the horizon. **Future Destination Structures** will give players new ways to **increase the attraction** of their cities and neighborhoods, drawing more residents and Uppies to the places they build. These structures will create **new opportunities** for communities to work together to make their neighborhoods **more desirable destinations**. We’ll have much more to share about Destination Structures as development progresses.

**Terminal owners benefit from the traffic moving through their connections.** Alongside the rewards terminals already earn from player travel, increased resident and Uppie traffic will mean **more Troves accumulating** at their terminals for owners to collect.

**Automation will make collecting those Troves even easier.** There will be different paths to automating Trove collection across Upland. For example, players will eventually be able to employ an Uppie at a Service Structure or assign an Uppie to a property to automate Trove collection. **Landmarks and terminals will have a similar path to automation**, allowing owners to automatically collect the Troves their locations generate. We’ll share more about these systems and their requirements as we get closer to launch.

**Terminals and landmarks play different but connected roles.** Terminals move travelers between cities, while landmarks give them a reason to visit. Desirable neighborhoods support resident populations, residents take daily journeys through terminals, and landmarks attract visitors. Together, these systems create a living network where **the places players own and the communities they build directly influence the rewards they can earn.**

### Building a More Connected Upland

This is only the beginning of the **destination** story. Future structures, which will be **announced separately**, are planned to help players **build up the attraction **of their cities and neighborhoods, make landmarks even more desirable destinations, and turn terminals into busier connections. Landmarks are already attractions in this system; these future structures will give communities more ways to strengthen the places around them.

**Simulated travel and automation **also lay groundwork for an Upland where **more systems** connect over time, including future gameplay such as** trade routes and fishing**. Those mechanics are not part of this preview, but the movement of residents and Uppies can help make the world around them** feel more alive**.

**This remains a development preview.** Simulated travel and automated Trove accumulation for terminals and landmarks are planned for after the Troves launch, not alongside it, and no release date is being announced today. We’ll share** more mechanics and details** as development progresses.
