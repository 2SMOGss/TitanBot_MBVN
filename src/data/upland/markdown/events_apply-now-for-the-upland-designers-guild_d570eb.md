## The Mission

The mission of the Upland Designers Guild is to assist the Upland team in designing various assets for Upland and move those assets into Upland as quickly as possible. Members of the Upland Designers Guild are those who have been selected by the Upland team through an application process. Guild members have proven their design skills and have the option to work with the Upland team to earn rewards such as UPX and more. 

‍

## Why should you join the Guild?

Acceptance into the Upland Designers Guild shows that you are one of the top designers in the Upland community. Your membership will enable you to take on design tasks as determined by the Upland team and earn various rewards. 

‍

As an example, a task could be to add textures and color to a 3D model, such as a landmark. In return, you would receive a certain amount of UPX (depending on the scope) when the model is brought to life in Upland.

‍

## How to apply?

Applications will be accepted and reviewed on a rolling basis****. To apply, please complete the application (link below) and submit the following materials:

- A short paragraph describing your 3D modeling experience. 
- A short paragraph detailing why you want to participate in the Upland Designers Guild. 
- A portfolio. Show us a sample of your work. (ie: Sketchfab profile, DeviantArt, etc.) ‍

Applications will be reviewed by the Upland design team based on their submitted material. As the work of the Guild expands, we may accept new members from time to time.
