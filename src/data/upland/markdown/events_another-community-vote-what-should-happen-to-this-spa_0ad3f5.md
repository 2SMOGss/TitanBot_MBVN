# Another Community Vote: What Should Happen to This SPARKLET Pool?

October 13, 2026

It’s time for another community vote! Starting **Tuesday, October 13**, you can help decide what should happen to the current pool of spent SPARKLET *(not all SPARKLET)*. The proposal is to **burn this pool and permanently remove it from circulation**. Read the details below, make your voice heard, and share this announcement with friends who care about SPARKLET so they can take part, too.

From **April — October 2026**, approximately **2.9M SPARKLET****was spent on seed generation and Uppie merges**. Based on community feedback, **Upland is recommending** that this **SPARKLET be burned** and permanently removed from circulation.

This vote is focused **only** on what should happen to the approximately **2.9M SPARKLET** generated from these 2026 activities. Upland will hold similar votes for **future SPARKLET spent** through activities.

Existing rewards and incentive pools are **currently well-funded**, and reducing circulating supply **supports the long-term sustainability** of SPARKLET. Now, the community is being asked to **ratify this recommendation**.

### Vote Details

**Voting Opens**: Tuesday, October 13 at 9:00 AM PT

**Voting Closes**: Friday, October 16 at 9:00 AM PT

**Proposal**: Based on previous community feedback, Upland recommends burning the ~**2.9M SPARKLET** spent on seed generation and Uppie merges so far in 2026.

- **Vote YES**: Burn the approximately **2.9M SPARKLET** spent on seed generation and Uppie merges in 2026. This SPARKLET will be permanently removed from circulation.
- **Vote NO**: Do not burn the SPARKLET. If the community votes no, we’ll go back to the drawing board and bring forward new proposals for how this SPARKLET should be handled. **Have a different idea for this specific SPARKLET pool?** Let us know what you would like to see done with it and why. Share your suggestions on our Nolt feedback board: [https://uplandfeedback.nolt.io/2981](https://uplandfeedback.nolt.io/2836)

The vote goes live **Tuesday, October 13 at 9:00 AM PT**. Read the proposal, cast your vote before **Friday, October 16 at 9:00 AM PT**, and help decide what happens to this pool. Bring the conversation to your friends, your neighborhood, and fellow SPARKLET holders, make sure they know when and why to vote!
