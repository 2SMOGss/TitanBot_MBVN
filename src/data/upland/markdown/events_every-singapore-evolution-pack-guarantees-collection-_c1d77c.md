September 04, 2026

This is the headline: **100% of Singapore Standard Packs and Singapore Deluxe Packs will include Collection properties**.

With no random Collection chance attached, every buyer walks away with Collection property access from **Singapore**.

The **Asian Modular Apartments: Rattan Shop Edition** brings the texture and character of a traditional rattan shop to Upland. Its street-level storefront celebrates a craft known for turning natural materials into furniture, baskets, decor, and everyday goods, while the apartments above add the density and utility of the Asian modular lineup.

The Rattan Shop Edition gives builders a warm, distinctive storefront that can stand on its own or connect with other Asian Modular Apartments to create larger, more detailed developments. It is a structure designed to make **Singapore** streets feel more lived-in, local, and complete.

Like all Modular structures, it can be combined with other structures in its regional set to create larger customized builds. This structure is region-locked to Asian cities.
