# Stock Car Pro Series : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/upland-partnerships/stock-car-pro-series
Upland's partnership with [Stock Car Pro Series](https://www.stockproseries.com.br/stockcar/proseries) in Brazil is an exciting an innovative partnership that brings racing to the metaverse. A digital twin of the Interlagos race track in Sao Paulo has been constructed in Upland, where players who own Stock Car Pro Series cars will be able to compete in exclusive races for great prizes. Players can also own other types of assets to increase their fan score and earn UPX through creating collections. Learn about all the Stock Car Pro Series assets below as well as other important information around collections. [Click here to learn more about car racing in Upland.](https://upland-guide.com/entertainment/car-racing)

Below are a list of the different Stock Car Series Pro assets










** 
** 

Essential Legits 



Essentials are the most common type of Legits. However, even within these Essential Legits, there are different rarity levels making some of them extremely difficult to come by.

These essentials are not only collectibles, but they boost your fan score.  Beyond that, that can be added to collections where you can earn a one time UPX bonus for completing the collection, and gain a fan score multiplier boost.





** 
** 

Memento Pass Legits 



Passes are a specific type of Legit that function like a voucher would.  Passes are redeemable for Mementos and Spotlights. [Learn about how to exchange your Pass for Mementos and Spotlights](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/upland-legits/legit-pass-exchange-for-memento-spotlight).

Owning a pass, memento or spotlight is required in order to have the opportunity to purchase a Stock Car Pro Series car.





** 
** 

Memento and Spotlight Legits 



Mementos are a type of Legit that commemorates a specific moment or event.  This is done by having the driver's statistics from said race included as part of the Legit. Some types of Mementos can be completely unique with only one of them created.

Mementos and spotlights can also boost your fan score and be included in collections to earn an UPX bonus and boosted fan points multipliers.

These Mementos can be a variety of items such as:

- Outfit Wearables (these can be worn by your [3D Ready Player Me Avatar](https://upland-guide.com/community/3d-avatar-identity/ready-player-me-avatar))

- Helmets

- Driver Caps

- Spotlights

A Spotlight is a special type of Memento in the fact that in addition to it commemorating a specific moment or race, the visual representation of the Legit is not a 2D image, but a video.





** 
** 

Cars 



In order to have an opportunity to purchase an official Stock Car Pro Series car, you will have to own either a Stock Car Pro Series pass, memento or spotlight.

These cars will be required for exclusive races where winners are able to win prizes.  (For the 2023 season, MV Motors vehicles can be used to qualify).

**Trainer Cars: **Perfect for Upland Racing beginners, these cars are designed with modest stats that provide a balanced and approachable introduction to the Stock Car Pro Series. Whether you're new to the racing scene or looking for a smoother learning curve, Trainer Cars offer a user-friendly option.

**Competition Cars: **Striking a balance between accessibility and performance, Competition Cars deliver increased stats that make them versatile contenders on the track. Resembling the real Stock Car Pro Series vehicles, these cars offer a taste of authenticity while still catering to a wide range of racers.

**Pro Cars: **For those seeking the pinnacle of racing excellence, Pro Cars are the ultimate choice, with top-tier stats and an impeccable, lifelike resemblance to real-world Stock Car Pro Series cars. These vehicles even come complete with sponsor logos. Every detail is finely tuned to replicate the thrill and prestige of professional Stock Car Pro Series Racing.





** 
** 

Block Explorers 



Block Explorers are different avatars that you can use.  In the future, block explorers will be used as trainable drivers for your cars.  They will also have an influence on your ability to excel at racing.

Learn more about block explorers [here](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers).








Complete these collections by to earn one time UPX reward, and a fan score multiplier. Stock Car Pro Series collections can be found in the main menu under racing.
