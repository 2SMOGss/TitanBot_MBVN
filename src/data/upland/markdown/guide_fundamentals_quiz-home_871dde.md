# Upland Guide Quiz : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/quiz-home
Test your knowledge about the Upland Metaverse before entering. The Upland Guide will help you avoid making critical errors.

If you are a new player and you think you are ready to dive into the game, test your knowledge in our quizzes below. Upland has the [missions feature](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/missions) that will help guide you through the basics, but we believe that's not enough! [This video](https://youtu.be/Jvvjbolxa9Q) explains why the Upland Guide is integral to your success in completing Upland's missions and beyond these introductory steps.

Different people have different motives for playing Upland. Some see it as a fun way to own some properties, some see it as a way to earn money. There is room for all types of strategies within the Metaverse. Having said that, just like in real life, there are people who try to take advantage others. It is paramount that you understand enough about the game not to fall into any traps, or at least be educated enough to be able to ask for advice. Join the [Upland Guide Discord server](https://discord.gg/rTcdta6wSt) where you can find additional information, and even ask for advice if you get stuck. Know that our [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/UplandGuide)channel is another great source of information to help you get started.

There is no limit to the number of times you can take a quiz. At the conclusion of each quiz, if you got an answer wrong, you will be directed to the exact web page on the Upland Guide where you can learn more about that topic. Before taking the quizzes detailed below, we recommend browsing the page below to learn about all the different features in Upland.
