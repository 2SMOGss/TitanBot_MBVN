During sparklet weekend, Upland has a sale in the Upland Store.  By purchasing Sparklet within Upland as opposed to crypto exchanges, there are a number of benefits:

- discounted price compared to market price on exchanges.  This is possible due to the reclaim requirements when sending Sparklet to your MetaMask wallet.

- ease of purchase.  No need to own a crypto wallet. (you will need one if you wish to sell Sparklet)

- purchase directly for USD

Sparklet is sold in $10, $20, $50, $100, $200, and $300 packages.  The amount of sparklet in each package can vary based on its market price.  The Upland Store price is based on the average market price of Sparklet from the past two weeks. The store price won’t change, but the number of tokens included may be adjusted depending on market fluctuations.

When purchasing from the Upland store, make sure you do this using a browser and not the app.  There are additional fees added when using the app store.  Even on your phone, use your browser and login through [play.upland.me](http://play.upland.me)

The sale starts on Fridays at 9AM PT for each Friday during the season.  There is no registration, and the quantities available are limited.

After purchasing your Sparklet, make sure to start staking it.  If you wish to sell that Sparklet, learn more about the [staking reclaiming requirements](https://youtu.be/ZkN1BGE88AQ).

Leveling up to the next status in the game - even becoming an Uplander will grant you sparklet. Normally players who become Uplanders or Pros do not receive any sparklet when graduating to the next level in the Metaverse.  If you are close to the status, it might make sense to either sell a property to get you to the next level, or maybe even put in a small amount of money in order to ensure you receive the bonus sparklet.
