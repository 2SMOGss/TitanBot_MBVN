We’ve changed the service that sends Upland email, and organized our messages into five subscription groups you can turn on or off. You get more control over what lands in your inbox, and the mail you want has a better chance of getting there.

Purchase receipts, security alerts, and account emails always stay on, whatever you choose. The new groups are only for the optional mail, so you can pick what fits how you play.

Here’s a look at the new groups, the two addresses to save, and how to set your preferences.

### The New Subscription Groups

You don’t have to take all of them. Choose the ones you want and leave the rest off.

- **Upland Recap.** Drops most weeks, with top strategic plays, community highlights, and a short overview of announcements you might have missed.
- **Game News & Releases.** Be the first to find out when new real-world cities launch on the map, new features go live, and gameplay guides drop. Sent around important release dates.
- **Events & Competitions.** Treasure Hunts, Troves, Racing, and more. Real-time alerts for live tournaments, global leaderboards, and in-game prize pools, sent during active challenges.
- **Just for You.** Picks based on how you play: custom milestones, deals, and strategy tips tuned to you. About once a month.
- **Style Shop.** Official apparel and gear drops, limited-edition merch, and early access to store discounts. Occasionally.

### Add These Two Addresses to Your Contacts

Upland email now comes from two addresses:

- **[[email protected]](https://www.upland.me/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)** for news, updates, and the groups above
- **[[email protected]](https://www.upland.me/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection)** for account email like purchase receipts and password resets Please add both to your contacts or your safe senders list. Inbox filters often treat a new sending address as unfamiliar, even when the mail is from us. Saving both helps your receipts, password resets, and other account email reach your inbox instead of spam or junk.

- **Gmail.** Open [Google Contacts](https://contacts.google.com/), create a contact for each address, and save. If a message from us is in Spam, open it and choose Not spam.
- **Outlook.** Go to Settings, then Mail, then Junk email. Under Safe senders and domains, add each address and save.
- **Apple Mail and iCloud.** Add both addresses to your iCloud contacts. If a message is in Junk, move it to your Inbox.
- **Yahoo Mail.** Add both addresses to your contacts. If a message lands in Spam, select it and choose Not spam. If you use another email app, the same idea applies: save both addresses as contacts, and mark anything in junk as not junk.
