# Block Explorer Stamina : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers/stamina
Your [block explorer](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers) plays an important role in the Upland Metaverse, as it is your game piece that moves around the map, but stamina adds an important update that will focus on which specific block explorer you have selected. As players collect [troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves)and [complete recipes](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/recipes), your block explorer will influence how many troves you can gather and recipes you can fulfill before needing to cooldown. This adds more utility to block explorers and adds another layer of decision making that connects movement, ownership and activity within the metaverse.

The stamina feature scales based on rarity. Higher-tier explorers, like [terraformers](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers/terraformer-wizard), will be able to operate longer and recover faster, while more common explorers will have tighter limits.

While collecting troves or completing recipes you don't need to actively change your block explorer when it reaches its limit. The system is designed for another explorer to automatically take over, ensuring that gameplay continues without interruption. Players with more (and rarer) block explorers will be able to stay active longer. This new mechanic gives weight to rarity and introduces a reason to think carefully about how each explorer is used.

Every 6 hours your stamina resets, and you will be able to collect more troves. Your stamina can be viewed in your profile, on each block explorer, and within your block explorer assets. There you will also be able to view when the stamina refills. Even if you don't own a block explorer, every player starts with 100 stamina.

When it comes to trove collecting, there are certain [level Uppies](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility) that can automatically collect troves from both [plants](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants)and structures. The block explorer stamina feature is independent of your Uppie's capacity to collect troves.

Beyond stamina, Upland is exploring modifiers for Block Explorers, where some block explorers may perform better when collecting certain types of troves, while others may improve the likelihood of higher-quality rewards or interact more effectively with specific [service structures](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/structure-types/service-structures). There is also room for time-based or [seasonal](https://upland-guide.com/metaverse-seasons)advantages, where using the right explorer at the right moment can produce better outcomes.

In the future, completing a full series of block explorers could unlock additional benefits that go beyond the sum of their parts. This creates a longer-term incentive structure that rewards both active play and deliberate collecting.
