# Dallas Evolution Brings Texas Mercantile to Main Street

October 09, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Dallas** with a little Texas storefront character and another opportunity to grow your place in the city. This week’s signature structure, **Main Street Modular Apartments: Texas Mercantile General Store**, brings a classic general-store theme to the modular lineup, giving your next neighborhood build a distinctly local touch.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release pairs property access with a new building opportunity. Deluxe Packs also include a **10% chance** of receiving a property in the existing **White Rock Hills Limited Collection**.

### Neighborhoods

No new neighborhoods are opening in this release. Pack properties come from Dallas’s existing footprint.

### Collections

The **White Rock Hills Limited Collection** is already published. This release does not introduce a new Collection.

Each **Dallas Deluxe Pack** has a **10% chance** of containing a White Rock Hills Collection property. Collection properties are not guaranteed in every Deluxe Pack.

### Details

Both **Dallas Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, October 9 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, October 10 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, October 16 at 9 AM PT

### Dallas Standard Pack

Includes one random property in Dallas with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX

### Dallas Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in Dallas with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 41,000 UPX**, a **10% chance** of receiving a property in the **White Rock Hills Limited Collection**, and the new **Main Street Modular Apartments: Texas Mercantile General Store** structure.

For players growing their Dallas portfolio or adding another storefront to a modular build, the Deluxe Pack brings properties and Texas character together in one release.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 41,000 UPX combined
**Collection Property Chance:** 10% White Rock Hills Limited Collection

Bring a little old-fashioned general-store charm to your next city block. **Main Street Modular Apartments: Texas Mercantile General Store** adds a Texas mercantile theme to the Main Street modular lineup, offering a new way to give your neighborhood its own personality.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Dallas Deluxe Pack in this release**.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about **building cities with depth**, not just opening new territory. With guaranteed property value, a chance at an existing Limited Collection property in Deluxe Packs, and a new signature structure, this Dallas release gives players another reason to build on what is already taking shape.

Choose the pack that fits your next move, and invite a returning friend or someone new to discover what you’re building in Dallas.
