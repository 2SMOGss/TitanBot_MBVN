# A Haunted Harvest Comes to the Neighborhood

October 14, 2026

**Storybook Harvest** is taking a mysterious turn. This week’s four new **Service Structures** bring three haunted neighborhood stops and one welcoming cider house to your autumn streets. Invite a friend back to explore what is lurking around the next corner, or introduce someone new to the community you are building.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, October 14 at 9 AM PT **for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes Mother Cluckers Chicken, Madame Moon’s Fortune Telling, Grimwood Apothecary, and The Happy Harvest Cider House for **$18**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, a **$6 saving** compared with buying all four individually for $24.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available in the Daily Store for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, October 14 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, October 15 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

### Mother Cluckers Chicken

Mother Cluckers Chicken offers a late-night neighborhood stop for hungry **Uppies** who are brave enough to follow the whispers. It is the kind of place where friends can gather for a bite, trade ghost stories, and try not to wonder who just joined the table. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 4,500

**Price:** $2.00

### Madame Moon’s Fortune Telling

Madame Moon’s Fortune Telling invites curious **Uppies** to ask what waits just beyond the next turn. Add it to an eerie shopping lane or a quiet neighborhood corner where fortunes are shared softly and some questions may be better left unanswered. *(Entertainment, 3 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 12,600

**Price:** $5.00

### Grimwood Apothecary

Grimwood Apothecary is a mysterious neighborhood destination for remedies, secrets, and stories that refuse to stay buried. Place it along a haunted main street or tucked-away corner where your **Uppies** can seek an answer — and hope they understand the warning that comes with it. *(Essentials, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 16,200

**Price:** $7.00

### The Happy Harvest Cider House

The Happy Harvest Cider House brings a warm fall gathering place to your neighborhood. Give your **Uppies** a cheerful stop for crisp autumn outings, harvest-season meetups, and a comforting cup of cider after exploring the mysteries down the road. *(Entertainment, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 22,050

**Price:** $10.00

Whether players are chasing whispers, seeking a glimpse of the future, searching for a mysterious remedy, or sharing cider with friends, this week’s **Service Structures** make **Storybook Harvest** neighborhoods even more memorable. Collectors who want all four can pick up the one-day **Service Structure Value Pack** before it disappears. Share your haunted harvest neighborhood with a returning friend or someone discovering Upland for the first time.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
