# Buenos Aires Evolution Opens Three Neighborhoods and Takes Two to Tango

September 18, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Buenos Aires** with **three new neighborhoods**, **three new Collections**, and a new reason to keep building. The **South America Modular Apartments: Two Can Tango Bar Edition** joins the lineup as another **middle section**, giving players a new piece to expand their South America modular developments.

With **100 Standard Packs and 300 Deluxe Packs** available, both options offer a **50% chance of receiving a Collection property**. Whether you are growing your **Buenos Aires** footprint, chasing a new Collection, or adding to your modulars, this release gives you a few different ways to make your next move.

### Neighborhoods

Three new neighborhoods are opening in this Evolution:

- Avellaneda
- Monserrat
- Parque Patricios

### Collections

**Buenos Aires** is adding **three new Collections**:

- **Monserrat:** Limited Collection
- **Avenida de Mayo:** Rare Collection
- **Avenida Varela:** Exclusive Collection Both the Standard Pack and Deluxe Pack have a **50% chance of containing a Collection property**. Collection properties are not guaranteed in every pack.

### Details

Both **Buenos Aires Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, September 18 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, September 19 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, September 25 at 9 AM PT

### Buenos Aires Standard Pack

Includes one random property in **Buenos Aires** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX** and a **50% chance of receiving a Collection property**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 50%

### Buenos Aires Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in **Buenos Aires** with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 44,000 UPX**, a **50% chance of receiving a Collection property**, and the new **South America Modular Apartments: Two Can Tango Bar Edition**.

For players looking to grow their property portfolio and their modular builds, the Deluxe Pack brings both together in one release.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 44,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 50%

Bring a little **Buenos Aires** nightlife to your next build. The **South America Modular Apartments: Two Can Tango Bar Edition** adds a tango-bar theme to the regional modular lineup, pairing local character with a practical addition for builders.

This is another **middle section** for the South America modular set. Combine it with your existing pieces to extend your builds, fill the space between sections, and give a growing modular block a new focal point. It is a piece to build around, not just a structure to place on its own.

### Looking Ahead

New neighborhoods open more of the city. New Collections give players fresh targets. And the **Two Can Tango Bar Edition** gives builders another way to bring those streets to life.

Choose your next neighborhood, plan your next Collection, or make room for the next piece of your modular build. **Buenos Aires is ready for its next step.**
