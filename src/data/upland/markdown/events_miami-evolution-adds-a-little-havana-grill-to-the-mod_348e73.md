# Miami Evolution Adds a Little Havana Grill to the Modular Lineup

September 11, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Miami** this week with a release built for players who want to keep expanding their modular developments. There are 5 new neighborhoods in this Evolution as well as a distinctly **Miami** addition to the East Coast Modular Apartments lineup: the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Little Havana Grill Edition**.

With only **100 Standard Bundles and 350 Deluxe Bundles** available, this week’s release gives players another limited opportunity to add **Miami** properties while unlocking a Cuban restaurant-inspired **middle section** that helps complete larger modular builds.

### Neighborhoods

This week is about adding depth to **Miami** and giving builders another piece to use across their East Coast modular developments.

The following neighborhoods are new to this evolution:

- KING HEIGHTS
- PALM GROVE
- BICENTENNIAL PARK
- HISTORIC BUENA VISTA EAST
- BELLE MEADE WEST

### Collections

No new Collections are being introduced in this release. The focus is on limited **Miami** property access and the debut of a new modular structure.

### Details

Both **Miami Evolution Bundles** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, September 11 at 9 AM PT

**Sale Starts:** Saturday, September 12 at 9 AM PT

**Sale Ends:** Friday, September 18 at 7 AM PT

### Miami Standard Bundle

Includes **Miami** property value with a guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX

**Quantity:** 100

**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX

### Miami Deluxe Bundle

Includes **Miami** property value with a guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 47,000 UPX, plus the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Little Havana Grill Edition**.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the **Miami Deluxe Bundle**, making it the premium option for players who want stronger combined property value and an important new piece for their East Coast modular builds.

**Price:** $20

**Quantity:** 350

**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 47,000 UPX

The **East Coast Modular Apartments: Little Havana Grill Edition** brings a distinctly **Miami** flavor to Upland’s streets. Inspired by the city’s Cuban restaurant culture, it adds a lively neighborhood grill at street level while preserving the apartments and density that make the East Coast modular lineup so useful.

More importantly, this is another **middle section** for the modular set. It can connect with previously released pieces to help builders extend longer blocks, fill out larger developments, and create more complete streetscapes instead of relying on standalone structures.

Like all Modular structures, it can be combined with other structures in its regional set to create larger customized builds. This structure is region-locked to East Coast cities.

### Looking Ahead

No new Collections are arriving this week, but **Miami**’s modular toolkit is getting deeper. With only **450 total bundles** available and the **East Coast Modular Apartments: Little Havana Grill Edition** adding another versatile **middle section**, this Evolution gives builders a practical new way to complete larger **Miami** developments.
