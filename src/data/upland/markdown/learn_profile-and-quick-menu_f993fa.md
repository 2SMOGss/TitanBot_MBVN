# Profile & Quick Menu

## Profile

Players spend a fair amount of time in the Profiles to get a snapshot of their progress, manage assets, etc. Familiarizing yourself with the elements of the profile page will help you be more comfortable with the experience.

There are two tabs: Details and Assets


**Details tab:**

Pencil icon: Edit your profile to change your Block Explorer and background color

Next to block explorer: See your current status and how much you need to increase your UPX networth to get to the next level.

The Referral Program button generates a unique URL that players can share to receive bonus UPX when their friends sign up.

The profile picture button allows you do download your profile pictures

For Visitors only: Click on the green CTA renews your Upland visa.

You can set your Upland Home Address, which is needed to vote and join a neighborhood. (Note: KYC is required to set an address)

**Assets tab:**

Shows your Asset Wallet, where you can see all your owned properties and lists of other assets.

## Quick Menu

Click or tap the three dots at the bottom of the screen to see the quick menu.


Here you'll find a variety of options, including a view of your properties, collections, Upland's mission center, leaderboards, partner content assets, a search for other players, game settings and much more.
