# Queens Evolution Cashes In with Money Exchange

August 14, 2026

City Evolution returns to Queens this week with another way to build in one of New York’s most dynamic boroughs. **Queens is already open**, so this release focuses on adding more property opportunities and the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Money Exchange Edition**.

This week’s Queens Evolution Pack gives players another way to enter or expand in the city without opening any new neighborhoods or introducing new Collections. With **250 Standard Packs** and **320 Deluxe Packs** available, players can grow their Queens footprint while the Deluxe Pack brings a new currency exchange **middle structure** to East Coast modular builds.

### Neighborhoods

**No new neighborhoods** are opening with this release. **Queens is already open**, and all properties included in these packs come from the city’s existing footprint.

### Collections

**No new Collections** are being introduced with this release.

### Details

Both Queens packs will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, August 14 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, August 15 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, August 21 at 9 AM PT

### Queens Standard Pack

Includes one random property from Queens with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 250
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX

### Queens Deluxe Pack

Includes random properties from Queens with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 41,000 UPX** and the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Money Exchange Edition** structure.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the **Queens Deluxe Pack**, making it a standout addition for players looking to bring more everyday cfity life to their East Coast modular builds.

**Price:** $20
**Quantity:** 320
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 41,000 UPX

The **East Coast Modular Apartments: Money Exchange Edition** brings a neighborhood currency exchange to Upland’s streetscape, adding another practical storefront to the ground floor with residential space above.

What makes this release **especially useful** is its place within the modular apartment system. While many recent releases have focused on corner structures, the Money Exchange is a **middle structure**, giving builders an important new piece for filling out longer street-facing designs and creating more complete modular complexes.

Like all Modular structures, this build is fully modular, making it ideal for stacking and combining into larger, customized builds. This structure is region-locked to East Coast cities.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about more than opening new neighborhoods. It is also about building more depth into cities that are already open. With more Queens properties entering circulation and the **Money Exchange joining the East Coast modular lineup**, this release gives players new ways to grow their footprint and bring their blocks to life.
