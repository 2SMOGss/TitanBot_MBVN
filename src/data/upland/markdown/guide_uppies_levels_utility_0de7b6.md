# Uppie Utility : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility
The higher the level of your Uppie, the more valuable it is. The following page describes certain utility that specific levels of Uppies have.
[Learn more about Uppies here.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies)

## All Level Uppies

Assigning an Uppie of any level to a property that has a resident on it makes the property eligible to spawn an Uppie quest. Note that an eligible property will not necessarily have a quest spawned at all times.

## Level 3

A level 3 Uppie can be created by merging a total of 4 level 1 Uppies.

Assigning a Level 3 or higher Uppie to a property will automatically handle petting for the [plants](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants)on that property. This means that any property with an assigned Uppie will be able to maintain a high [wellbeing](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/ornamental-plants/health-and-wellbeing)score without the player manually petting those plants. [Learn how to assign an Uppie to a property.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility/uppie-plant-care)

Uppies and Residents are drawn to properties where plants are healthy and actively maintained. Players who consistently care for their plants will naturally create spaces that attract more activity to their properties and neighborhoods.

Keeping plants healthy helps players receive the maximum potential Trove drops, while higher well-being can improve the chances of receiving more and rarer Troves.

Note that when purchasing a property with an Uppie assigned to it you are **not** purchasing the Uppie as well

## Level 5

A level 5 Uppie can be created by merging a total of 16 level 1 Uppies.

Employing a level 5 Uppie or higher at a **service structure** will affect your service score of that structure when the Uppie is employed there. This will play a large part of residents score. [Learn more about Uppie employment here.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/employing-uppies)

Assigning a level 5 Uppie or higher to a **property **will do more than care for plants (as described in level 3), they will also collect [Troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves)from those plants automatically. The collection capacity will depend on the Uppie’s level. Higher-level Uppies assigned to properties will be able to collect more Troves each day. The collection capacity is independent of the collection capacity when you are manually collecting troves. This will be based on your [block explorer's stamina](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-assets/block-explorers/stamina).

## TBD Level

Uppies Will Bring Properties to Life. When assigned to a property, Uppies will move around that space in 3D and in real time, bringing new activity, personality, and life to the places players own and develop. Players will see activity throughout their properties as Uppies move around and care for plants, adding charm, personality, and a sense of daily life to neighborhoods across Upland.

Players who want their unique Uppie’s distinct look to appear on the property will be able to enhance it. Once enhanced, that Uppie will always appear as its unique self wherever it is assigned, even after being sold.
