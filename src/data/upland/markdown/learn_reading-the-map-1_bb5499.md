# Game Tips

There's a lot going on in Upland. Here, we share tips to help new players get off to a good start. These tips are in no particular order, and there are many other guides out there to boost your experience. Feel free to check out guides from veteran players on YouTube, Telegram, Discord, Reddit, X, and other channels they broadcast to.

### Connect With The Community

The key to success in Upland is connecting with the community. Active players progress faster and enjoy a richer experience. While solo play is an option, the vibrant Upland community offers support, creativity, and fun! Join the in-game chat, [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/upland), or [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/UplandMe/) to get involved and make the most of your journey.

### Use Your Free UPX Wisely

In Upland, your first property is free! Use the UPX given to you to acquire an unowned Fair Start Act (FSA) parcel (i.e., the gray-colored FSA parcel), and you're off and running!

### Sends

Sends are the only way to move Block Explorers to a specific location (within its discovery range). Sends are needed for activities such as Treasure Hunts and city tours.

### Locate Your Block Explorer

If you take your eye off the screen, you may lose your Block Explorer! Block Explorers are always on the move without player input. If you can't find your Block Explorer, simply press the pawn icon, and you will automatically jump to your Block Explorer's location.

### Camera Controls

Feel free to move the camera from the top-down position. To do this, you can tilt the camera by holding Shift + Arrow key.

### Increase Your UPX Earnings

Properties come with a passive UPX yield that players can add to their wallets. However, the savviest players boost these property yields by completing collections. Property Collections are listed in the player's profile. We highlight some of the easier collections below.

- Newbie: Own any one property in Upland.
- City Pro: Own five properties in the same city.
- King of the Street: Own three properties on the same street.

Take a look at the Collections in each city and plan your property acquisitions for more opportunities.

### Join Activities

Some activities are really easy to join and can be rewarding. For example, players who complete a city tour (which are only available after a city first opens), earn free Sparklet *and* recuperate 100% of their registration fee.

### Location, Location, Location

When acquiring properties, look for locations that offer advantages. For example, when someone travels to your address, you earn a little free UPX. This makes properties around "hot" locations (e.g., stadiums, City Halls, Terminals, etc.) good spots to plant a flag if you can.

And if your property doesn't have anything interesting around it, build Structures, decorate with ornaments, plants, or outdoor decor, or launch a Metaventure to make it an interesting attraction! It's up to you!
