# Reading the Map

## The map is your home screen

The game happens here. Architects see a lot of information on the map that can be a little jarring at first. A major part of familiarizing yourself with Upland is learning what all the icons and symbols represent.

Take a look at the numbered items in the image and use the key below for brief explanations.

## Getting familiar with core elements

- **Block Explorer.** Your character on the map. Wanders cities automatically. You can direct it manually using a "Send".
- **UPX balance.** Top right. Your in-game currency for buying and trading properties.
- **Store.** Buy UPX, SPARKLET, STEM, structures, vehicles, and seeds.
- **Events icon.** Active in-game events. Worth checking — many drops and contests are time-limited.
- **Property colors.** Bright green = unminted (claim to be first owner). Dark green = on the secondary market. Blue = owned, not listed. Yellow accent on green = FSA (Fair Start Act), reserved for newer players.

> **Note:** Start by tapping a bright green or FSA property to see its price and yield. FSA properties are cheaper and reserved for newer players — make use of them while you can.
