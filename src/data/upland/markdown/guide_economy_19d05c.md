🏪 Where Assets Are Traded

Upland Marketplace

Upland gives players two distinct ways to buy and sell assets — the Open Marketplace and player-owned Metaventure stores.

🔓 Open Marketplace

List & Buy Directly

Upland properties — along with any 3D assets placed on them — can be listed for sale by any player. Set your price in UPX or USD, and any player in the world can purchase it directly. It's the most accessible way to trade your portfolio.

**⚠️ Important Restrictions:** Depending on your in game status, and whether the propery is FSA status, you may encounter USD sale restrictions

[FSA Sale Restrictions](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/fsa-properties) [USD Sale Cooldown](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status)

🏗️ Metaventures

Player-Owned Storefronts

Metaventures are player-owned stores that specialize in specific types of assets — decor, cars, collectibles, and more. They offer a curated shopping experience and a powerful business opportunity for entrepreneurial Uplanders.

Players can list their items at any Metaventure for a commission

- Build your own Metaventure to sell your own products and earn on others'

- To buy: travel directly to the store, or own a property in the same city — then shop from anywhere

- Metaventures can develop their own brand reputation and loyal customers

[🏗️ Learn More About Metaventures →](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures)

💱 How Value Flows

The Upland Economy

Upland runs on four distinct currencies, each serving a different purpose. Understanding how they interact is the key to building a successful earning strategy.

[📖 How to Earn](https://upland-guide.com/earning-in-upland)

UPX

In-Game Currency

UPX is Upland's core in-game currency and the foundation of the entire economy. It can be purchased directly from the Upland store using real USD. UPX is used to travel to new cities and mint (purchase) unminted properties on the map. Items — including properties and 3D NFT assets — can be priced and sold for UPX on the open marketplace and in Metaventures.

[📖 How the UPX Economy Works →](https://upland-guide.com/economy/financial-pools)

USD

Real Money Earnings

Players can earn real US dollars by selling their Upland assets — either properties listed on the open marketplace or items sold through Metaventures. USD earnings represent the most direct path to real-world value from your Upland portfolio. To sell for USD, you must complete KYC (Know Your Customer) identity verification and meet eligibility requirements.

[✅ Complete KYC to Sell for USD →](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/earning-in-upland)

$SPARKLET

ERC-20 Token · World-Building Fuel

$SPARKLET is an ERC-20 fungible token that fuels all value creation in the Upland metaverse. It is the resource required for world-building, joint community projects, no-code NFT minting, and manufacturing 3D map NFTs such as cars, decor items, and more. Unlike UPX, $SPARKLET has broader market utility — it can be traded externally and is listed on exchanges, making it a key token for players interested in the crypto side of Upland. For players not crypto-native, Sparklet can be purchsaed in game.

[🔥 Required for manufacturing](https://upland-guide.com/metaventures/manufacturing)
[📈 Where to buy Sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/where-to-buy-sparklet)

STEM

Life Token · Sustaining the Living Metaverse

STEM is the token responsible for sustaining all living things in the Upland metaverse. It powers the Life system — including plant growth, Totems, and Protem mechanics — keeping the living elements of your properties thriving. STEM can currently be purchased directly in the Upland store, and in the future will also be earnable through in-game activities. Think of it as the water and sunlight your metaverse ecosystem needs to survive and grow.
