# Vancouver Evolution Gears Up with Uppie Outfitters

October 02, 2026

City Evolution heads to **Vancouver** with a new storefront inspired by the great outdoors. This week’s signature structure, **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Uppie Outfitters Edition**, brings a camping and outdoor-gear store theme to your next city build, pairing Vancouver’s outdoor spirit with a fresh opportunity to grow your property footprint.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release keeps the focus on property access and neighborhood character. Standard Packs offer a **guaranteed minimum property value of 23,000 UPX**, while Deluxe Packs pair a **guaranteed combined minimum property value of 55,000 UPX** with the new signature structure.

### Neighborhoods

**No new neighborhoods** are opening in this release. Pack properties come from **Vancouver**’s existing footprint.

### Collections

**No new Collections** are being introduced with this Evolution.

### Details

Both **Vancouver Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, October 2 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, October 3 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, October 9 at 9 AM PT

### Vancouver Standard Pack

Includes one random property in **Vancouver** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 23,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 20,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 23,000 UPX

### Vancouver Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in **Vancouver** with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 55,000 UPX**, plus **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Uppie Outfitters Edition**.

For players looking to grow their property portfolio and give their next build an outdoors-inspired storefront, the Deluxe Pack brings both together in one release.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 55,000 UPX combined

Bring a little trailhead spirit to the city block. **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Uppie Outfitters Edition** takes its inspiration from camping and outdoor-gear stores, adding an adventure-ready theme to the Mainstreet modular lineup. It’s a fitting addition for players bringing **Vancouver**’s love of the outdoors into their neighborhood designs.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Vancouver Deluxe Pack in this release**.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about **building cities with depth**, not just opening new territory. This **Vancouver** release gives players another opportunity to establish a foothold, grow an existing portfolio, or bring a new storefront theme to a modular build.

Choose the pack that fits your next move and bring a little outdoor spirit to **Vancouver**.
