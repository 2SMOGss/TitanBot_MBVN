## How NFLPA Legits Collections Work

The taste of sweet victory for completing a rare Collection is second to none. Here is what you need to know to get started with NFLPA Legits Collections. 

‍

### Types of NFLPA Legits

These Collections are made from NFLPA Legits. There are currently two kinds of Legits: Mementos and Essentials. 

- Mementos are rare, “1 of 1” Legits that capture real-world data such as player performances for each game. 
- Essentials are commonly available Legits with unlimited minting supply, but are limited by time and/or football season. 

**Tip: ***You can read all about Legits and Essentials in the *[NFLPA Legits Guidebook](https://community.upland.me/nflpa-legits-guidebook)*. *

‍

### Completing NFLPA Collections

There are 14 NFLPA Legits Collections, 12 of which are available today for *each of the* *32 football teams. *To complete a Collection, players simply need to move Legits into a team’s corresponding Collection. Please note that UPX earnings for Collections are underway and will be released later this season. 

‍
