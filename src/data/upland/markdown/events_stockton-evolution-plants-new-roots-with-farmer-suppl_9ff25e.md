# Stockton Evolution Plants New Roots with Farmer Supply

July 31, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Stockton** this week with a fresh reason to build in the heart of California’s Central Valley. **Stockton is already open**, so this release focuses on adding new property opportunities, another shot at Collection properties, and the all-new **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Farmer Supply Editions**.

This week’s **Stockton Evolution Pack** gives players another way to enter or expand in the city without opening any new neighborhoods. With **250 Standard Packs** and **350 Deluxe Bundles** available, players can grow their Stockton footprint while the Deluxe Bundle brings a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property** and an exclusive farm supply storefront structure.

### Neighborhoods

No new neighborhoods are opening with this release. **Stockton is already open**, and all properties included in these packs come from the city’s existing footprint.

### Collections

The **Stockton Deluxe Bundle** has a **10% chance** of containing a Collection property.

Collection properties are not guaranteed in every Deluxe Bundle. The **Stockton** Standard Pack does not include a chance to receive a Collection property.

### Details

Both **Stockton** offerings will be available in **limited quantities** in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, July 31 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, August 1 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, August 7 at 9 AM PT

### Stockton Standard Pack

Includes one random property from **Stockton** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 10,000 UPX**. This pack does not include a chance to receive a Collection property.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 250
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** None

### Stockton Deluxe Bundle

Includes one random property from **Stockton** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 40,000 UPX**, a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property**, and the all-new **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Farmer Supply Editions** structure.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the **Stockton Deluxe Bundle**, making it a standout addition for players looking to bring more Central Valley character to their builds.

**Price:** $20
**Quantity:** 350
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 40,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10%

The **Mainstreet Modular Apartments: Farmer Supply Editions** brings a farm supply storefront to **Stockton**’s streetscape. With a practical neighborhood shop on the ground floor and residential space above, it adds a playful nod to the city’s Central Valley setting while giving builders a distinctive new Mainstreet destination.

Like all Modular structures, this build is **fully modular**, making it ideal for stacking and combining into larger, customized builds.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about more than opening new neighborhoods. It is also about **building more depth into cities that are already open**. With more **Stockton** properties entering circulation, a Collection chance in the Deluxe Bundle, and Farmer Supply arriving as this week’s signature structure, Stockton is getting a fresh crop of building possibilities.

For players ready to grow their **Stockton** footprint, this is the week to plant something new.
