# Treasure Hunting

## Treasure Hunting

Treasure hunting is the adventure mechanic. Send your Block Explorer across a city to find hidden treasures within a time limit. Proximity hints guide you. Speed matters.

#### Three tiers

- **Standard.** Available to all players.
- **Limited.** For Uplander status and above.
- **Exclusive.** Higher ranks. Includes pinata mini-games and bigger rewards.

#### Treasure Rush

Treasure Rush is a timed event in which players spawn and collect as many treasures as possible within a 30-minute window. No sends are consumed during a Rush. Leaderboards rank the top hunters per city.

#### Strategy

- Own property across the city you're hunting in — free traversal to your own properties is a real advantage.
- Higher-tier cities offer more substantial rewards.
- Watch for "Sparked Up" Treasure Tiers during SPARKLET Weeks — treasure chests can drop SPARKLET.
