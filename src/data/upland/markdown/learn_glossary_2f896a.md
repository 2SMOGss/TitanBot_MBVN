# Glossary

#### Some essential terms

**Architect.** A human player. You are an Architect.

**Block Explorer.** Your character on the map. Wanders autonomously. Direct it with a Send.

**Collection.** A set of related properties that, when completed, grants a UPX bonus and a permanent earnings multiplier.

**Commerce Score.** Measures a neighborhood's economic strength through Office Structures and reciprocal bonds between neighborhoods.

**FSA (Fair Start Act).** Properties reserved for newer players (net worth below 100,000 UPX). Cheaper, ideal first buys.

**Influence Score.** Measures how attractive your neighborhoods are to potential residents.

**IPU.** Iconic Parody Uppie. The rarest tier — created by merging two Level 10 Uppies.

**KYC.** Identity verification. Required for USD withdrawals and voting.

**Legit.** A collectible NFT with permanent ownership. Includes sports licenses (FIFA, NFLPA, F.C. Porto, AFA), seeds, and themed items.

**Metaventure.** A player-run shop. Sell items, earn revenue, build a brand inside Upland.

**Mint.** To become the first owner of an unminted property.

**Recipe.** A combination of Troves that, processed at a service structure, produces a higher-order good Uppies request.

**Resident Score.** Tracks how many Uppies live on your properties. Updates every 24 hours.

**Send.** A directed move that sends your Block Explorer to a specific property.

**SPARKLET.** Upland's resource/utility token. Powers building, manufacturing, fuelling vehicles, seed generation, and Metaventures. Tradable outside Upland as an ERC-20 on Ethereum and Base.

**STEM.** The dedicated Life currency. Account-bound, non-tradable. Keeps plants, farming, and animals accessible.

**Trove.** A fungible in-game item produced by structures, plants, fishing, animals, and trade routes. Used in Recipes.

**Uplander.** The status tier at which you gain permanent ownership of your assets.

**UPX.** Upland's in-game transactional currency. Fixed rate: 1,000 UPX = $1 USD. Not tradable outside Upland.

**Upland Appchain.** Upland's dedicated, transparent blockchain. Records property, structure, Uppie, and transaction data. Public block explorer at [ledger.upland.me](https://ledger.upland.me).

**Uppie.** A digital resident. Lives on properties, has personality, requests goods, levels up through merging.
