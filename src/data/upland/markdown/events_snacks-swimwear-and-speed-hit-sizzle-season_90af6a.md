# Snacks, Swimwear, and Speed Hit Sizzle Season

August 19, 2026

**Sizzle Season** is shifting into high gear with four new **Service Structures** built for sweet stops, classic snacks, beach-ready style, and full-throttle fun. From cinnamon treats and fresh popcorn to swimwear shopping and a neighborhood kart track, this week’s lineup gives players four new ways to make their neighborhoods feel lively, useful, and ready for one more summer adventure.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, August 19 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required.
