# Seattle Evolution Makes Waves With The Salty Anchor

October 16, 2026

City Evolution returns to Seattle with three new neighborhoods, a new Limited Collection, and a signature storefront built for the waterfront spirit of the Emerald City. **Mainstreet Modular: The Salty Anchor Marine Supply** brings a marine-supply theme to the modular lineup, giving Seattle builders a fresh way to add local character to their city blocks.

With **100 Standard Packs** and **300 Deluxe Packs** available, this release opens new property access across Portage Bay, Mann, and Broadway. Both pack types also include a chance to receive a Collection property, while Deluxe Packs bring the new signature structure into the mix.

### Neighborhoods

**Three new Seattle neighborhoods** are opening in this release:

- Portage Bay
- Mann
- Broadway Together, these neighborhoods expand Seattle’s playable footprint and give players more places to claim property, grow their portfolios, and shape the city’s next chapter.

### Collections

This release introduces the new **Broadway Limited Collection**, giving Seattle collectors a fresh target in one of the newly opened neighborhoods.

The **Seattle Standard Pack** has a **10% pack-level chance** of containing a Collection property, while the **Seattle Deluxe Pack** has a **30% pack-level chance**. A Collection property is not guaranteed, and these odds do not guarantee a Broadway Collection property specifically.

### Details

Both **Seattle Evolution Packs** will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, October 16 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, October 17 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, October 23 at 9 AM PT

### Seattle Standard Pack

Includes one random property in Seattle with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 15,000 UPX** and a **10% pack-level chance** of receiving a Collection property.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 15,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10% per pack

### Seattle Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in Seattle with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 46,000 UPX**, a **30% pack-level chance** of receiving a Collection property, and the new **Mainstreet Modular: The Salty Anchor Marine Supply** structure.

For players expanding their Seattle footprint, the Deluxe Pack brings together two properties, the strongest Collection-property odds in this release, and a signature structure made to give a modular streetscape unmistakable Seattle flavor.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 46,000 UPX combined
**Collection Property Chance:** 30% per pack

**Mainstreet Modular: The Salty Anchor Marine Supply** brings a little saltwater soul to your block. Think of the neighborhood shop where **Uppies swap fishing stories**, dream up their next boating adventure, and insist the catch was bigger than anyone remembers. With its marine-supply theme and Seattle spirit, The Salty Anchor **adds character to your Mainstreet Modular lineup** and gives your neighborhood a story beyond the buildings.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Seattle Deluxe Pack in this release**. Like other modular structures, it supports flexible building and long-term customization, giving players more ways to develop city blocks while preserving a strong sense of place and regional identity.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about **building cities with depth**, one neighborhood at a time. With three new neighborhoods, a new Limited Collection target, stronger property access, and The Salty Anchor arriving as Seattle’s signature structure, this release gives players several new ways to build their place in the city.
