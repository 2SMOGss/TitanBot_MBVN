# Troves 1.0 Is Fully Launched: Level 100, Rare Genomes, and More to Discover

November 03, 2026

The initial full launch of **Troves** is here. With **over 500 Troves**, **over 700 recipes**, new **Platinum and Diamond banking tiers**, and **Upland progression now open to Level 100**, the complete first chapter of Troves has arrived. Explore new finds across the map, turn them into ingredients, craft higher-level Troves, and **build toward the biggest progression goals** yet.

This is the **final phase of the initial Troves launch**, but it is only the beginning of what comes next. **Uppie Quests** are up next, and rare **Genome rewards** will begin dropping from certain crafting recipes in the **coming days**. Beyond that, **automation, farms, trade routes, fishing, and more** are on the horizon, bringing new ways to collect, craft, and explore Upland.

### More Room to Progress, with Automation Ahead

**All remaining Troves and recipes** in the launch are **now live**, along with **Platinum and Diamond banking tiers** and a progression cap of **Level 100**. There is plenty to explore, craft, and work toward, with progression through **Level 75** achievable through manual collecting and crafting.

**Progression beyond Level 75**, along with Platinum and Diamond banking, is **designed specifically for automation**. These goals require resource quantities that will be extremely difficult to gather and craft by hand. Although they are unlocked now, they are not intended to be practical targets through manual play alone. **Upcoming automation** will provide the **large-scale collection and crafting** needed to pursue them.

For now, keep **discovering Troves**, **fulfilling recipes**, and **banking** toward your next milestone. Banking remains the primary way to increase your Upland progression level, and deposits into the Trove Vault are permanent, so keep the ingredients you want to use in recipes before banking them.

**Automation is coming soon**, and we’ll share more about how it will support these higher-tier goals. Level 100 is the current progression cap, with **further increases** planned for the future.

### Rare Genomes

In the coming days, certain crafting recipes will offer **a rare chance to receive a Genome Trove** as an additional reward. Genomes represent animal life, including chickens and cows, giving players something new to discover as they craft.

These Genomes will be **the foundation for living animals** on Upland’s map. In a future update, players will be able to use them as recipe ingredients to bring those animals to life. **Genome rewards will arrive first**, with animal creation coming later. We will announce when these go live.

### Automation Will Power the Next Era of Troves

**Automation** will be a major part of progressing through **Platinum and Diamond**. These banking tiers are available now, but their large requirements are **designed with future automated collection and crafting in mind**.

**Uppies will be able to work at Service Structures**, automatically collecting and crafting specific types of Troves for players. **Farms will produce resource Troves at industrial scale**, helping supply the quantities needed for Platinum and Diamond. These systems will expand what players can collect and create beyond what they gather and craft by hand.

**Automation, Uppie employment, and farming are coming in future updates** and are not active today. Alongside **trade routes, fishing, and other planned features**, they will open up new ways to put your assets to work and pursue Upland’s biggest progression goals. We’ll share more about how these systems work in **upcoming announcements**.

### The Future Is Just Beginning.

With over **500 Troves**, over **700 recipes**, and **progression up to Level 100**, there is more than ever to discover, craft, and work toward in Upland. Explore the newly available Troves, try new recipes, and set your sights on your next banking milestone. With **rare Genomes, automation, farming, and more still ahead**, today’s full launch is the foundation for much more to come. **Bring back a friend** or **introduce someone new**, and start discovering what you can build together.
