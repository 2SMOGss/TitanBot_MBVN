# Uppies Levels : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels
In Upland there are both common and rare Uppies from levels 1-10. Some are everyday citizens, families searching for good schools and parks, professionals focused on their careers, business owners opening shops, or artists adding color and character to their neighborhoods. Upland also features iconic Uppies (IPUs) (level 11) who are humorous and fictional characters created as parodies, each with unique designs and personalities. These Uppies will stand out for their distinct identities and stories, adding a new layer of fun and cultural flavor to the game. [Learn more about Uppies, how to discover them in the metaverse.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies)

Here’s the expected full progression path of an Uppie. Learn about the [different utilities](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility) that each level holds.

- Level 1* **— Newborn**

- Level 2* — **Toddler**

- Level 3* — **Child**

- Level 4* — **Teen**

- Level 5+ — **Adult**

- **IPU (Iconic Parody Uppie)** — Extremely rare, obtainable by merging two Level 10 Uppies

* Levels 1–4 will have multiple mints of the same Uppie, while in levels 5+ each Uppie will be one of a kind.
