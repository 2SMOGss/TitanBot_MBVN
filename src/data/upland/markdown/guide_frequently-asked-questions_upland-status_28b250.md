# Upland Status : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-status
As you begin to level up, the game begins to change, and so will your strategy. There is no right or wrong strategy and there is no way to "win" at Upland. Don't be discouraged when you see players with a net worth of millions of UPX. You don't know how much money they have invested, or how long they have been playing for. There is a place for everyone in Upland. This page will detail what you are able to do at each status, as well as some common strategies to help you get to the next level

There are 8 different statuses in Upland: Note that the in game currency UPX is valued at $1 USD = 1000 UPX. (the cryptocurrency UPX is not related and a mere coincidence)

Where net worth is the sum of the following:

- Your total UPX value (the amount of liquid UPX you have).

- Property value. This is the total amount of UPX spent on properties (minting + secondary market values)

In addition to your status, a second metric that helps to measure your journey is the progression score. [Learn more about that here.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/progressions-score)










** 
** 

Visitor: < 10,000 UPX net worth 




- You do not yet own your digital assets. This only happens once you reach uplander status. This is the reason you must renew your visa at least once every 7 days. You can do this by logging in to Upland and within your profile click renew visa. There is no limit to the amount of times you can do this.

- You may mint properties. [FSA properties](https://upland-guide.com/fsa-properties) are reserved for new players and are typically the cheapest ones available. Watch this video to learn [how to mint cheap FSA properties.](https://youtu.be/bZizjkz8nEg)

- You are not able to sell or trade properties (this is because you do not yet own your digital assets)

- You are not able to own [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet), or other assets aside from properties. If you have won spark either through treasure hunting or a contest, you must level up before the item is delivered to you otherwise you forfeit it.





** 
** 

Uplander: 10,001 – 100,000 UPX net worth 




- You own your digital assets. A digital wallet is created for you on the EOS blockchain. Here all your in game assets are recorded and providing you proof of ownership

- You are able to sell and trade your properties on the marketplace

- You are able to purchase [FSA properties](https://upland-guide.com/fsa-properties)

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 30 days.

- You are able to list 20 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

- You are able to own [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet), but you must verify your identity through the [KYC Process](https://upland-guide.com/fundamentals/tilia-kyc).

- If you wait until [sparklet weekend](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend) to level up you will get an 30 sparklet.





** 
** 

Pro: 100,001 – 1,000,000 UPX net worth 




- You are unable to purchase FSA properties

- If you wait until [sparklet weekend](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend) to level up to pro you will get an 120 [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet).

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 14 days.

- You are able to list 35 properties for sale in USD at any given time.





** 
** 

Director: 1,000,001 – 10,000,000 UPX net worth 




- You will be eligible to receive a custom made Block Explorer made to your specifications by the Upland Team. [Apply in city hall.](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-city-hall)

- You are guaranteed a 500* [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet) reward when you level up. If you wait until [sparklet weekend](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend) to level up you will get an additional 20% spark (100) for a total of 600* sparklet.

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 7 days.

- You are able to list 50 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

* The Sparklet amount is multiplied by the [Sparklet adjustment factor](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/history/adjustment-history)





** 
** 

Executive: 10,000,001 – 100,000,000 UPX net worth 




- You will be eligible to receive a custom made 3D object to place on one of your properties made to your specifications by the Upland Team. To register you must travel to [City Hall](https://upland-guide.com/frequently-asked-questions/upland-city-hall)

- You are guaranteed a 1000* [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet) reward when you level up. If you wait until [sparklet weekend](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend)  to level up you will get an additional 20% spark (200) for a total of 1200* sparklet.

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 3 days.

- You are able to list 75 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

* The Sparklet amount is multiplied by the [Sparklet adjustment factor](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/history/adjustment-history)





** 
** 

Chief Executive: 100,000,001 - 500,000,000 UPX net worth 




- You are guaranteed a 2000* [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet) reward when you level up. If you wait until [sparklet weekend](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/sparklet-weekend) to level up you will get an additional 20% sparklet (400*) for a total of 2400* sparklet.

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 3 days.

- You are able to list 100 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

* The Sparklet amount is multiplied by the [Sparklet adjustment factor](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/history/adjustment-history)





** 
** 

Vice Chair: 500,000,001 - 1 Billion UPX net worth 




- [Receive a new badge specifically for Vice Chairs.](https://upland-guide.com/2024/10/07/vice-chair-status)

- Opportunity to obtain unique , high-status model planes

- You will be awarded with 2500* [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet)

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 3 days.

- You are able to list 125 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

* The Sparklet amount is multiplied by the [Sparklet adjustment factor](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/history/adjustment-history)





** 
** 

Chair: Over 1 Billion UPX net worth 




- [Receive a new badge specifically for Chairs.](https://upland-guide.com/2024/10/07/vice-chair-status)

- Opportunity to obtain unique , high-status model planes

- You will be awarded 3000* [sparklet](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet).

- You are able to sell properties for USD after owning it for 3 days.

- You are able to list 150 properties for sale in USD at any given time.

* The Sparklet amount is multiplied by the [Sparklet adjustment factor](https://upland-guide.com/economy/sparklet/history/adjustment-history)
