# Status Levels

Your Upland status is your progression rank. You level up as your net worth (UPX balance + property value) grows.

| Status | Net worth threshold |
| --- | --- |
| **Visitor** | On signup |
| **Uplander** | 10,000 UPX |
| **Pro** | 100,000 UPX |
| **Director** | 1,000,000 UPX |
| **Executive** | 10,000,000 UPX |
| **Chief Executive** | 100,000,000 UPX |
| **Vice Chair** | 500,000,000 UPX |
| **Chair** | 1,000,000,000 UPX |

> **Note:** Becoming an Uplander is the key gate. Until you cross it, your assets aren't permanently yours. When you stay a visitor, you must renew your "visa" once every 7 days. If you miss that, your assets and properties will be recycled back. Most new players target Uplander status in their first week or two.
