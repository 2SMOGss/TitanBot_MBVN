# Upland Strategy : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/upland-metaverse-strategy
There are numerous ways to play the game. There is no right or wrong way. You don't have to invest hundreds or thousands of dollars in order to have fun in the metaverse, and maybe even earn some money. Of course, it can be difficult to determine what your strategy is even before becoming familiar with Upland. In addition to that, your strategy will change and evolve along the way, especially as you begin to level up or as more features are introduced. Dale the Llama has created a checklist strategy to help you determine which common starting strategies are right for you.
