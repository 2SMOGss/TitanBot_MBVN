# Sizzle Summer Starts Early in Upland

July 23, 2026

With **Genesis Season** officially wrapped, it is time to turn up the heat and get ready for **Sizzle Season** and a summer full of fun across Upland. We are **kicking things off early** with four summer-ready **Service Structures** that bring boardwalk bites, tropical nightlife, surf culture, and skate-park energy to the neighborhood. We will share more about this year’s **Sizzle Season** theme next week, but this lineup offers the** first look** at the sizzle summer fun ahead.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, July 22 at 9 AM PT**. As always, all structures are available to players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### Kentucky Fried Kiosk

Crispy chicken, quick service, and the kind of irresistible aroma that can turn any ordinary corner into a must-visit summer stop. **Kentucky Fried Kiosk** is a natural fit for boardwalks, food courts, festival grounds, and lively neighborhood blocks where **Uppies** want something hot, fast, and satisfying. *(Entertainment, 1 Service Unit)*

**Spark Hours:** 3,600
**Price:** $1.99

### The Tipsy Tiki Bar

Tropical drinks, island colors, and a laid-back atmosphere built for long summer evenings. **The Tipsy Tiki Bar** brings instant vacation energy to beachfront districts, nightlife strips, resort blocks, and neighborhood gathering spots where **Uppies** can relax and soak up the **Sizzle Season** spirit. *(Entertainment, 2 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 8,550
**Price:** $4.99

### Quickserver Surf Co

Boards, gear, beach essentials, and everything an **Uppie** needs before chasing the next big wave. **Quickserver Surf Co** is a perfect addition to coastal neighborhoods, shopping streets, boardwalk districts, and summer destinations built around sun, sand, and adventure. *(Essentials, 4 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 13,500
**Price:** $6.99

### Off the Wall Skate Park

Ramps, rails, bowls, and plenty of room for **Uppies** to show off their best tricks. **Off the Wall Skate Park** gives recreation districts, youth-focused neighborhoods, and energetic downtown builds a bold centerpiece where summer action never slows down. *(Entertainment, 24 Service Units)*

**Spark Hours:** 29,700
**Price:** $9.99

Whether players are serving crispy chicken, mixing tropical drinks, gearing up for the surf, or landing tricks at the skate park, this week’s **Service Structures** bring an early burst of summer energy to every kind of city build. Consider this the first taste of what **Sizzle Season** has in store.

Keep shaping the neighborhoods that bring **Uppies** home.
