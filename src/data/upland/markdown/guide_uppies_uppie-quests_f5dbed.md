# Uppie Quests : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/uppie-quests

Uppie Quests send players across the map to gather [Troves](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves), complete [recipes](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/recipes), and earn the special Friend Request Troves needed to befriend new Uppies. When an Uppie Card appears above a property, a quest is waiting there.

To be eligible to spawn an Uppie Quest, a property must have both a resident and an assigned Uppie. Uppies of any level can fulfill the Uppie requirement, from Level 1 all the way up. Quests do not appear at all times on a property, thus these requirements don't guarantee a quest appearing on the property, but they are the requirements for eligibility. Uppies beyond level 2 have [additional utility](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/levels/utility) beyond quest spawning eligibility.

Players will be actively looking for these quests, and the benefits are clear for the player who completes the quest, but what about the property owner? There will also be benefits for property owners when other players select an Uppie Quest from their property. As of September 2026, no specific details have been shared.

An Uppie Quest is a form of [trove recipe](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/recipes). Different from a recipe, an Uppie quest is a recipe requested by a specific Uppie, meaning that the combination of troves required may not exist elsewhere. The below quest shows that the Uppie is requesting a briefcase, seashell and a pearl necklace. Claiming the quest and completing it after obtaining all the required items gives you a chance to receive a friend request trove, but it is not guaranteed. You may receive other items instead, making each completed quest a chance to earn the Friend Request Troves you’ll need to befriend new Uppies.

Players can only hold up to 3 quests at once. Before accepting another quest, you must either complete one or dismiss it. Some quests may also be time-limited, giving you a set window to gather the required Troves and complete the recipe.
