# August Standard Challenges

August 02, 2022

[8.10.2022 UPDATE]

Standard Challenges have been rescheduled and will begin on Thursday, August 11th at 9AM PT. These challenges will run until Sunday, August 14th at 9AM PT.

--

The following Challenges will go live, **Thursday, August 11th at 9AM PT** and run until **S****unday, August 14th at 9AM PT**:

- **Rising Star:** Measures how many Collections were completed during the Challenge* *and held through the end of the Challenge
- **Great Start: **Measures the Visitor who had the most unique sends to their properties
- **People’s Champ:** Measures the number of unique sends by a Player to Visitor properties ‍

## Changes to the Rising Star Challenge

In an attempt to increase competition for the top spots, we have introduced a tweak to the scoring. For this month’s Rising Star Challenge, only Collections that are completed during the Challenge period and held through the end of the Challenge will be counted towards the leaderboard. We will monitor the effect of this change and make adjustments as necessary.
