# School Buses Return With a Chance to Win the Fire Engine 2025

September 10, 2026

The first bell is about to ring, and a new fleet of yellow buses is rolling into Upland.

For this year’s **Back to School Sale**, three different School Bus models are returning in separate, limited bundles. Whether you want the sleek Series V, the compact Mini, or the classic full-size School Bus, this is your chance to add more character to your streets and put active School Bus utility to work across your city.

There is also a rare prize waiting at the top of the lineup. Players who purchase the **full-size School Bus bundle** will have a chance to instead receive one of only **two Fire Engine 2025 vehicles **available in this sale.

### Why School Buses Matter

School Buses are more than vehicles for your collection. Their Service Score multiplier is active now, giving them an immediate role in the neighborhoods and educational services that help a city feel alive.

Place a School Bus on a property with an **Elementary School or High School Service Structure** to apply a multiplier boost to that structure’s Service Score. School Buses do not provide a boost to University Service Structures.

That makes this sale an opportunity to strengthen your city right away. The vehicles you own, the structures you build, and the way you plan your properties already work together. Add the right School Bus to the right school property and give your educational network an active boost.

### Sale Details

Quantities are limited across all three bundles, with only **110 School Buses available in total**. The Fire Engine 2025 opportunity is exclusive to the **$150 full-size School Bus bundle**.

**Registration Opens:** Thursday, September 10 at 9:00 AM PT

**Sale Begins:** Friday, September 11 at 9:00 AM PT

**Sale Ends:** Monday, September 14 at 9:00 AM PT

Three separate limited bundles will be available:

### School Bus Series V

A sleek, modern addition to any fleet, with the lowest mint count of the three School Bus models in this sale.

**Price:** $115

**Quantity:** 25

### School Bus Mini

**Price:** $125

**Quantity:** 35

A compact take on the classic School Bus, built for players who want something distinctive on their streets while putting active school-based utility to work.

### School Bus

The classic full-size School Bus features a fully rendered 3D interior and is the **only bundle that includes a chance to get the Fire Engine 2025**. If you receive the Fire Engine, **you will receive it instead of a School Bus.**

**Price:** $150

**Quantity:** 52 (50 School Buses plus 2 Fire Trucks)

### Two Fire Engine 2025 Vehicles Are Up for Grabs

A total of **two Fire Engine 2025 vehicles** will be available in the School Bus bundle. These rare and exclusive service vehicles are built to stand out in any collection, but their long-term appeal goes beyond looks.

Expanded utility is in development for service vehicles with complementary Service Structures, including fire trucks, ambulances, taxis, and police vehicles. The planned system will allow a matching vehicle parked at its related Service Structure to further enhance that structure and increase its Trove output potential, including improved chances at rarer Troves.

Owning a Fire Engine 2025 means holding one of Upland’s rarest service vehicles while preparing for a future where matching vehicles, structures, and city planning become even more connected.

### Put Your Bus to Work

Before registration opens, decide where your new School Bus can make the biggest immediate impact:

- Add a limited School Bus model to your vehicle collection.
- Place it on an eligible property with an **Elementary School or High School Service Structure** to activate its Service Score multiplier.
- Choose the **full-size School Bus bundle** if you want a chance at one of the **two Fire Engine 2025 vehicles**.
- Think ahead about how service vehicles and complementary structures could fit into your long-term city plans. The buses are fueled, the routes are planned, and Uppies are ready for class. Choose your School Bus, boost your educational network today, and you could send a rare Fire Engine 2025 racing into your collection.
