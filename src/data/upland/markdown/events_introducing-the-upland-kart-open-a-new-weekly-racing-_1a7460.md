A new way to **get started with racing** is coming to Upland!

The **Upland Kart Open** is a **free weekly contest** designed for new and developing racers who want to try competitive racing in a fun, accessible format. Each Kart Open will run alongside the [Pro Racing Championship](https://www.upland.me/events/sizzle-2026-a-new-championship-racing-year-begins), using the same track and the same weekly competition window.

There is **no season-long leaderboard**, championship standing, or long-term commitment. **Every week is a fresh contest** and a new opportunity to race.

**New to racing** or looking for tips? Head to the **Race Chat** channel in the Upland app or on Discord to meet other racers, ask questions, and **learn how to get started**.

### Free to Enter

The Kart Open has **no registration fee**.

Each weekly contest will run from **Thursday at 9:00 AM PT through Monday at 9:00 AM PT**, matching the Pro Racing Championship qualifying window.

Racers will compete on **the same community-created track featured in that week’s championship event**, bringing the whole racing community together around one schedule and one course.

### How It Works

The weekly Kart Open leaderboard will be ranked by each racer’s **fastest single completed run**.

Unless otherwise announced for a specific race, all Kart Open events will follow these standard requirements:

- **MPS:** 80
- **Race Type:** Both private and public races count
- **Power-Ups:** Not permitted These requirements may change from race to race to mix things up. Any changes will be announced before that week’s contest begins; otherwise, the standard requirements above will apply.

Racers may make **unlimited attempts** during the contest window, giving everyone the chance to learn the track, practice their lines, and improve their best time.

Results will be **announced each Monday** and prizes will be awarded.

### Weekly Prize Pool

The top three Kart Open racers each week will receive:

- **1st Place:** 25,000 UPX
- **2nd Place:** 15,000 UPX
- **3rd Place:** 10,000 UPX The **top three times** will also be featured on the [Commissioner Stream](https://www.youtube.com/@MetaverseRacingNewsNetwork/streams) alongside that week’s championship results.

**Each week stands on its own**. Once one contest ends, the **leaderboard resets** and the next Kart Open begins on a new track.

### Built for New Racers

The Kart Open is intended to provide a **welcoming, low-stakes introduction** to Upland racing.

It is a place to **learn how racing events work**, gain experience on community-created tracks, improve through repeated attempts, and **compete for weekly prizes **without paying a registration fee.

Racers who have posted a time in the **Sizzle Season Pro Racing Championship** may still participate in the Kart Open for fun and post times to the leaderboard. However, their times will not count toward the official Kart Open standings, and they will **not be eligible** for weekly prizes.
