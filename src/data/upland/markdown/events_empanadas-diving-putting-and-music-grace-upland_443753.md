# Empanadas, Diving, Putting And Music Grace Upland

September 16, 2026

**Sizzle Season** may be over, but that doesn’t mean the Service Structures are cooling down any time soon! With this array of new structures, there’s plenty for your Uppies to be getting involved in as temperatures cool.

These **Service Structures** will be available starting **Wednesday, September 16 at 9 AM PT** for players with **Uplander status** and above. No registration is required. Each structure is expected to remain in the store for roughly four weeks. Once a player purchases one, it will disappear from that player’s store.

### One-Day Service Structure Value Pack

Collectors who want the complete lineup can grab the **Service Structure Value Pack**, which includes all four of this week’s new structures for **$17.99**. That is **25% off regular pricing**, making it the best way to add the entire release to one collection.

The **Service Structure Value Pack** will be available for 24 hours only, from **Wednesday, September 16 at 9 AM PT through Thursday, September 17 at 9 AM PT**. The individual structures will remain available separately during their store rotation.

**Spark Hours:** 25,650

**Price:** $9.99

Empanadas, diving, golfing and live music are all covered this week for your **Uppies**, giving them a much wider range of ways to stay entertained and excited to live within your neighborhoods.
