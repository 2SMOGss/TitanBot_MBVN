# Nashville Evolution Rides In with Yeehaw Outfitters

July 24, 2026

City Evolution returns to **Nashville** this week with a new reason to build in Music City. **Nashville is already open**, so this release is focused on adding fresh property opportunities, a **Priest Lake Standard Collection** target, and the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Yeehaw Outfitters Edition**.

This week’s **Nashville Evolution Pack** gives players another way to enter or expand in the city without opening any new neighborhoods. With **250 Standard Packs** and **350 Deluxe Packs** available, players can grow their Nashville footprint while the Deluxe Pack brings a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property** and an exclusive cowboy apparel store structure.

### Neighborhoods

No new neighborhoods are opening with this release. **Nashville is already open**, and all properties included in these packs come from the city’s existing footprint.

### Collections

The **Nashville Deluxe Pack** has a **10% chance** of containing a Collection property.

**Priest Lake:** Standard Collection

Collection properties are not guaranteed in every Deluxe Pack, but Priest Lake gives **Nashville** collectors a new target to chase during this release.

### Details

Both **Nashville** packs will be available in limited quantities in the Upland Store. Unsold properties will be removed at the end of the sale and may be reintroduced in a future drop.

**Registration Opens:** Friday, July 24 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, July 25 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, July 31 at 9 AM PT

### Nashville Standard Pack

Includes one random property from **Nashville** with a **guaranteed minimum UPX value of 15,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 15,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 250
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 15,000 UPX

### Nashville Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties from **Nashville** with a **guaranteed combined minimum UPX value of 41,000 UPX**, a **10% chance of receiving a Collection property**, and the all-new **East Coast Modular Apartments: Yeehaw Outfitters Edition** structure.

The only way to get this structure in the current release is through the **Nashville Deluxe Pack**, making it a standout addition for players looking to bring some Music City style to their builds.

**Price:** $20
**Quantity:** 350
**Guaranteed Minimum UPX Value:** 41,000 UPX
**Collection Property Chance:** 10%

The **East Coast Modular Apartments: Yeehaw Outfitters Edition** brings a cowboy apparel store to **Nashville**’s streetscape. With western wear on the ground floor and residential space above, it gives players a playful new destination built for hats, boots, and plenty of Music City character

Like all Modular structures, this build is **fully modular**, making it ideal for stacking and combining into larger, customized builds.

### Looking Ahead

City Evolution is about more than opening new neighborhoods. It is also about **building more depth into cities that are already open**. With Priest Lake entering the Collection chase and Yeehaw Outfitters arriving as this week’s signature structure, **Nashville** is getting a fresh dose of property opportunity and western flair.

For players ready to expand their Music City footprint, this is the week to saddle up.
