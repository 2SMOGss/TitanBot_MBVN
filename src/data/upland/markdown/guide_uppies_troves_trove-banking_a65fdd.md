# Trove Banking : Upland Guide

**Source:** https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/trove-banking
Crafting trove recipies is not the only thing you can do with troves. Through recipes, a number of troves can be exchanged for a rare trove. Trove banking provides a different use case for the troves. Banking permanently deposits troves into your collection, or vault. Once banked, they cannot be withdrawn or used elsewhere.

This permanent commitment is what gives Trove Banking its prestige. Your bank represents what you have collected, completed, and chosen to preserve as part of your Upland history. When you have collected enough copies of a Trove to reach its next banking level, the banking button will become active. Selecting the bank icon will permanently add those Troves to your bank and help advance that Trove’s level.

Every Trove will have its own banking progression where the initial banking levels will include:

- Bronze

- Silver

- Gold

- Platinum

- Diamond

- And more….

The number of Troves required to reach each level will depend on the Trove itself. More common Troves will generally require a larger quantity to bank, while Troves found deeper within the system may require fewer to be banked.

For example, reaching Bronze for a Heart Trove might require banking 20 Heart Troves, while reaching Silver might require a higher total. However, players do not need to unlock each banking level in order. A player who has enough Heart Troves to meet the Diamond requirement can bank them all at once and advance directly to Diamond, bypassing Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.

This creates meaningful choices. You may decide to pursue higher levels in a few specialized Troves, build a broad collection across many categories, or find your own balance between depth and coverage.

Banking troves are an integral part of progressing in your Upland journey and advancing your [progression score.](https://upland-guide.com/uppies/troves/progressions-score) This score enables Upland to further gamify other aspects of the platform beyond your [player status](https://upland-guide.com/upland-status). In order to advance your progression score, players may need to satisfy a combination of requirements. This example shows that beyond banking troves you will need to complete other milestones as well.
