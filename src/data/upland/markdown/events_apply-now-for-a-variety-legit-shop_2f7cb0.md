# Apply Now for a Variety Legit Shop

August 23, 2023

Attention Uplanders! We are officially opening up applications for players to apply to run their very own Variety Legit Shops. This brand-new shop type will open up an opportunity for players to sell a wide range of Legits that were previously not yet offered in Metaventure Shops. This includes Legits such as Spirit Halloween, Jacky Tsai, Mangueira, Luiza Possi, and upcoming Legits that may not have their own dedicated shops, as NFLPA does. 

‍

We’re thrilled to introduce this fresh addition to the Metaventure Shop lineup. It's a chance for players to showcase and sell their entire collection of Legits like never before. Keep reading to find out more about the application process for a Variety Legit Shop.

‍

## Variety Legit Shop Application Details 

Players must meet all the requirements below at the time of submitting their application to qualify for a Variety Legit Shop. The Upland Team will review all applications and email applicants about their approval status within 3-4 weeks of submitting their applications. 

‍

If one or more requirements are not met, the applicant will be notified via email as to the reason. Players may reapply after being notified if they correct the necessary details and as long as they meet all requirements. Please note that submitting multiple applications will not mean your applications will all be reviewed or shops launched together. 

‍

Before proceeding with your application, please take the time to review all the requirements listed below thoroughly. 

‍

## Variety Legit Shop Application Requirements

- The applicant must complete KYC identity verification before submitting their application.
- The applicant status must be an Uplander or above. 
- The applicant cannot be in Alcatraz. 
- The Variety Legit Shop name submitted in the application must not contain any hate speech, violence, aggression, profanity, or derogatory/inappropriate text. The Variety Legit Shop name does feature the word “Upland” or any other IP. Additionally, the name must be unique from all other Metaventures of the same type (i.e. I can have a Block Explorer Shop named ‘123 Shop’ and a Variety Legit Shop named ‘123 Shop’, but not two Block Explorer Shops named ‘123 Shop’)
- The applicant must be willing to begin paying monthly Venture Dues (Upland’s dynamic model of operating fees) beginning the first week of the month following being launched.
- The applicant must have a fully constructed building on the desired property for the location of the Variety Legit Shop at the time of submitting the application. The following types of buildings are ineligible for this shop type: factories, showrooms, landmarks, and micro houses. 
- The property designated for the Variety Legit Shop cannot be listed on any other Metaventure Application and must not be listed for sale or in any offers.
- The applicant must own a minimum of 20 Legits total of the types eligible to be sold (Spirit Halloween, Mangueira, Jacky Tsai, or Luiza Possi). ‍

## How to Apply for a Variety Legit Shop

To apply for a Variety Legit Shop, players will need to send their Block Explorer to the neighborhood City Hall in the same city they wish to have their shop and submit their application with background images. However, it's important to note that players can only apply for a Variety Legit Shop in cities with active City Halls. If a city does not have a City Hall or if the City Hall is under construction, players will have to wait until it is ready and operational before submitting Variety Legit Shop or any other Metaventure applications. 

‍

To locate a nearby City Hall, refer to the list of locations available [here](https://www.upland.me/events/new-improved-city-halls-coming-to-your-city).
