# Miami Is Evolving With Main Street Modular: The Wandering Magic Shop

November 20, 2026

**Miami is evolving, and the celebration keeps growing.** With events bringing the community together across the city, this week’s City Evolution keeps the spotlight right where the energy is. City Evolution 46 gives players new opportunities to own, build, and leave their mark in Miami, with property packs and the new **Main Street Modular: The Wandering Magic Shop**.

This release is not about opening new territory. It is about Miami evolving through the players already showing up, building memorable blocks, and giving the city its character. With **100 Miami Standard Packs** and **300 Miami Deluxe Packs** available, players can add one or two Miami properties to their portfolios. Deluxe Packs also bring **Main Street Modular: The Wandering Magic Shop** to the Main Street Modular lineup.

### Neighborhoods

No new Miami neighborhoods are opening in this release. Pack properties come from Miami’s existing playable footprint, so the city’s evolution continues through what players claim and build, not through new territory.

### Collections

No new Miami **Collections** are being introduced in this release.

### Details

**Registration Opens:** Friday, November 20 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Starts:** Saturday, November 21 at 9 AM PT
**Sale Ends:** Friday, November 27 at 9 AM PT

### Miami Standard Pack

Includes one random property in Miami with a **guaranteed minimum property UPX value of 10,000 UPX**.

**Price:** 10,000 UPX
**Quantity:** 100 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 10,000 UPX

### Miami Deluxe Pack

Includes two random properties in Miami with a **guaranteed combined minimum property UPX value of 48,000 UPX**, plus the **Main Street Modular: The Wandering Magic Shop**, **exclusive to the Miami Deluxe Pack in this release**.

For players ready to build deeper into Miami, the Deluxe Pack pairs two properties with this week’s signature structure, creating a clear path from adding to a portfolio to giving a block more character.

**Price:** $20 on web / $28.49 on mobile
**Quantity:** 300 packs
**Guaranteed Minimum Property UPX Value:** 48,000 UPX combined

**Main Street Modular: The Wandering Magic Shop** brings a playful spark to Miami’s ongoing celebration. Picture **Uppies carrying the day’s energy down the block**, swapping imaginative stories about what might be behind the door, and turning an ordinary stroll into one more memorable Miami moment. The structure gives builders a whimsical focal point for a lively Main Street scene while adding fresh character to the city’s existing neighborhoods.

Like other modular structures, it supports flexible building and long-term customization, giving players more ways to develop city blocks while preserving a strong sense of place and regional identity.

This signature structure is **exclusive to the Miami Deluxe Pack in this release**.

**Miami is evolving through the players who keep showing up, claiming properties, building memorable blocks, and bringing the city to life.** This release adds fresh property opportunities and a signature structure without opening new territory, so the next chapter belongs to the community already making Miami’s moment special.
