# Istanbul Is Coming to Upland

October 26, 2026

The wait is over. **Istanbul, Türkiye** is coming to Upland on **Thursday, October 29, 2026 at 9 AM PT**.

Positioned on the Bosphorus where Europe and Asia meet, Istanbul carries more than **2,000 years of political, religious, and artistic history**. Known across its long history as **Byzantium and Constantinople**, the city served as the capital of the Eastern Roman and Ottoman empires. Now its layered history, waterfront character, and unmistakable energy are ready to open **a new chapter in Upland**.

This will be **Upland’s first city release to open with Troves**, giving players even more to discover as they explore Istanbul. If you know an Uplander who has been waiting for a reason to return, or a friend ready to discover Upland for the first time, **invite them to arrive with you**.

### Hot Air Balloon Celebration

Celebrate Istanbul’s arrival with a **first-of-its-kind Hot Air Balloon Trove celebration**. The celebration takes off one hour after the city opens, on **Thursday, October 29 at 10 AM PT**, when hot air balloons go live in Upland across Istanbul.

The balloons give players a **chance to collect special Troves** that are **normally obtainable only through recipes**. For this celebration, you can **collect those Troves directly**, without fulfilling the recipes normally needed to obtain them. With a chance to even get some troves that haven’t yet been released!

A **25-minute cooldown** applies, and the balloons will **relocate randomly every hour across Istanbul** throughout the celebration. Explore the map and return throughout the weekend for more chances to collect before the celebration ends on **Saturday, October 31st at 9 AM PT**.

We have never done a celebration like this before, and it is just **a small preview of what Troves make possible**. They give us new ways to bring rewards, exploration, and special events together, creating more reasons to jump in and discover what is happening across Upland.

**And for the first time in Upland, we are showing off real-time movement on the map.**

That’s right: the hot air balloons will move around in the air above a property in real time. This is the first time we are bringing real-time animation and movement into the game, and we are excited for you to see it. It is **a preview of what is to come in Upland’s future**.

**Don’t miss this special occasion.** Come to Istanbul, collect special Troves, and see the balloons in motion for yourself!

**Celebration Starts:** Thursday, October 29 at 10 AM PT
**Celebration Ends:** Sat, October 31st at 9 AM PT
**Cooldown:** 25 minutes
**Balloon Movement:** Random relocation every hour across Istanbul

### Istanbul City Pass Sale

The City Pass is back, and this time it celebrates **the arrival of Istanbul**.

Holding an **Istanbul City Pass** at the snapshot will reserve an Istanbul property address for the holder to mint, with a chance for that property to be part of a Collection. The pass also boosts both [All-Time and seasonal Collector Scores](https://www.upland.me/events/collector-score-is-back-for-harvest-season) and unlocks the ability to construct the **Istanbul Townhouse**. Property minting costs are not included in the pass price.

- **Sale Start:** Tuesday, October 27 at 9 AM PT
- **Sale Ends:** Friday, October 30 at 9 AM PT
- **Web Price:** $5

- **Quantity:** 1,000 Passes
- **Snapshot Date:** Friday, October 30 at 9 AM PT
- **Mint Period:** Fri Oct. 30 — Mon Nov. 2nd 9 AM PT **What You Get with an Istanbul City Pass:**

Bring the **charm of an Istanbul** side street to your neighborhood with the **Istanbul Townhouse**, the unique structure unlocked by the City Pass. Its rich red facade, cream-colored trim, and rows of tall, blue-glazed windows make an immediate impression, topped by a warm terracotta-colored roof.

The tall, four-story silhouette and bay-style frontage give it a distinctly townhouse feel, with a dark wooden front door and crisp architectural details from street level to the roofline. Picture a colorful Istanbul streetscape, one striking home at a time. Your City Pass unlocks the ability to construct this standout design and bring that character to your own build.
